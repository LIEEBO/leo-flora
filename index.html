Our Production Process

From raw materials to finished products — every step is quality-controlled in our factory.

Step 1

Design & Molding

Professional designers create petal molds based on real flower species. Precision molding ensures lifelike shape and texture.

Step 2

Material Selection & Dyeing

High-grade silk, Real-Touch PU, velvet, and UV-resistant materials selected. Hand-dyed for natural, fade-resistant color gradients.

Step 3

Hand Assembly

Skilled craftsmen hand-assemble each bloom — layering petals, adding stamens, and shaping every stem for a realistic finish.

Step 4

Quality Check & Packing

Strict quality control on shape, color, and durability. Products are carefully packed for safe international sea or air shipping.
