## Why Choose Faux Floral Arrangements for Your Coastal Wedding?

Coastal weddings come with unique challenges—wind, sun, and humidity can all take a toll on fresh flowers. Faux floral arrangements offer a practical solution without compromising on style. Here are a few reasons why more brides are choosing high-quality artificial flowers:

- **Durability:** They withstand weather conditions, ensuring your bouquets and centerpieces look perfect all day.
- **Allergy-Friendly:** Perfect for guests who suffer from pollen allergies.
- **Cost-Effective:** You can achieve a lush, high-end look for a fraction of the cost of fresh flowers.
- **Eco-Friendly:** Reusable and sustainable, reducing waste.

When selecting faux flowers, look for ones with realistic textures and subtle color variations. Modern realistic artificial flowers are crafted with such detail that they are often mistaken for fresh blooms. This means you can have the garden-party vibe you love without the last-minute scramble to keep flowers hydrated.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

If you're inspired by this Newport wedding and want to bring a similar coastal garden-party aesthetic to your own event, here are some practical tips:

1. **Mix Textures:** Combine different flower types, greenery, and even dried elements to create depth and visual interest.
2. **Use a Focal Color Palette:** Stick to a limited palette like white, ivory, green, and pale blue to keep the look cohesive and elegant.
3. **Invest in Good Greenery:** Faux greenery, like eucalyptus and ferns, adds volume and a natural feel without breaking the bank.
4. **Create Focal Points:** Use a wedding flower wall backdrop behind the head table or as an altar. This becomes a stunning photo backdrop and a conversation piece.
5. **Plan for the Weather:** If you're outdoors, secure all arrangements well. Faux flowers are heavy, so ensure vases and stands are weighted.
6. **Repurpose Your Florals:** After the ceremony, move altar arrangements to the reception, and use bouquets as table centerpieces. This maximizes your investment.
