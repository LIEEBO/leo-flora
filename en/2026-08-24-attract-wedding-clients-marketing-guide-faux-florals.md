## Step 1: Build a Brand That Converts Interest Into Inquiries

### Create a Website That Books Clients

Your website should do three things clearly: show your best work immediately, explain what you offer without confusion, and make it easy to inquire in seconds. Most wedding clients browse on mobile first, so your site must be fast, clean, and easy to navigate. ### Optimize Your Google Business Profile

For local searches, your Google Business Profile is often the first impression. Make sure you upload recent event photos regularly, collect and respond to reviews, and clearly list your services and service areas. This is one of the highest-intent traffic sources for booking clients.

### Build Visual Consistency

Your brand visuals are a trust signal. Clients assume that how you present your brand reflects how you run your business. Use consistent colors, photography style, and typography. Think of your visuals as your silent salesperson—especially when showcasing **fake flowers centerpiece** designs.
