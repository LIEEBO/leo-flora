## Mixing Fresh and Faux for a High-End Look

One of the best-kept secrets in wedding floral design is mixing fresh flowers with high-quality faux ones. Nhu and Long's wedding achieved a luxurious look by combining real greenery with silk blooms for their hanging installations. This approach gives you the best of both worlds: the fragrance and organic feel of fresh flowers, plus the longevity and structure of artificial ones.

For your **fake flowers centerpiece** ideas, try pairing a few fresh stems with silk roses or peonies in your table arrangements. The faux flowers will hold their shape all day, while the fresh ones add a natural touch. This technique is especially useful for multi-day weddings, as the artificial pieces can be reused for different events without wilting.

## Practical Tips for Recreating This Look

Here are some actionable tips to help you bring this floating floral fantasy to life:

- **Start with a vision board.** Pin images of overhead installations, flower walls, and centerpieces to clarify your style and color scheme.
- **Choose high-quality artificial flowers.** Look for silk or latex blooms that mimic real petals—they'll look far more realistic in photos.
- **Rent or buy a sturdy structure.** For overhead pieces, use a truss system or a wooden frame that can safely support the weight of the flowers.
- **Test your hanging method.** Whether you use fishing line, clear wire, or decorative ribbon, make sure it's secure and won't sag.
- **Build in advance.** Assemble your flower wall and hanging elements a few days before the wedding to reduce last-minute stress.
- **Consider the venue.** Check with your venue about hanging restrictions and ceiling height to ensure your design is feasible.
