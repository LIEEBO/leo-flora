# South Asian Wedding Decor: Affordable Ideas & Venue Tips

Planning a multi-day South Asian wedding is a beautiful whirlwind of color, tradition, and celebration. From the sangeet to the baraat and the reception, every event deserves a stunning backdrop. But with so many events, floral budgets can quickly spiral. The good news? You can achieve a grand look without the grand price tag by mixing smart venue choices with creative floral solutions.

In this guide, we’ll share practical tips for decorating a large venue like The Westin Philadelphia (a popular spot for South Asian weddings), and show you how **affordable wedding flowers**—especially artificial ones—can bring your vision to life.

## Choosing a Venue That Works for Multi-Day Events

When scouting venues, look for flexibility and space. The Westin Philadelphia, for example, offers over 15,000 square feet across 14 event spaces, including a 7,500-square-foot Grand Ballroom that can host 150 to 350 guests. Having ceremony, sangeet, and reception under one roof simplifies logistics and keeps guests close.

But beyond size, consider these practical points:

- **Getting-ready suites:** Ensure there’s ample space for the bridal party and family to prepare.
- **Vendor storage:** A dedicated area for decor and floral supplies keeps things organized.
- **Dance floor and stage:** Confirm if the venue provides these or if you need to rent.
- **Baraat access:** If you plan a baraat, check the entrance and timing rules.

Once you’ve secured the venue, you can focus on **wedding venue decoration ideas** that transform the space.

## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas for a Grand Look

Large ballrooms can feel overwhelming, but strategic decor makes them intimate and festive. Here are ideas that work beautifully for South Asian weddings:

### 1. Mandap and Stage Backdrops

Create a focal point with a lavish mandap or stage backdrop. Use draping fabric in rich colors like marigold, fuchsia, or emerald, and accent with artificial flower garlands. These can be reused across events, saving money and time.

### 2. Table Centerpieces That Wow

Round banquet tables with textured ivory linens look elegant, but centerpieces add the wow factor. Instead of expensive fresh flowers, use **bridal bouquet artificial flowers** and matching centerpieces. Think lush roses, peonies, and orchids in low arrangements that don’t block sightlines.

### 3. Entrance and Foyer Displays

Make a statement from the moment guests arrive. Line the entrance with potted artificial topiaries or floral arches. These are durable, weather-resistant, and can be moved to different areas throughout the weekend.

### 4. Lighting and Fabric

Soft lighting and fabric draping can transform a plain ballroom. Use string lights, uplighting, and sheer drapes to create a warm, romantic atmosphere. Add artificial flower vines to chandeliers or archways for a whimsical touch.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

1. **Plan early:** Order artificial flowers well in advance to allow time for arrangements.
2. **Mix real and faux:** Use artificial flowers for high-impact areas (backdrops, centerpieces) and real flowers for personal bouquets if desired.
3. **Reuse and repurpose:** Design arrangements that can move from ceremony to reception.
4. **Test lighting:** Check how artificial flowers look under venue lighting—some colors may appear different.
5. **Hire help:** If DIY feels overwhelming, hire a florist to arrange your artificial flowers.
