Choosing the perfect artificial tree or plant for your wedding venue can feel overwhelming. You want the lush, romantic look of a garden wedding, but you're worried about getting the size wrong. A tree that's too tall can overwhelm a cozy space, while one that's too small gets lost in a grand ballroom. But with the right measurements and a few styling tricks, you can create a stunning atmosphere that wows your guests.

This guide will walk you through how to measure for artificial trees and plants, where to place them for maximum impact, and how to incorporate them into your wedding venue decoration ideas. Whether you're planning an intimate backyard ceremony or a large hotel reception, these tips will help you choose the perfect faux greenery.

## Why Size Matters for Artificial Trees in Wedding Decor

Before you start shopping, think about the role your artificial tree will play. Is it a dramatic focal point behind the sweetheart table, or a subtle accent to soften a corner? The size should match its purpose and the scale of the room.

**Focal Point Trees:** If you're creating a wedding flower wall backdrop, a tall, full tree can frame it beautifully. For a grand entrance, place a slim olive tree or bamboo in tall planters on either side of the doorway. These make a statement without blocking the flow of guests.

**Accent Trees:** For smaller spaces like a cocktail table or a lounge area, choose compact plants like faux ferns or small topiaries. They add a touch of greenery without overwhelming the scene.

## How to Measure for an Artificial Tree or Plant

Getting the size right starts with measuring your space. Here's a simple method:

1. **Measure the height:** Use a tape measure from the floor to the ceiling. Then, subtract about 2 feet (0.6 meters) for clearance. This gives you the maximum height for your tree.
2. **Measure the width:** Consider the diameter of the tree's widest point. Make sure it fits in the designated area without blocking walkways or doorways.
3. **Consider the pot:** The height of the planter or pot counts toward the total height. If you're using a floor pot, factor that in.

**Pro Tip:** If you're placing a tree behind a head table, you want it to be visible above the heads of seated guests. A tree that's at least 6 feet tall usually works well. For a wedding flower wall backdrop, you might want a tree that's 7-8 feet tall to create a dramatic frame.

## Placement Ideas for Artificial Trees and Plants

Where you place your faux greenery can transform the entire venue. Here are some practical ideas for wedding planners and DIY brides:

### Ceremony Arch

Frame your ceremony arch with two symmetrical artificial trees. This creates a natural, romantic focal point. Choose trees with soft, cascading branches like a weeping willow or a flowering cherry blossom.

### Reception Tables

For centerpieces, use small potted faux plants like succulents or lavender. They add a touch of nature without taking up too much space. For a more dramatic look, place a taller faux tree in the center of a large round table.

### Entryway and Lounge Areas

Greet guests with a pair of tall, slim trees at the entrance. In a lounge area, group a few different-sized plants to create a cozy, garden-like nook.

### Photo Booth Backdrop

Combine a large artificial tree with a wedding flower wall backdrop for a stunning photo op. Guests will love posing in front of the lush greenery.

## Incorporating Bridal Bouquet Artificial Flowers

While trees set the scene, your bridal bouquet artificial flowers complete the look. When choosing your bouquet, consider the scale of your venue. For a grand ballroom, a cascading bouquet with long, flowing stems works beautifully. For a smaller, intimate gathering, a compact round bouquet is perfect.

**Matching Styles:** If you're using a lot of greenery in your decor, choose a bouquet with similar foliage. For a rustic theme, mix eucalyptus and baby's breath. For a modern look, go with minimalist orchids or calla lilies.

**Durability:** Artificial bouquets are a great investment because they last forever. You can even use them as a keepsake after the wedding. Plus, they're perfect for brides who want to avoid wilting flowers on a hot summer day.

## Practical Tips for Using Artificial Trees in Weddings

- **Check the lighting:** If you're using string lights or uplighting, make sure your artificial trees are rated for indoor use and won't be damaged by heat.
- **Secure your trees:** In outdoor settings, wind can be a problem. Use weighted bases or sandbags to keep your trees stable.
- **Think about the season:** For a winter wedding, consider artificial Christmas trees with white lights. For a spring wedding, choose blossoming branches or pastel-colored blooms.
- **Mix and match:** Don't be afraid to combine different heights and textures. A tall tree next to a low planter creates visual interest.

## Conclusion

Choosing the right sized artificial tree doesn't have to be stressful. By measuring your space, considering placement, and thinking about the overall theme, you can create a beautiful, cohesive wedding venue that your guests will remember. Whether you're building a grand entrance, a photo-worthy backdrop, or a cozy lounge, faux trees and plants offer endless possibilities. And with your bridal bouquet artificial flowers, you'll have a stunning, lasting reminder of your special day.
