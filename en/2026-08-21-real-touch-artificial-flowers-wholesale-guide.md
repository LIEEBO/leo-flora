# Real Touch Artificial Flowers Wholesale: A Complete Guide for Wedding Planners and DIY Brides

If you're planning a wedding or decorating an event space, you've probably searched for flowers that look stunning yet stay within budget. That's where Real Touch artificial flowers come in. Unlike standard silk blooms, these premium faux florals feature soft-touch coatings that feel surprisingly lifelike. Whether you're designing a bridal bouquet artificial flowers arrangement or building a wedding flower wall backdrop, understanding how these flowers are made and how to source them wholesale can save you time, money, and stress.

In this guide, we'll walk you through what Real Touch flowers are, how they're manufactured, and what you need to know before placing a wholesale order. By the end, you'll feel confident navigating supplier conversations and making smart choices for your next event.

## What Does "Real Touch" Actually Mean in Wholesale Sourcing?

Real Touch artificial flowers are a step above typical silk flowers. The petals are coated with materials like latex or polyurethane (PU), giving them a soft, almost velvety feel that mimics fresh blooms. In the wholesale world, "Real Touch" isn't just a marketing buzzword—it's a material promise you can verify through samples.

When you're sourcing these flowers for a project like a fake flowers centerpiece or a bridal bouquet, you should ask suppliers exactly what creates that touch effect. Common options include:

- **Latex coating** – Soft and petal-like, ideal for roses, tulips, and orchids that will be viewed up close.
- **PU coating** – Slightly firmer, offering better durability if flowers will be handled repeatedly.
- **Foam or fabric core with coating** – Affects how the flower drapes, how thick the edges are, and how well it recovers after being packed.
- **Mixed-material construction** – Often combines Real Touch petals with silk leaves or plastic stems for balance and realism.

The material you choose impacts not just the look and feel, but also the cost, color consistency, and how the flowers survive shipping. That's why it's essential to know what you're buying before you commit to a large order.

## What to Know Before Buying Real Touch Flowers Wholesale

If you're buying in bulk for a wedding or event business, you'll want to plan carefully. Here are the key details to confirm with any supplier:

### Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) and Lead Times

Most wholesale suppliers require a minimum order of 500–576 pieces per style. Samples typically take about 15 days to arrive, while bulk production runs 60–90 days after you approve the sample and pay the deposit. If you're on a tight timeline, factor this in early.

### Shipping and Trade Terms

Most China-based exporters quote FOB Xiamen, with sea freight as the standard shipping method. For larger assortments, you'll likely use a full container load (FCL). For trial orders, less-than-container-load (LCL) is a good option to test the waters.

### Quality Control Risks

When ordering Real Touch flowers, watch out for these common issues:
- Inconsistent petal feel across batches
- Color drift between samples and bulk production
- Weak stems that can't support heavy blooms
- Petals crushed or deformed from packing pressure

Always request samples before committing to a full order. This is your chance to check the touch, color, and durability firsthand.

## Practical Tips for Using Real Touch Flowers in Your Decor

Once you have your flowers, here are some tips to make them shine:

1. **Condition them after unpacking** – Gently fluff petals and reshape stems. Real Touch flowers often need a few minutes to recover from being packed.
2. **Mix with greenery** – Pair Real Touch blooms with silk eucalyptus or ferns to add texture and fullness to arrangements.
3. **Store properly** – Keep flowers out of direct sunlight to prevent fading. Store them in a cool, dry place with room to breathe.
4. **Use floral foam for centerpieces** – Even though stems are artificial, floral foam helps you position them securely in vases or containers.
5. **Build your wall backdrop in sections** – If you're creating a wedding flower wall backdrop, attach flowers to a grid or panel in small sections. This makes it easier to transport and set up.
