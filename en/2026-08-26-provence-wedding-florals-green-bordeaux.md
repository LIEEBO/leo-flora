When Carly and Thomas chose Chateau de Sannes for their Provence wedding, they knew the property would be the star. Nestled among vineyards and olive groves, the chateau provided a breathtaking backdrop for a celebration that flowed effortlessly from a garden ceremony to a long family-style table. But what truly set the tone was the floral palette—a rich combination of green and bordeaux that echoed the natural beauty of the French countryside.

For wedding planners and DIY brides looking to recreate this look, the key is balancing elegance with practicality. While fresh flowers are stunning, many couples are turning to high-quality artificial flowers for wedding decor to achieve the same aesthetic with less stress and cost. Here’s how you can bring a touch of Provence to your own celebration.

## The Magic of Green and Bordeaux

Green and bordeaux is a timeless pairing that feels both romantic and grounded. The deep wine tones of bordeaux add warmth and sophistication, while lush greenery brings a fresh, organic feel. At Chateau de Sannes, this combination was used in cascading bouquets, table runners, and even the ceremony arch, creating a cohesive look that tied the entire venue together.

One of the easiest ways to incorporate this palette is through a wedding flower wall backdrop. Imagine a lush wall of eucalyptus, ivy, and dark red roses—it’s the perfect photo opportunity and a stunning focal point for your ceremony or reception. Whether you use fresh or faux blooms, a flower wall instantly elevates the space and gives guests a memorable experience.

## Why Choose Artificial Flowers for Wedding Decor?

While fresh flowers have their charm, artificial flowers offer several advantages that busy brides and planners appreciate. First, they are weather-proof—no wilting under the Provence sun or drooping in unexpected rain. Second, they can be prepared weeks in advance, saving you last-minute stress. Finally, they are cost-effective, especially when you purchase wholesale silk flowers in bulk.

Modern silk flowers look incredibly realistic, and with a little creativity, guests won’t be able to tell the difference. You can mix them with fresh greenery or use them entirely on their own. For destination weddings like this one, artificial flowers are a game-changer because you can ship them ahead and assemble everything on-site without worrying about refrigeration or hydration.

## Practical Tips for a Provence-Inspired Look

Ready to recreate this style? Here are some practical tips to get you started:

- **Focus on texture:** Combine different shades of green—sage, eucalyptus, olive—with velvety bordeaux roses and darker accents like dried berries or thistle. Texture adds depth and realism.
- **Use a flower wall strategically:** Place your wedding flower wall backdrop behind the sweetheart table, at the entrance, or as a photo booth. It’s a versatile piece that can also double as a ceremony backdrop.
- **Go big with centerpieces:** Long family-style tables call for abundant, low arrangements. Use a mix of trailing greenery and bordeaux blooms to create a lush, abundant feel without blocking conversation.
- **Incorporate candles:** The soft glow of candles against deep red and green florals creates an intimate, romantic atmosphere. Pair pillar candles with scattered petals for a polished look.

## Where to Source Wholesale Silk Flowers

If you’re planning a large event, buying wholesale silk flowers is the smartest way to stay on budget. Look for suppliers that offer a wide range of colors and flower types, including realistic roses, peonies, and greenery. Many online retailers offer bulk discounts and even custom color matching, so you can get the exact shade of bordeaux you envision.

Before you order, order samples first. This lets you see the quality and color in person, ensuring they match your theme perfectly. Also, consider mixing high-end silk flowers with more affordable faux greenery to balance cost and realism.
