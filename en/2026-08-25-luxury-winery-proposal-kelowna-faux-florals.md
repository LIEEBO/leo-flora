## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

If you’re inspired to create your own luxury winery proposal or wedding setup, here are a few tips:

- **Choose a focal point**—like a heart-shaped floral arrangement or an arch—and build the rest of the decor around it.
- **Mix textures**—combine silk flowers with greenery and candles for depth and warmth.
- **Plan for the weather**—outdoor venues can be windy or sunny; secure all elements and opt for sturdy faux stems.
- **Test your lighting**—visit the venue at the same time of day as your event to see how the light hits your decor.
- **Keep it personal**—add a custom sign, a favorite quote, or a meaningful song to make the moment uniquely yours.
