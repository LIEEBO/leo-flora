## Faux Floral Arrangements: Practical Beauty for Your Wedding

One of the biggest challenges for coastal weddings is wind and weather. At Castle Hill Inn, the breeze off the bay is part of the charm, but it can wreak havoc on delicate fresh petals. That’s where **faux floral arrangements** shine. They hold their shape, don’t wilt, and can be pre-assembled days ahead. For your own wedding, try mixing faux blooms with preserved greenery for a natural look. A cascading bouquet or altar arrangement can be built with high-end artificial flowers that photograph just as beautifully as fresh ones.

A standout element of many Newport weddings is the ceremony backdrop. Imagine a lush wall of white roses, pale blue delphiniums, and ivy framing your vows. ## Practical Tips for Recreating This Look

1. **Choose a cohesive color palette.** Stick to whites, greens, and soft blues for that coastal garden feel. Use **fake flowers centerpiece** designs that mix textures—matte petals with glossy leaves—to add depth.
2. **Invest in high-quality faux blooms.** Not all artificial flowers are equal. Look for silk or latex options that feel realistic to the touch.
3. **Build your own flower wall.** With a grid base and zip ties, you can create a **wedding flower wall backdrop** in a weekend. Attach stems in layers for fullness.
4. **Mix fresh and faux strategically.** Use real flowers for the bouquet (if you love the scent) and faux for large-scale decor like centerpieces and arches. This balances budget and authenticity.
5. **Test in natural light.** Faux flowers can look different under the sun or indoor lighting. Set up a mock arrangement at your venue beforehand.
