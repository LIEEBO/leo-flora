## Why Faux Florals Work for Coastal Garden Weddings

A coastal garden wedding, like the one at Castle Hill Inn, relies on a lush, romantic feel that blends seamlessly with the natural surroundings. The original wedding featured white, ivory, green, and pale blue hues, with an emphasis on floral design. Realistic artificial flowers allow you to achieve that same look without worrying about weather, transport, or freshness. For a venue with ocean breezes, faux blooms stay perfect all day—no drooping petals or browning edges. Plus, you can pre-assemble your arrangements weeks in advance, which is a huge time-saver for DIY brides. When choosing your faux floral arrangements, look for high-quality silk or latex flowers that mimic real peonies, roses, and hydrangeas. These materials catch light beautifully and add depth to your decor.

## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas Inspired by Newport

To recreate the spirit of a Newport summer, focus on layering textures and colors. Start with a neutral base—white linens, ivory tablecloths, and pale blue accents. Then, incorporate greenery like eucalyptus or ferns in your faux floral arrangements. For the ceremony, consider an arch draped in realistic artificial flowers and trailing vines. For reception tables, mix tall and low centerpieces: tall arrangements in clear vases with floating candles, and low clusters of flowers in ceramic pots. Don't forget the little details—place card holders with tiny blooms, or a faux-flower crown for the bride. These wedding venue decoration ideas work whether you're at a historic estate, a beachfront hotel, or a garden venue. The key is to keep the palette soft and the design airy, just like the original Newport wedding.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides Using Artificial Flowers

If you're planning to DIY your wedding florals, realistic artificial flowers are your best friend. Here are a few tips to make your project smooth:

- **Start with a color palette.** Stick to white, ivory, green, and pale blue to match the coastal garden theme. Buy stems in bulk from reputable online sellers that specialize in high-end faux blooms.
- **Invest in good greenery.** Realistic leaves and filler flowers make the biggest difference. Mix different shades of green for depth.
- **Use floral foam and sturdy bases.** For centerpieces, use wet floral foam (even for faux flowers) to keep stems secure. For arches, choose a lightweight frame and attach flowers with zip ties or hot glue.
- **Add real elements if you wish.** Some brides combine faux flowers with a few real blooms for scent or texture. That's perfectly fine—just ensure the faux ones are high quality so they don't clash.
- **Test your arrangements ahead of time.** Set up a mock table or ceremony arch a few weeks before the wedding to see how everything looks in natural light. Adjust colors or heights as needed.

## Why Realistic Artificial Flowers Are a Smart Investment

Many brides worry that artificial flowers will look cheap or plastic. But today's realistic artificial flowers are incredibly lifelike—think soft petals, subtle color variations, and even scent options. They're also cost-effective: you can reuse them for other events, sell them after the wedding, or keep them as keepsakes. For wedding planners, using faux floral arrangements means less worry about delivery delays or flower availability. Plus, they're eco-friendly if you choose high-quality options that last for years. In a coastal setting like Newport, where wind and salt air can damage real blooms, faux flowers hold up beautifully. They allow you to focus on the overall design and guest experience, rather than frantically refreshing flower buckets.
