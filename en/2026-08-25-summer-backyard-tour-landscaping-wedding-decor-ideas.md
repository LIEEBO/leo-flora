## How to Bring This Look to Your Wedding or Event

You don’t need a massive garden to capture this aesthetic. Here are some practical tips for using faux florals to create a similar vibe:

- **Mix textures and heights:** Use a variety of silk flowers—like hydrangeas, roses, and eucalyptus—to create depth in your centerpieces. A **fake flowers centerpiece** can look just as lush as fresh blooms, especially when you layer different shades of pink and green.
- **Go big with installs:** For a dramatic entrance or ceremony arch, consider using **wholesale silk flowers** in bulk. They’re cost-effective and can be pre-arranged, saving you time and stress on the big day.
- **Incorporate evergreens:** Evergreen branches and faux greenery add structure and keep your decor looking full year-round. Use them in table runners or hanging installations.
- **Think about color palettes:** Blush pink, soft white, and deep green are timeless and romantic. Choose silk flowers that match your wedding colors for a cohesive look.

## Practical Tips from Our Landscaping Journey

If you’re planning a large-scale project (like a wedding venue or event space), here are a few lessons we learned:

- **Hire professionals early:** Book your landscaper or decorator in the fall for a spring install—it’s their slower time, so you’ll get more attention.
- **Finalize designs quickly:** We had our design locked by February, and the installation was done by late April. The same goes for your wedding florals—order your silk flowers early to ensure availability.
- **Embrace phases:** You don’t have to do everything at once. Start with key areas and expand over time. The same applies to decor: focus on high-impact spots like the ceremony backdrop and reception tables.
