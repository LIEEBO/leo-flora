# How to Choose the Right Sized Artificial Tree and Plant

Are you dreaming of lush greenery but worried about finding the perfect faux tree for your space? You're not alone. Many wedding planners and DIY brides face the same challenge—falling in love with a gorgeous artificial tree only to discover it's too tall or too wide once it arrives. But don't fret! With a few simple measurements and style tips, you'll find the ideal artificial plant that not only fits your room but also elevates your décor—whether you're decorating a venue or your own home.

## Step 1: Decide Where Your Tree or Plant Will Live

Before you start browsing, think about the purpose of your artificial greenery. Is it a focal point or a subtle accent? Will it be seen from seated or standing eye level? This will guide your size and style choices.

- **Living Room:** A large faux fiddle leaf fig or bird of paradise creates an eye-catching anchor in corners or beside sofas.
- **Foyer:** Looking for the best plants in foyer spaces? Try a tall, slim faux olive or bamboo tree to create a grand entrance.
- **Home Office:** Need a touch of greenery to boost creativity? Small faux palms or snake plants work beautifully near desks.
- **Kitchen Nook:** A colorful faux arrangement in a terracotta planter adds cheer without taking up much space.
- **Small Spaces:** Even a dorm or studio can benefit from faux plants—choose compact greenery with slim bases to save floor space.

💡 **Interior design tip:** Faux plants soften harsh lines and bring a calming, natural balance to your décor.

### Keep in mind when decorating with your artificial greenery:
- **Center of Attention:** Some items are subtle, while others—like vibrant artificial arrangements—can add a pop of color and command attention.
- **Furniture:** Choose greenery that blends with your existing décor. For eclectic styles, mix different plant types freely.
- **Safety:** If you have small children, fireplaces, candles, or pets, place your new tree in a safe spot.
- **Theme:** From minimalist to rustic, there's a tree for every style. Keep your décor scheme consistent.
- **Pets:** Our plants are so realistic, some animals might think they're real. Discourage pets from chewing on them.

## Step 2: How to Measure for an Artificial Tree or Plant

Measuring is easier than you think. Follow these simple steps:

1. **Measure your ceiling height**—you want the tree to be at least 12-18 inches shorter than the ceiling for breathing room.
2. **Measure the width** of the area where the tree will sit—account for the tree's full spread, not just the pot.
3. **Consider the pot size**—a large pot adds height but also takes floor space.
4. **Visualize the shape**—tall and slim for narrow corners, round and bushy for open areas.

For artificial Christmas trees, the same rules apply. A 6-foot tree works well in most rooms, but if you have high ceilings, go taller. For everyday greenery, a 3-4 foot tree is perfect for tabletops or sideboards.

## Step 3: Choose the Right Size and Shape for Your Space

Now that you have measurements, think about the shape. A tall, narrow tree fits tight corners, while a fuller tree anchors a large room. - **For a tabletop:** Small topiaries or succulent arrangements add charm.

## Step 4: Practical Tips for Perfect Placement

- **Scale matters:** A tree that's too small gets lost; one that's too big overwhelms.
- **Eye level:** Place tall trees where they're seen from standing eye level, and shorter ones for seated views.
- **Lighting:** Highlight your artificial greenery with warm lights to enhance its realism.
- **Balance:** Pair a large tree with smaller plants to create visual interest.

Choosing the right sized artificial tree or plant doesn't have to be daunting.
