# Detroit Hotels & Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas: A Travel + Floral Guide

When I first visited Detroit in 2013, I never imagined I'd end up buying a house there. But the city's raw energy, historic architecture, and creative spirit hooked me. Now, years later, Detroit has transformed into a must-visit destination for couples seeking a unique wedding backdrop. Whether you're planning a wedding in the Motor City or just scouting for inspiration, these hotels offer stunning settings—and I'll share how to elevate them with **artificial flowers for wedding** decor.

## Why Detroit Deserves a Spot on Your Wedding Travel List

Detroit isn't just about cars and Motown; it's a city of rebirth. Once-vacant skyscrapers now house boutique hotels, and historic buildings have been lovingly restored. For wedding planners and DIY brides, this means a wealth of **wedding venue decoration ideas** that blend old-world charm with modern luxury. Imagine exchanging vows under a cast-iron dome or dancing in a Parisian-inspired bistro—Detroit delivers.

## Top Detroit Hotels for Wedding Stays & Inspiration

### 1. The Siren Hotel: A Secret Rooftop Gem

Perched in the former Wurlitzer building, The Siren Hotel offers a glass-enclosed terrace with panoramic views of downtown. It's a favorite for pre-wedding gatherings or intimate elopements. The space feels exclusive and romantic—perfect for a small ceremony with a **bridal bouquet artificial flowers** that match the vintage vibe.

### 2. Roost at Book Tower: Paris in the Midwest

Set inside the 1920s Book Tower, Roost is a love letter to Paris. The three-story rotunda, with its 6,000 glass panels and 7,000 jewel embellishments, is one of Detroit's most romantic interiors. The 117 apartment-style rooms feature full kitchens and live plants, ideal for a wedding party's home base. For a grand entrance, imagine a bride descending the rotunda stairs with a trailing bouquet of **artificial flowers for wedding**—no wilting, just perfection.

### 3. The Aladdin Sane Lounge & Le Suprême

At Roost, you'll find Le Suprême, a French bistro that could be straight out of Paris, and The Aladdin Sane lounge, where cocktails are mixed by candlelight. These venues are perfect for rehearsal dinners or post-wedding brunches. Decorate with faux florals to keep the ambiance elegant without the hassle of fresh flowers.

## Practical Tips for Wedding Decoration with Artificial Flowers

### 1. Embrace the Architecture

Detroit's hotels are architectural marvels. Instead of hiding them, use **wedding venue decoration ideas** that highlight the space. For instance, at the Book Tower's rotunda, place tall arrangements of artificial flowers at the base of columns to draw the eye upward.

### 2. Mix Textures & Colors

Artificial flowers have come a long way. Choose high-quality silk blooms that mimic real petals. Combine them with greenery, berries, or even gold accents to complement the hotel's decor. For a Parisian theme, think soft roses, lavender, and eucalyptus.

### 3. Create a Focal Point

Use a **bridal bouquet artificial flowers** as a key photo prop. Since they won't wilt, you can take photos before the ceremony, during, and after—no last-minute replacements. Plus, you can keep the bouquet as a keepsake.

### 4. Plan for Lighting

Many historic hotels have dim, romantic lighting. Test your floral arrangements in the venue's actual light. Artificial flowers can look even more stunning under string lights or chandeliers, so don't be afraid to add sparkle.
