## Why Floating Flowers Are a Game-Changer

One of the most memorable elements of Nhu and Long’s celebration was the overhead floral installation. Flowers appeared to float effortlessly above the guests, creating a dreamy, ethereal atmosphere. This design trick works wonders in large venues with high ceilings, drawing the eye upward and making the space feel more intimate. The best part? You don’t need fresh blooms to achieve this look. Using high-quality artificial flowers for wedding installations allows you to create dramatic, gravity-defying arrangements that stay perfect from the first dance to the last toast.

For planners, floating flowers are also a logistical win. They can be pre-assembled, hung safely, and reused for multiple events—ideal for multi-day weddings like Nhu and Long’s. Plus, they eliminate the worry of wilting petals or allergic reactions among guests.

## The Beauty of a Bridal Bouquet That Lasts Forever

While the overhead florals were the showstopper, the bride’s bouquet was equally stunning. Nhu carried a lush arrangement that complemented the modern aesthetic of the day. Many brides are now opting for a bridal bouquet artificial flowers—not just for their durability, but for their versatility. You can choose blooms that are out of season, experiment with unique colors, and even add non-floral elements like succulents or feathers without worrying about freshness.

A faux bouquet also serves as a keepsake. After the wedding, you can preserve it in a shadow box or use it as a centerpiece in your home. For destination weddings in humid climates like Vietnam, artificial flowers are a practical choice—they won’t droop in the heat and can be transported easily.

## Creating a Fake Flowers Centerpiece That Wows

The reception tables at Gem were adorned with centerpieces that echoed the floating theme. Low, lush arrangements with cascading vines and modern geometric vases created a cohesive look. If you’re planning your own wedding, consider a fake flowers centerpiece as your base. Not only are they cost-effective, but they also allow you to build your tablescape weeks in advance.

Mix realistic faux blooms with dried elements like pampas grass or bunny tails for texture. Use clear vases filled with water or fairy lights to mimic the freshness of real flowers. The key is to choose high-quality silk or latex flowers that look natural up close. Your guests won’t be able to tell the difference—and you’ll save your budget for other details.

## Practical Tips for Using Artificial Flowers in Your Wedding

If you’re inspired by Nhu and Long’s floral-forward design, here are some actionable tips for incorporating artificial flowers for wedding decor:

- **Mix high and low:** Use premium faux flowers for focal points (bouquets, centerpieces) and more affordable options for bulk decor like garlands or backdrop draping.
- **Think about texture:** Combine matte and glossy petals, velvet leaves, and metallic accents to add depth and realism.
- **Test in natural light:** Artificial flowers can look different under sunlight vs. indoor lighting. Always check your arrangements in the actual venue before the big day.
- **Rent or buy?** If you’re a planner, consider renting faux floral installations. For DIY brides, buying and reselling after the wedding is a smart way to recoup costs.
- **Don’t forget the scent:** Since faux flowers don’t have a fragrance, add a few drops of essential oil or scented candles nearby to engage the senses.
