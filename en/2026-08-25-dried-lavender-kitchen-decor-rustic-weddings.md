## Why Dried Lavender Works for Wedding Decor

Dried lavender is not just for kitchens—it's a beautiful addition to wedding decor. Whether you're styling a barn reception or an intimate backyard gathering, dried lavender adds texture, color, and a calming scent. It pairs beautifully with **silk flowers wedding decor**, creating a cohesive look that feels both organic and polished.

According to Ntola Obazee, creative director at Kutchenhaus, 'Dried lavender can be used across the entire home, and we often see it hung up in places such as the bathroom or on doors. However, what most people don’t automatically think of is using it in the kitchen. If you have a rail above the worktop, or at the end of your cabinet or island, then lavender, along with other dried herbs, is great at adding scent as well as visual interest to the space.' This advice translates perfectly to wedding venues, where lavender can be hung from beams, draped over tables, or tucked into centerpieces.

## How to Get the Look: Tips for Using Dried Lavender

Clodagh’s kitchen features large Belfast sinks, rich dark wood finishes, a flagstone floor, and open shelving—and hanging from the beams are bunches of dried lavender. She spaced them between cookware, creating a fairytale effect. To recreate this in your own home or wedding, follow these practical tips:

- **Hang from beams or shelves:** Use twine or ribbon to tie small bunches of dried lavender. Space them evenly along a wooden beam or shelf for a rustic touch.
- **Mix with faux florals:** Combine dried lavender with **faux floral arrangements** to create long-lasting centerpieces that don’t wilt. This is especially useful for weddings, where you want everything to look fresh all day.
- **Use as place setting accents:** Tuck a small sprig of lavender into napkin rings or place cards for an elegant, fragrant detail.
- **Create a lavender garland:** String lavender along with eucalyptus or baby’s breath for a stunning garland that can drape over tables or doorways.

## The Benefits of Mixing Dried and Faux Flowers

Dried lavender is a low-maintenance alternative to fresh flowers, but it can be fragile. For event decor, mixing dried elements with **silk flowers wedding decor** is a smart strategy. Silk flowers look realistic, last forever, and can be reused for multiple events. Plus, they come in a variety of colors and styles, so you can match any wedding theme.
