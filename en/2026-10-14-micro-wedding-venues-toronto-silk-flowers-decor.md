# Micro Wedding Venues in Toronto: A Floral Lover's Guide to Small Celebrations

Planning a micro wedding in Toronto gives you more flexibility over your guest list, venue style, and level of planning support. From restaurant receptions and historic houses to creative studios and all-inclusive packages, the city has intimate venues for different guest counts, budgets, and wedding-day plans. But choosing the right space is only half the story. For DIY brides and wedding planners, the real magic happens in the details — especially the flowers.

In this guide, we'll walk through what to look for in a Toronto micro wedding venue, share standout options, and show you how silk flowers wedding decor can transform a small space without blowing your budget.

## How to Choose a Micro Wedding Venue in Toronto

Start with your guest count, then compare what each venue includes — from catering and décor to tables, chairs, staffing, and cleanup. When comparing venues, also look at outside-vendor policies, getting-ready space, parking, accessibility, setup times, and event end times. These details affect both your budget and how smoothly the day runs.

Before booking, confirm the venue's current capacity, package details, pricing, and availability. And if you're planning to bring in your own décor — like faux floral arrangements — ask about their outside-vendor policy early. Some venues allow it; others don't. Knowing this upfront saves you from last-minute surprises.

## Practical Tips for Styling a Micro Wedding with Faux Flowers

- **Mix textures and heights.** Combine different types of blooms and greenery to create depth. Tall arrangements work well in venues with high ceilings.
- **Focus on key areas.** Prioritize the ceremony backdrop, aisle, and head table. These are the spots that photographs capture most.
- **Reuse and repurpose.** Use the same arrangements for the ceremony and reception by moving them during the cocktail hour.
- **Test your lighting.** Silk flowers can look different under warm vs. cool lighting. Check them in the venue if possible.
- **Order samples first.** Before committing to a large wholesale order, request samples to check color and quality.
