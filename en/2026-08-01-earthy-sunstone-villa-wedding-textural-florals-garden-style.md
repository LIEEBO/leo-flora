## How to Design Your Own Textural Floral Arrangements

Creating a contemporary garden style with faux flowers is easier than you think. Here are practical tips to get that high-end, organic look:

### 1. Mix Textures, Not Just Colors

The key to a natural, garden-style arrangement is variety. Combine smooth petals (like roses) with spiky elements (like lavender or veronica) and soft fillers (like amaranth or astilbe). Add in dried grasses or preserved foliage for that earthy vibe.

### 2. Use a Loose, Asymmetrical Shape

Avoid tight, round bouquets. Instead, let stems fall naturally, creating a cascading or asymmetrical silhouette. This mimics how flowers grow in a garden—effortless and unstructured.

### 3. Layer Foliage First

Start your arrangement by building a base of greenery. Eucalyptus, ferns, and olive branches provide structure and fullness. Then, add your focal flowers, and finish with accent blooms and grasses.

### 4. Choose Quality Silk Flowers

Invest in high-quality **faux floral arrangements** that feel soft to the touch. Look for flowers with wired stems so you can bend and shape them easily. Mixing a few preserved real elements (like dried grasses) with silk flowers adds authenticity.

## Practical Tips for Wedding Planners and DIY Brides

If you’re planning a garden-style wedding, here are some insider tips:

- **Plan your color palette** – Stick to 3-5 complementary shades to keep the look cohesive.
- **Order samples first** – Before buying wholesale, order a few stems to check color and quality.
- **Create a timeline** – Assemble your arrangements 1-2 weeks before the wedding, and store them in a cool, dry place.
- **Repurpose ceremony florals** – Use altar arrangements as centerpieces at the reception to maximize your investment.
- **Consider rental decor** – If you don’t want to keep the flowers, some companies rent silk arrangements, which is even more sustainable.
