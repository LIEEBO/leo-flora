## What Is Ralph Lauren-Inspired Christmas Décor?

This trend blends classic American heritage style with luxurious holiday textures and colors. Picture deep navy and hunter-green tartan ribbons, gleaming gold nutcrackers, velvet bows, and metallic finishes. It’s equal parts rustic lodge and Park Avenue sophistication—a balance that works beautifully for weddings.

Key features include:

- **Tartan Plaids**: Green, navy, and red patterns that evoke equestrian charm.
- **Gold Accents**: Nutcrackers, reindeer, and candle holders that add a regal touch.
- **Velvet & Ribbon**: Deep, rich textures that bring warmth and dimension.
- **Natural Greenery**: Garlands, wreaths, and cedar sprays to soften the glamour.
- **Classic Candlelight**: Etched glass or metallic votives for a nostalgic glow.

## Why This Look Is Perfect for Weddings

Ralph Lauren-inspired décor is more than a color scheme—it’s a feeling.
