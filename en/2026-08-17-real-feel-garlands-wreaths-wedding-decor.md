When planning a wedding, every detail matters—from the aisle to the reception tables. One trend that has taken the wedding world by storm is the use of real feel garlands and wreaths. These lifelike faux florals offer the beauty of fresh greenery without the stress of wilting or maintenance. In this guide, we'll explore how these versatile pieces can elevate your wedding decor, whether you're a DIY bride or a seasoned event planner.

## Why Choose Real Feel Garlands and Wreaths?

Real feel garlands and wreaths go by many names: real touch, PE, realistic, true-to-life, and faux. Their popularity among social media influencers is no accident—they bring a touch of nature to any setting while being incredibly practical. Here are just a few reasons to consider them for your big day:

- **Lifelike Appearance**: Designed to mimic the look and texture of fresh greenery, they create a warm and inviting atmosphere without the upkeep.
- **Durability**: Unlike fresh greenery, these garlands and wreaths won't wilt or dry out. You can enjoy their beauty throughout the entire wedding—from ceremony to reception—and even reuse them for future events.
- **Versatility**: Use them to drape over archways, line the aisle, adorn mantels, or accent tables. Their classic look complements any decor style.
- **Allergy-Friendly**: For guests sensitive to pollen or other allergens, real feel options provide the beauty of greenery without the sneezing.
- **Easy Storage**: After the wedding, simply store them for your next event or even as home decor. No need to buy new decorations each time.
- **No Maintenance Required**: Enjoy the aesthetic benefits without the hassle of watering or pruning.

## Creative Ways to Use Real Feel Garlands in Weddings

Real feel garlands are incredibly flexible. Here are some creative ways to incorporate them into your wedding decor:

- **Ceremony Arch**: Drape a lush garland over a wooden arch or metal frame to create a stunning focal point for your vows.
- **Aisle Decor**: Line the aisle with smaller garlands or intertwine them with fairy lights for a magical walk down the aisle.
- **Table Runners**: Use garlands as table runners for a rustic or boho look. Pair with candles and silk flowers wedding decor for an elegant touch.
- **Backdrop for Photo Booth**: Create a beautiful wedding flower wall backdrop by attaching garlands to a frame. This not only serves as a photo backdrop but also adds depth and texture to your venue.

## Wreaths: More Than Just a Door Decoration

While wreaths are often associated with front doors, they can be used in many creative ways at a wedding:

- **Welcome Sign**: Hang a wreath above the welcome table or use it as a base for a signage display.
- **Chair Decor**: Attach small wreaths to the backs of chairs for a charming detail.
- **Ceiling Hangings**: Suspend wreaths from the ceiling at varying heights for a whimsical, floating garden effect.
- **Centerpieces**: Use a wreath as a base for a centerpiece, filling the center with candles or flowers.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

If you're planning to use real feel garlands and wreaths, here are some practical tips to ensure success:

1. **Choose the Right Length and Size**: Garlands come in lengths from 5 to 15 feet, and wreaths range from 24 to 36 inches. Measure your space before purchasing to ensure a perfect fit.
2. **Mix Styles**: Combine pine, cedar, or a mix of both to add variety and depth. You can also mix in some wholesale silk flowers to add color and personalization.
3. **Lighting Options**: Opt for lit garlands or wreaths to add a warm glow to evening events. Battery-operated lights are easy to manage and safe for indoor use.
4. **Storage and Reuse**: After the wedding, carefully pack your garlands and wreaths in storage bins. They can be reused for anniversaries, holidays, or even as home decor.
5. **Test the Look**: Before the big day, set up a mock arrangement to see how the garlands and wreaths look in your venue's lighting. This will help you make any adjustments in advance.
