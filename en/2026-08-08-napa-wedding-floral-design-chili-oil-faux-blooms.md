## H2: Designing Your Own Dramatic Color Shift

Ready to recreate the chili oil effect? Here’s how to plan your own floral transformation:

### 1. Start with a Neutral Base

For the ceremony and cocktail hour, stick to white and green. Think white roses, ranunculus, and eucalyptus. These colors feel airy and romantic, setting a calm tone. Use **artificial flowers for wedding** in this phase to save money—you can reuse them later or mix with fresh accents.

### 2. Introduce a Bold Accent for the Reception

As guests move to dinner, swap in deep reds, burgundies, and even orange-toned blooms. In the Napa wedding, the red symbolized chili oil—a nod to the couple’s love of cooking. You can choose any bold color that resonates with your story. Use **fake flowers centerpiece** arrangements with red dahlias, garden roses, or anemones to create that pop.

### 3. Play with Lighting and Texture

Lighting amplifies the transformation. During the ceremony, keep lighting soft and natural. For the reception, use warm, dim lighting that makes the reds glow. Mix textures—velvet ribbons, matte ceramics, and glossy leaves—to add depth.

### 4. Create a Seamless Transition

If you’re using a mix of fresh and faux, plan how the switch happens. For example, have ceremony arrangements that can be easily moved to the reception, or use different centerpieces that are pre-set. With **realistic artificial flowers**, you can even have your coordinator swap out just the focal blooms.
