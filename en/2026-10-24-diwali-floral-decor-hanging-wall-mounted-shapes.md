## Practical Tips for Stunning Floral Décor

- **Hydrate properly:** Soak the foam thoroughly before inserting stems. For fresh flowers, this ensures longevity; for artificial flowers, it helps secure the stems.
- **Choose the right flowers:** Marigolds, chrysanthemums, and roses are traditional and work well. For artificial options, look for high-quality realistic artificial flowers that mimic the texture and color of fresh blooms.
- **Mix textures:** Combine different flower sizes and greenery to add depth. Artificial greenery like faux fern or ivy can fill gaps nicely.
- **Secure hanging:** Use sturdy hooks or ropes, especially for outdoor use. Double-check the weight capacity.
- **Reuse and store:** After the event, remove flowers, clean the foam, and store shapes for future use. Artificial flowers can be reused indefinitely.
