## Golden Blooms at Lan Su: Chrysanthemum Showcase Celebrates Floral Art

Every fall, Portland’s Lan Su Chinese Garden transforms into a breathtaking display of autumn’s bounty during its annual Chrysanthemum Floral Design Showcase. This year, floral artist Amanda Bayha took first place with a stunning red, orange, and gold floral sculpture that captured the essence of the season. Her creation, like the other entries, highlights the chrysanthemum’s versatility and its deep cultural significance. For wedding planners and DIY brides, these showcases offer a treasure trove of inspiration—especially when you want to capture that golden autumn palette without the fleeting lifespan of fresh flowers.

### Why Chrysanthemums Are Making a Comeback in Weddings

Chrysanthemums are often overlooked in wedding design, but their intricate petals, rich colors, and symbolic meanings (longevity, joy, and fidelity) make them a hidden gem. The Lan Su showcase demonstrates how these blooms can be used in dramatic, sculptural arrangements that feel both modern and timeless. For couples planning fall weddings, incorporating chrysanthemums—whether fresh or artificial—adds warmth and texture. And with realistic artificial flowers, you can enjoy their beauty long after the season ends, without worrying about wilting or allergies.

### Bringing the Showcase Home: Tips for Your Own Floral Art

1. **Mix Textures and Tones**: Bayha’s winning design combined deep reds, burnt oranges, and shimmering golds. Recreate this by layering different flower types—chrysanthemums, dahlias, and autumn leaves—in both fresh and faux varieties. Silk flowers wedding decor can seamlessly blend with real blooms, giving you the best of both worlds.
2. **Create a Focal Point**: Use a large floral sculpture or arch as your ceremony backdrop. A wedding flower wall backdrop made of silk chrysanthemums and cascading greenery can be a showstopper that guests will remember.
3. **Think Beyond the Vase**: Chrysanthemums can be used in unconventional ways—floating in water features, woven into centerpieces, or even as hair accessories. Artificial versions are durable and hold their shape, making them perfect for intricate designs.
4. **Embrace the Season**: Don’t limit yourself to fall colors; chrysanthemums come in a wide range of hues. Use them to add pops of color to any wedding theme, from rustic to modern.
