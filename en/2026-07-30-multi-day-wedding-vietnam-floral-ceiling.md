The most striking element of Nhu and Long’s wedding was the overhead floral installation. Hundreds of blooms in soft pinks, whites, and greens appeared to float above the guests. This effect is achievable with a **wedding flower wall backdrop** suspended from the ceiling or a custom-built frame. Nhu and Long honored Vietnamese traditions while embracing contemporary aesthetics. The ceremony space featured a minimalist arch adorned with cascading greenery and flowers. For a similar look, use **affordable wedding flowers** like baby’s breath, eucalyptus, and carnations arranged in a modern, asymmetrical design. ## Practical Tips for Recreating This Look

1. **Start with a focal point** – Whether it’s a floral ceiling or a wall backdrop, choose one area to invest in. Use **affordable wedding flowers** for the rest of the decor.
2. **Mix real and artificial** – Combine fresh blooms with high-quality silk flowers to save money while maintaining a lush appearance. **Bridal bouquet artificial flowers** are perfect for the bride’s bouquet as they can be kept as a keepsake.
3. **Consider a multi-day setup** – If your wedding spans multiple days, use artificial flowers for installations that stay up throughout. This reduces waste and labor costs.
4. **Work with a skilled florist** – A professional can help you balance tradition and modern design, ensuring your **wedding flower wall backdrop** looks natural and stunning.
