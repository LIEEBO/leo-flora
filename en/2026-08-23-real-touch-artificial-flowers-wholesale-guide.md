## Real Touch Artificial Flowers Wholesale: What Brides & Planners Should Know

If you're planning a wedding or managing event decor, you've likely come across the term "Real Touch" artificial flowers. These aren't your grandmother's stiff silk blooms. They're crafted with soft, latex or PU coatings that mimic the feel of fresh petals, making them a favorite for **realistic artificial flowers** in close-up settings like bridal bouquets and table centerpieces.

For wedding planners and DIY brides, sourcing Real Touch flowers wholesale can be a game-changer—you get premium aesthetics at a fraction of the cost of fresh florals, with the flexibility to prepare everything weeks in advance. But how are they made, and what should you look for when buying in bulk? Let's dive into the material science and practical sourcing tips.

### How Real Touch Artificial Flowers Are Made

The secret behind Real Touch flowers lies in their construction. Unlike standard silk flowers, which use woven fabric, Real Touch blooms are created by coating or molding petals with soft-touch materials like:

- **Latex coating** – Gives a supple, petal-like feel, ideal for roses, tulips, and orchids.
- **PU coating** – Slightly firmer, offering more durability for stems that get handled often.
- **Foam or fabric core** – Provides structure and helps the flower recover its shape after being packed tightly in cartons.

These materials are applied over a fabric or foam base, then shaped and colored to match natural varieties. The result? Flowers that not only look lifelike but feel real to the touch—perfect for **wedding venue decoration ideas** where guests might brush against petals or hold bouquets.

### Why Choose Real Touch for Your Wedding Decor?

Real Touch artificial flowers are a smart choice for weddings because they offer the beauty of fresh blooms without the worry of wilting. Here's why they're becoming a staple in **silk flowers wedding decor**:

- **Durability** – They withstand heat, humidity, and handling, making them ideal for outdoor ceremonies or long receptions.
- **Reusability** – After the big day, you can repurpose them for bridal showers, anniversaries, or even sell them to recoup costs.
- **Consistency** – Every stem arrives looking perfect, with no variation in color or size—something fresh flowers can't guarantee.

For planners, this means less stress and more creative freedom. You can design elaborate installations weeks in advance, knowing your flowers will look exactly as envisioned.

### Practical Tips for Buying Real Touch Flowers Wholesale

If you're ready to source Real Touch flowers for your event, here are some insider tips to ensure a smooth purchase:

1. **Request samples first** – Before committing to a bulk order, order samples to check the material feel, color accuracy, and stem strength. Most suppliers offer samples within 15 days.
2. **Understand MOQs** – Typical minimum order quantities range from 500 to 576 pieces per style. If that's too high, ask if they offer trial orders via LCL (less than container load) shipping.
3. **Plan for lead times** – Bulk production usually takes 60–90 days after sample approval and deposit. Factor this into your wedding timeline.
4. **Ask about packing** – Quality packing prevents damage during sea freight. Ensure the supplier uses sturdy cartons and tissue wrap to protect petals and stems.
5. **Check the QC process** – Inquire about quality control for petal feel, coating consistency, and color fastness. A reputable supplier will have clear standards.
