When Carly and Thomas chose Chateau de Sannes for their Provence wedding, they knew the venue would be a showstopper. But it was their thoughtful floral design—a blend of deep bordeaux and lush green—that truly tied the celebration together. From a garden ceremony to a long family-style table, every detail felt intentional and inviting. If you're dreaming of a similar look, this real wedding offers plenty of inspiration. And the best part? You can recreate it on any budget with smart choices like affordable wedding flowers and bridal bouquet artificial flowers.

## Why Green and Bordeaux Florals Work So Well

The color palette of deep wine and verdant green is timeless yet unexpected. It evokes the richness of autumn, the romance of a vineyard, and the freshness of a garden—all at once. At Chateau de Sannes, this combination complemented the stone architecture and the surrounding olive groves. For your own wedding, consider using bordeaux dahlias, garden roses, and ranunculus, paired with eucalyptus, olive branches, and ferns. The contrast creates depth and texture, making your floral arrangements look custom and high-end.

## Recreating the Look with Artificial Flowers

If you love this aesthetic but worry about the cost of fresh blooms, you're not alone. Many couples are turning to artificial flowers for wedding decor. Today's high-quality silk and faux florals look incredibly realistic, and they offer several advantages: they don't wilt, you can prepare them weeks in advance, and they can be repurposed after the event. For a chateau-style wedding, opt for faux bordeaux roses and green hydrangeas. Mix them with real greenery for a hybrid approach that saves money without sacrificing style.

## Practical Tips for a Floral-Filled Wedding

1. **Set a budget and prioritize.** Decide which elements matter most—ceremony arch, centerpieces, bouquets—and allocate your funds accordingly. Using affordable wedding flowers for large installations can free up money for the bridal bouquet.
2. **Invest in the bridal bouquet.** It's the most photographed floral item, so consider a mix of real and artificial, or go all-in on a premium silk bouquet. Bridal bouquet artificial flowers are now so realistic that guests won't know the difference.
3. **Think about the flow.** Like Carly and Thomas, design your floral decor to guide guests from ceremony to reception. Use matching arrangements to create visual continuity.
4. **Mix high and low centerpieces.** For a long family-style table, alternate tall, dramatic arrangements with low, lush clusters. This keeps sightlines open and adds dimension.
5. **Plan for the weather.** If you're marrying outdoors, artificial flowers are a lifesaver—they won't droop in the heat or get ruined by a sudden drizzle.

## Making It Your Own

While the chateau was the star, the couple's personal touches made the day memorable. Don't be afraid to incorporate local elements—like olive branches or lavender—alongside your chosen palette. And remember, your floral choices should reflect your story, not just a trend. Whether you opt for fresh, faux, or a combination, the goal is to create an atmosphere that feels like you.

## Final Thoughts

A wedding at Chateau de Sannes may be a dream, but you can bring that same romantic, garden-inspired feel to any venue. With a thoughtful color palette and a mix of fresh and artificial flowers for wedding decor, you can achieve a luxurious look that fits your budget. So take inspiration from Carly and Thomas, and let your florals set the tone for a day you'll never forget.
