‘Dried lavender can be used across the entire home, and we often see it hung up in places such as the bathroom or on doors. ## How to Get the Look in Your Kitchen

Ready to bring this fairytale charm into your own home? Here are practical tips for styling dried lavender in your kitchen:

- **Hang from beams or rails**: Use twine or ribbon to tie small bunches of dried lavender and hang them from ceiling beams, pot racks, or a rail above your worktop. Space them evenly for a balanced look.
- **Mix with other dried herbs**: Combine lavender with rosemary, thyme, or sage for a more varied and aromatic display. This also adds different textures and colors.
- **Use as a centerpiece**: Place a small vase of dried lavender on your island or dining table as a simple, elegant centerpiece. It’s a low-maintenance alternative to fresh flowers.
- **Incorporate into open shelving**: Tuck small bunches of lavender between stacks of plates or jars on open shelves for a subtle, fragrant touch.
- **Refresh periodically**: While dried lavender lasts for months, it will eventually lose its scent and color. Replace it every few months to keep the look fresh.
