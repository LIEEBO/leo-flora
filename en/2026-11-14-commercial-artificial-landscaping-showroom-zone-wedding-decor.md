## Introduction

When planning a wedding, the visual impact of your décor is paramount. But have you ever noticed how artificial plants can look lackluster when simply grouped together without a cohesive design? In commercial spaces, the same principle applies. Oakco’s North America showroom zone is a prime example of how thoughtful landscaping—even with artificial plants—can create a breathtaking environment. By integrating layout, ground contours, and planting as one design, they achieved a seamless look. In this article, we’ll explore how you can apply these commercial landscaping secrets to your wedding décor, using realistic artificial flowers and wholesale silk flowers to elevate your big day.

## The Power of Purposeful Placement

In Oakco’s showroom, feature trees were positioned strategically to draw the eye and create focal points. For weddings, this translates to using tall artificial trees or large floral arrangements as backdrops for ceremonies or photo booths. The key is to vary heights and textures. Combine towering faux trees with lower arrangements of realistic artificial flowers to create depth. For example, a bridal bouquet artificial flowers display can be mirrored in table centerpieces, tying the theme together. Remember, it’s not about quantity but about thoughtful composition.

## Shaping Planting Areas for Flow

Oakco shaped planting areas to guide foot traffic and create natural boundaries. At your wedding, use artificial hedges or flower beds to define spaces—like separating the cocktail hour from the dining area. Ground contours were also considered; you can mimic this with varying levels of floral arrangements, such as elevated centerpieces or floor-standing arrangements. Use wholesale silk flowers to create lush, full-looking beds without the cost of fresh flowers. This approach ensures guests move smoothly through your venue while being surrounded by beauty.

## Mixing Textures and Heights

Matching smaller plants by height and leaf shape was crucial in Oakco’s design. For weddings, mix different types of artificial flowers and greenery. Combine realistic artificial flowers like roses and peonies with faux foliage like ferns and eucalyptus. Vary heights: use tall stems in floor vases, medium arrangements on tables, and low bowls for accent pieces. This layered look adds richness and realism. Don’t forget to incorporate bridal bouquet artificial flowers that complement the overall scheme—perhaps using the same blooms in the bouquet as in the table arrangements.
