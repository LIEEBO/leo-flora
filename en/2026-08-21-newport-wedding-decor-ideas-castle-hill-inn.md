# A Newport Wedding That Captured the Spirit of Summer at Castle Hill Inn

Imagine exchanging vows on a lush lawn overlooking the sparkling Narragansett Bay, with the salty breeze carrying the scent of garden roses. That’s exactly the magic this Rhode Island couple created at the historic Castle Hill Inn. Their wedding was a romantic, airy coastal garden party—classic and formal, yet softened with a dreamy, floral-forward design. For brides and planners seeking wedding venue decoration ideas that blend elegance with nature, this celebration is a treasure trove of inspiration.

## The Vision: Classic Elegance Meets Coastal Garden Charm

The couple’s vision was clear: a wedding that felt timeless, romantic, and deeply connected to its seaside setting. They chose a palette of white, ivory, green, and pale blue—colors that mirrored the ocean, the sky, and the estate’s manicured gardens. The design leaned into a floral-forward aesthetic, with blooms everywhere—from the ceremony arbor to the reception tables. Yet, the overall feel was airy and uncluttered, letting the natural beauty of the 40-acre estate shine.

For DIY brides, this is a masterclass in restraint. You don’t need to fill every corner with flowers. Instead, focus on focal points: the ceremony backdrop, the head table, and the cake. Use flowers to frame views, not block them. At Castle Hill, the ceremony overlooked the bay, so the floral decor was low and elegant, allowing guests to soak in the panorama.

## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas: From Ceremony to Reception

One of the most striking elements was the ceremony arch—a lush, organic installation of white and ivory blooms, accented with greenery and pale blue delphiniums. This is a perfect example of how a wedding flower wall backdrop can transform a space. Whether you’re marrying indoors or out, a flower wall creates a stunning focal point for your vows and doubles as a gorgeous photo backdrop.

For the reception, the couple carried the floral theme through with centerpieces of garden roses, hydrangeas, and ranunculus in low, mercury-glass vases. This allowed guests to converse easily while still feeling surrounded by beauty. They also added hanging floral installations above the head table, drawing the eye upward and adding dimension to the room.

Another clever idea: use potted olive trees and ferns to line the aisle and define the reception space. These green elements brought a fresh, garden-party vibe and could be repurposed as take-home favors for guests—a sustainable twist on decor.

## The Secret to Long-Lasting Wedding Florals: Realistic Artificial Flowers

While fresh flowers are undeniably beautiful, they can be fragile and expensive—especially for a multi-day celebration or a destination wedding. That’s why many planners are turning to realistic artificial flowers. These high-quality faux blooms look incredibly lifelike, but they won’t wilt in the sun, droop in humidity, or bruise during transport. For a coastal wedding like this one, where salt air and wind are factors, artificial flowers offer peace of mind.

You can use a wedding flower wall backdrop made of realistic artificial flowers to create that “wow” moment without worrying about petals falling or colors fading. And because they’re reusable, you can repurpose them for the bridal shower, rehearsal dinner, or even your home after the wedding. Many couples choose a mix: fresh flowers for the bridal bouquet and artificial for large installations—saving money without sacrificing style.

When shopping for faux florals, look for features like natural-looking stems, subtle color variations, and soft, realistic petals. High-end brands use materials like silk and latex to mimic the texture of real flowers. Pair them with fresh greenery to make the arrangement look even more authentic.

## Practical Tips for a Floral-Forward Wedding on Any Budget

1. **Invest in a statement piece.** If you can only splurge on one thing, make it the ceremony backdrop or a flower wall. It’s what guests will see first and photograph most.
2. **Mix fresh and artificial.** Use fresh flowers for small, hand-held items like bouquets and boutonnieres. Use realistic artificial flowers for arches, walls, and hanging installations.
3. **Choose seasonal blooms.** If you’re using fresh flowers, pick varieties that are in season to reduce costs. For artificial, you can have any flower year-round—even peonies in December!
4. **Repurpose ceremony decor.** Move the ceremony arch to behind the head table or the sweetheart table for the reception. It’s a great way to get more mileage from your decor.
5. **Consider the environment.** Wind, heat, and humidity can wreak havoc on fresh flowers. Artificial blooms are a reliable alternative for outdoor weddings.
6. **Rent or DIY?** For large installations, renting from a wedding decor company can be cost-effective. But if you’re crafty, DIY flower walls using artificial flowers can be a fun project—just start early and test your design.

## Conclusion

This Castle Hill Inn wedding is a beautiful reminder that the best weddings reflect the couple and their surroundings. By blending classic elegance with the natural beauty of the coast, they created a day that felt both timeless and personal. Whether you’re planning a seaside celebration or a garden party in the city, these wedding venue decoration ideas—from a lush flower wall backdrop to the strategic use of realistic artificial flowers—can help you craft a stunning, stress-free day.
