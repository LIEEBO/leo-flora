## Lighting and Atmosphere: The Midsummer Glow

The couple’s design included dramatic lighting to enhance the fairytale vibe. String lights, lanterns, and uplighting can turn a forest into an enchanted glade. Pair these with floral installations—like a flower-covered arch or a hanging floral chandelier—to create a cohesive look.

**Tip:** Use artificial flowers for hanging installations because they’re lightweight and won’t shed petals. Secure them with floral wire and fishing line for a floating effect.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

1. **Plan your color palette:** Midsummer Night’s Dream lends itself to deep blues, purples, greens, and pops of gold or pink. Choose faux flowers in these shades to create a cohesive scheme.
2. **Mix textures:** Combine smooth silk petals with textured faux greenery and berries for depth.
3. **Reuse and repurpose:** Artificial flowers can be moved from ceremony to reception, saving money and reducing waste.
4. **Test lighting:** Faux flowers look best under warm, diffused light. Avoid harsh spotlights that can make them appear plastic.
5. **Order samples:** Before committing, order sample stems to check quality and color accuracy.
