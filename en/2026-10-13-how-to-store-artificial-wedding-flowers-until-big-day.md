# Order Now, Marry Later: How to Store Artificial Wedding Flowers Until Your Big Day

One of the most underrated perks of choosing artificial flowers for your wedding is the gift of time. You can lock in your dream florals months—even a year—before your wedding and know they'll look just as stunning on the day as when they first arrived. No last-minute panic, no sold-out styles, no racing against the seasons. Order when the design you love is available, store it properly, and show up on your wedding day with that item completely handled.

Here's everything you need to know about ordering early and storing your silk flowers wedding decor so they stay flawless until you say "I do."

## Why Ordering Early Is Your Superpower

Fresh florists are brilliant at what they do, but they can't escape one limitation: timing. Fresh flowers must be ordered and arranged close to the date, forcing you to finalize your entire floral look during the most chaotic weeks of wedding planning.

With artificial flowers, time is on your side. Limited collections sell out, colorways get discontinued, and once a design is gone, it's gone. If you've fallen in love with a style, secure it the moment you find it—even if your wedding is six, nine, or twelve months away. The style you love may not wait. Your flowers absolutely will.

For couples planning a wedding flower wall backdrop or matching aisle arrangements, ordering early also means you can coordinate every piece from the same dye lot. That's a huge advantage when you want a seamless, cohesive look from ceremony to reception.

## How to Store Your Artificial Wedding Flowers Properly

Storage is easier than most people expect. The original packaging is your best friend—it's designed to protect shape, stems, and detailing during transit, and it will do the same job in storage. If you've already unpacked, a clean lidded box lined with tissue paper works perfectly.

- **Keep them in a cool, dry spot away from direct sunlight.** A wardrobe shelf, spare room, or linen cupboard is ideal. UV exposure fades colors over time, and prolonged heat can affect petal texture. Skip the garage, car boot, or anywhere near a window.
- **Don't stack heavy items on top.** Bouquets have lots of dimensional detail, and sustained weight will flatten the blooms. Give everything space and it will hold its shape beautifully.
- **Keep larger pieces upright where possible.** Heavier arrangements can sit on the base of the box, with smaller pieces beside or above them so nothing bears unnecessary weight.
- **Think of them like a nice piece of clothing.** If you'd store a delicate garment somewhere, that's the right spot for your florals too.

## A Five-Minute Refresh Before the Big Day

Even with perfect storage, a quick once-over before the wedding is always a good idea. Our go-to refresh routine:

- Give each piece a light shake to resettle any blooms that have compressed slightly.
- Use your fingers to gently fluff petals and reshape any bent stems.
- For larger installations like a wedding flower wall backdrop, steam gently with a handheld steamer on low heat to release wrinkles—hold it a few inches away and let the fabric relax.
- Check for any dust and wipe with a dry microfiber cloth.

This simple routine takes minutes and ensures your flowers look fresh and full when it matters most.

Not all artificial flowers are created equal. Cheaper options may fade, shed, or lose their shape over months of storage.
