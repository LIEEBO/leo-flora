When you envision a summer wedding by the sea, the image that comes to mind is often one of soft breezes, timeless elegance, and blooms that seem to dance with the tide. The Castle Hill Inn in Newport, Rhode Island, recently played host to a celebration that perfectly captured that spirit—a romantic, airy coastal garden-party wedding overlooking Narragansett Bay. If you're dreaming of a similar aesthetic but want to keep your decor both budget-friendly and long-lasting, this article is for you. We'll break down the key elements of that stunning Newport wedding and show you how to recreate the look with **faux floral arrangements** and **realistic artificial flowers** that will wow your guests and last beyond the big day.

## The Magic of a Coastal Garden-Party Theme

The original wedding at Castle Hill Inn was defined by its classic, formal yet floral-forward design. The color palette—white, ivory, green, and pale blue—mirrored the surrounding ocean and sky, creating a serene and airy atmosphere. To bring this look to life without the stress of wilting petals, start by selecting **realistic artificial flowers** in those same hues. Think creamy white roses, soft ivory peonies, and pale blue hydrangeas. Mix in lush green foliage like eucalyptus or ferns to add texture and depth. The key is to choose high-quality faux stems that mimic the natural movement and subtle color variations of fresh flowers. This foundation will allow you to build a cohesive, romantic look that feels both effortless and intentional.

## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas for an Estate Setting

A historic estate like Castle Hill Inn offers a spectacular backdrop, but you can adapt its charm to any venue with the right decor strategy. For a coastal garden-party vibe, focus on creating focal points that draw the eye. Consider a ceremony arch draped in **faux floral arrangements** of white and pale blue blooms, with greenery cascading down the sides. For the reception, use tall centerpieces with mixed-height vases filled with **realistic artificial flowers**—this adds drama without blocking guests' views. Don't forget the small touches: place small bud vases on cocktail tables, and line the aisle with lanterns and loose petals. The goal is to make the space feel lush and inviting, as if the garden itself has bloomed just for your celebration.

## Why Choose Faux Florals for Your Wedding?

Many DIY brides and wedding planners are turning to **faux floral arrangements** for good reason. They offer incredible flexibility: you can design and assemble your decor weeks in advance, avoid last-minute worries about flower availability or weather, and even repurpose the arrangements for your home or as gifts after the wedding. **Realistic artificial flowers** have come a long way—today's options are nearly indistinguishable from fresh, especially when viewed from a distance or in photos. For a coastal garden-party theme, they also hold up beautifully against wind or humidity, ensuring your decor looks perfect from ceremony to last dance. Plus, they're often more cost-effective, allowing you to allocate your budget to other priorities like photography or catering.

## Practical Tips for DIY Floral Decor

If you're planning to create your own **faux floral arrangements**, start with a clear vision. Sketch your centerpieces, bouquets, and ceremony decor before buying anything. Invest in a few high-quality statement flowers (like large roses or peonies) and fill in with more affordable greenery and filler blooms. Use floral foam or sturdy containers to secure stems, and consider adding a subtle scent with a few drops of essential oil on the petals for an authentic sensory experience. When arranging, work in odd numbers and vary heights for visual interest. Finally, test your designs in the actual venue lighting to ensure colors look natural. With patience and creativity, you can achieve a professional look that rivals any fresh-flower wedding.
