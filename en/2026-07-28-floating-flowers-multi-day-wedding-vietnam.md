Multi-day weddings, like Nhu and Long’s, require decor that stays fresh and vibrant from the first tea ceremony to the final dance. ### Practical Tips for Using Artificial Flowers in Your Wedding Decor

1. **Mix Real and Faux:** For a natural look, combine fresh flowers with high-quality artificial stems. Use faux for large installations (like arches or hanging pieces) and real for small bouquets or boutonnieres.

2. **Choose High-Quality Silks:** Not all artificial flowers are created equal. Look for realistic petals, natural color variations, and flexible stems. Brands like Afloral or Ling’s Moment offer excellent options for DIY brides.

3. **Plan for Reuse:** After the wedding, repurpose your **fake flowers centerpiece** arrangements as home decor or donate them to a local community center. This adds value and reduces waste.

4. **Lighting Matters:** Faux florals can look flat under harsh lighting. Use warm string lights, uplighting, or candles to add depth and a romantic glow to your installations.
