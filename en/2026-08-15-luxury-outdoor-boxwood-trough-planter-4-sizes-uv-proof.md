## Practical Tips for Using Boxwood Troughs in Event Decor

Here are some insider tips to get the most out of your boxwood trough planters:

- **Create a grand entrance:** Line the walkway with large troughs filled with faux greenery and soft white blooms. It sets the tone for an elegant affair.
- **Frame the ceremony arch:** Place two matching troughs at the base of your arch. Add a few trailing vines for a romantic, garden-inspired look.
- **Use as table runners:** For long banquet tables, place smaller troughs down the center and tuck in candles or fairy lights. It adds height and depth without blocking conversation.
- **Mix textures:** Combine the boxwood with other faux floral arrangements, like lavender sprigs or eucalyptus, to create a layered, organic feel.
- **Plan for all weather:** Even if rain is in the forecast, your boxwood will stay picture-perfect. No browning, no drooping, just consistent beauty.
