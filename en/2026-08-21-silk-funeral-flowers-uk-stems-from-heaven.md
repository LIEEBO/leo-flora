## Why Choose Silk Flower Bouquets for Funerals?

The decision between fresh and artificial flowers isn't just about aesthetics — it's about practicality and lasting meaning. In the UK, where rain and wind can strike at any moment, fresh flowers often fail to hold up. A bouquet that looks stunning at the church door may wilt by the time guests reach the reception venue.

### The Hidden Costs of Fresh Funeral Flowers

Fresh flowers are fragile. They require constant hydration, protection from the elements, and replacement every few days if the memorial is to be maintained. For families grieving a loss, the logistics of refreshing flowers can add unnecessary stress. Worse, a wilted arrangement can feel like a fading tribute — the opposite of what families intend.

### The Lasting Advantages of Silk

Silk funeral flowers offer permanence. They don't droop, fade, or require watering. A high-quality silk bouquet retains its shape and colour for years, allowing families to keep the arrangement as a keepsake long after the ceremony. This emotional value is immeasurable — a lasting connection to the loved one that fresh flowers simply cannot provide.

Whether you're selecting flowers for a funeral or planning your own wedding decor, these tips will help you get the most from your silk arrangements.

### 1. Prioritise Realism Over Price

Look for flowers with real-touch petals and natural colour gradients. Cheaper options often look plastic and shiny. Stems From Heaven's range uses matte finishes and subtle veining that mimic nature.

### 2. Consider the Venue Setting

For outdoor ceremonies, silk flowers are a no-brainer. They won't wilt in the sun or droop in the rain. Indoors, they provide consistent beauty without the need for refrigeration or constant misting.

### 3. Mix Fresh and Silk Strategically

Some families choose to combine a few fresh blooms with silk stems for a hybrid look. This balances the scent of fresh flowers with the longevity of silk. Just be aware that fresh elements will need replacing.
