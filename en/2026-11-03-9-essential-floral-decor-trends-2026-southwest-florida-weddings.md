Southwest Florida’s waterfront views provide the perfect backdrop for a coastal-inspired tablescape. ## 2. Holiday Glam with Metallics

Holiday parties in Naples and Fort Myers call for sparkle! Metallic gold or silver chargers, mirrored table runners, and crystal glassware instantly elevate your holiday tablescape. One of the best ways to make your tablescape stand out is to embrace the season. For winter holiday parties, deep reds and evergreen accents make a bold statement. Using **realistic artificial flowers** in seasonal hues allows you to achieve vibrant colors without the cost of out-of-season blooms. Create a **fake flowers centerpiece** with tropical flowers like hibiscus and bird of paradise for summer events.
