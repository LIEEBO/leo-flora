## Why Floral Foam Matters for Your Wedding Flowers

Floral foam, also known as OASIS foam, is a staple in floral design. It acts like a soft pin cushion, holding each stem securely while providing moisture to fresh blooms. For artificial flowers, dry foam offers the perfect base to anchor stems without the need for water. If you're working with a mix of fresh and fake flowers centerpiece designs, understanding foam types is key:

- **Wet Floral Foam:** Best for fresh flowers like roses, lilies, and carnations. It keeps stems hydrated and vibrant.
- **Dry Floral Foam:** Ideal for artificial or silk flowers where no water is needed.
- **Heavy-Duty Foam:** Perfect for large or dense arrangements that need extra support.

## Step-by-Step: Cutting Stems for Floral Foam

### 1. Choose the Right Tools

Sharp tools are essential for clean cuts that allow stems to absorb water efficiently. Keep these on hand:

- **Floral Shears:** Best for soft, tender stems.
- **Pruning Knife:** Perfect for woody or thicker stems.
- **Cutting Mat:** Protects your workspace.

### 2. Prepare the Foam

Before cutting stems, soak your foam properly:

- Fill a clean bucket with cool water.
- Gently place the foam on the water's surface—do not push it down.
- Let it soak for 15–30 minutes until it's heavy and dark green.

This ensures even hydration, crucial for fresh arrangements.

### 3. Cut Stems at an Angle

Cut stems at a 45-degree angle to maximize water absorption. This simple trick exposes more surface area, helping flowers drink up water and stay fresh longer. For artificial flowers, a straight cut is fine, but angling can help with stability in foam.

### 4. Remove Lower Leaves

Strip any leaves that would sit below the foam line. Submerged leaves can rot and breed bacteria, shortening the life of your arrangement.

### 5. Insert Stems Gently

Trim stems so they sit snugly in the foam without wobbling. Insert them gently—forcing can damage the foam or snap the stem. For artificial flowers, you may need to use a bit more pressure, but still be careful.

## Arranging Your Flowers for Maximum Impact

Now comes the fun part—designing your display. Here are some tips:

- **Start with a focal point:** Place your largest or most striking bloom first, then build around it.
- **Angle stems:** Insert at slight angles for a natural, flowing look.
- **Leave gaps:** Allow small spaces for air and water circulation.
- **Experiment with styles:** Try tall and vertical for drama, round clusters for balance, or mixed spreads for an organic feel.

## Practical Tips for Long-Lasting Arrangements

- **Keep foam moist:** Check water levels daily and add more as needed.
- **Avoid direct sunlight:** Place arrangements in cool spots to prevent wilting.
- **Refresh stems:** If flowers start to droop, re-cut stems and reinsert.
- **Use floral preservative:** A little packet can go a long way for fresh flowers.
- **Mix real and faux:** Combine fresh greenery with artificial blooms for a full look that lasts.
