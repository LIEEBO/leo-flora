## Introduction

Planning a wedding involves countless decisions, and one of the most impactful is the table décor. Candlesticks are a classic choice that adds elegance and warmth to any reception. But when you're sourcing wholesale wedding candlesticks, the options can be overwhelming. With about 100 products in a typical wholesale category—five-arm candelabras, crystal holders, glass hexagonal lanterns, brass-style lanterns, and even a peacock holder—how do you choose? The key is to build a mixed order that balances style, budget, and practicality. In this guide, we'll walk you through the process, with tips on incorporating fake flowers centerpieces and affordable wedding flowers to complete the look.

## Why a Mixed Order Makes Sense

A mixed order of candlesticks allows you to create visual interest across your venue without committing to a single style. It also gives you flexibility: you can use different heights and designs for different tables, or mix and match to suit various themes. For DIY brides and wedding planners, this approach is both cost-effective and creative. By combining candlesticks with artificial flowers, you can achieve a cohesive yet dynamic wedding venue decoration idea that impresses guests.

## Choosing the Right Candlesticks for Your Venue

When selecting candlesticks, consider the overall ambiance you want to create. For a romantic, traditional wedding, five-arm candelabras and crystal holders are ideal. For a modern or rustic theme, glass hexagonal lanterns and brass-style lanterns work beautifully. Don't be afraid to include a statement piece like a peacock holder for the head table. The goal is to create a harmonious mix that reflects your personal style. Remember, these candlesticks will serve as the foundation for your centerpieces, so choose designs that complement your floral arrangements.

## Pairing Candlesticks with Fake Flowers Centerpieces

Candlesticks alone can look bare, but adding a fake flowers centerpiece instantly elevates the table. Artificial flowers offer a huge advantage: they're long-lasting, require no water, and can be reused for other events. For a cohesive look, wrap the base of the candlestick with a garland of faux greenery or place a small arrangement of silk flowers around it. Consider using flowers that match your wedding colors—roses, peonies, or hydrangeas in soft pastels or bold hues. The combination of flickering candlelight and lifelike blooms creates a magical atmosphere.

## Practical Tips for Placing Your Order

Before you finalize your mixed order, create a floor plan and count how many tables you have. Decide on a ratio of tall candlesticks to short ones—typically, taller pieces go on larger tables or in the center, while shorter ones work well on smaller tables or as accents. Don't forget to order extra candles and replacement parts. If you're working with a supplier, ask for samples to check quality. Finally, consider the season and venue: outdoor weddings may require sturdier, wind-resistant designs.
