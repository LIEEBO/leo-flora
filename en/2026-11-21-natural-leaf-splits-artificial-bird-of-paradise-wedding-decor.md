## Why Real Bird of Paradise Leaves Split

Strelitzia nicolai is a striking tropical plant with large, paddle-shaped leaves. In their natural habitat, these leaves are exposed to wind and weather, causing them to develop splits along the veins. This is a normal part of growth, not a sign of disease. As the leaf expands, the softer tissue between veins tears, allowing wind to pass through and preventing the entire leaf from ripping. Mature leaves typically show more splits than younger ones, giving the plant a rugged, authentic appearance.
