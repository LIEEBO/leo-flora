## Textural Florals: The Secret to Contemporary Garden Style

What makes this design stand out is its focus on texture. Instead of uniform blooms, mix flower shapes and sizes: round peonies, spiky snapdragons, airy astilbe, and trailing ivy. The contrast between soft petals and rough foliage creates visual interest that feels curated, not chaotic. For a **fake flowers centerpiece**, choose high-quality silk or latex blooms that mimic real textures. Pair them with dried grasses or preserved moss for an organic, garden-fresh look.

Pro tip: Use floral foam or a chicken wire base to anchor your arrangements. Insert taller stems like delphiniums or faux lavender first, then fill in with smaller flowers and greenery. This layering technique gives your centerpieces that professional, lush appearance. For the ceremony arch, drape garlands of mixed foliage and hang single blooms at varying heights—perfect for photos.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

Recreating this style on a budget? Here's how to make it work:

1. **Choose artificial flowers wisely**: Invest in high-quality stems for focal flowers (like roses and peonies). Use budget-friendly greenery for filler. Mix real dried elements (like lavender or eucalyptus) with faux for authenticity.
2. **Plan in layers**: Start with large structural pieces (arch, backdrop), then move to centerpieces, and finish with small accents (place cards, cake topper). This prevents overwhelm.
3. **Test your arrangements**: Mock up one centerpiece a month before the wedding. Adjust colors, heights, and textures. Take photos in different lighting to ensure it matches your vision.
4. **Rent or repurpose**: Consider renting **artificial flowers for wedding** from a decor company. Or repurpose ceremony florals for the reception—simply move arrangements to the head table or sweetheart table.
5. **Embrace asymmetry**: Nature isn't perfectly symmetrical. Let your arrangements feel loose and organic. Use odd numbers of stems and vary flower heights for that effortless garden vibe.
