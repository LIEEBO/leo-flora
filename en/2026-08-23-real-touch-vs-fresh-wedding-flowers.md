Planning your wedding flowers is one of the most exciting—and stressful—parts of the big day. If you're getting married in Kelowna or the Okanagan, you've probably noticed that floral costs can vary dramatically. The choice between traditional fresh blooms and modern Real Touch florals is a big one. Both can be stunning, but they offer very different experiences. Let's break down what you need to know to make the best decision for your wedding, your budget, and your peace of mind.

## What Are Real Touch Wedding Flowers?

Real Touch flowers are high-quality faux florals designed to look and feel incredibly realistic. Unlike traditional silk flowers, modern Real Touch florals have natural texture and movement, photograph beautifully, and mimic real blooms almost perfectly. Many brides are surprised they can't tell the difference—especially in photos. These flowers are a game-changer for couples who want the perfect look without the fragility of fresh petals.

## Fresh Wedding Flowers: The Classic Choice

Fresh flowers have always been the traditional option for weddings. They offer natural fragrance, organic one-of-a-kind arrangements, and seasonal beauty. However, they come with limitations—especially in a place like the Okanagan, where heat, weather, and sourcing can affect quality and pricing. Fresh flowers are also sensitive to the elements; they can wilt in the sun, droop in humidity, and need to be prepared within days of your wedding. For many, the classic look is worth it, but it's important to weigh the pros and cons.

## The Cost Difference: Real Touch vs Fresh

One of the biggest deciding factors for brides is cost. In Canada, wedding flower budgets can vary widely. Many couples spend $1,000–$2,000 on the lower end, while more elevated weddings often reach $2,500–$7,000 or more. Luxury floral designs can climb significantly higher depending on scale. Fresh floral arches alone can cost $1,000–$3,000+, centrepieces $100–$300 each, and bouquets $250–$400. That adds up fast.

With Real Touch floral rentals, you can achieve a similar elevated look with more control over your budget. Typical pricing includes floral pillars at $700–$900, centrepieces at $18–$65, and bouquets starting at $125. This allows many couples to create a full, impactful design while saving thousands—without compromising on the overall look and feel. If you're looking for affordable wedding flowers, Real Touch is a smart choice.

## Real Touch vs Fresh: What's the Difference?

### Fresh Flowers: Best For
- Traditional weddings
- Couples who love natural fragrance
- Highly seasonal designs

Considerations: Sensitive to heat and weather, must be prepared within days of the wedding, and are typically single-use (not reusable).

### Real Touch Flowers: Best For
- Luxury designs on a realistic budget
- Outdoor weddings (heat-safe)
- Brides who want consistency and peace of mind

Benefits: No wilting, no weather stress, you know exactly what your flowers will look like ahead of time, and they're reusable and more sustainable. They're ideal for large installations like floral pillars, arches, and aisle flowers. Plus, they make a fantastic wedding flower wall backdrop—a trend that's here to stay.

## Practical Tips for Choosing Your Wedding Flowers

1. **Think about your venue and season.** Outdoor weddings in the Okanagan can be hot—fresh flowers may not survive the ceremony. Real Touch flowers are heat-safe and will look perfect from morning to night.
2. **Set a budget and stick to it.** Decide how much you're willing to spend on florals overall. Real Touch rentals can help you stretch your budget further, leaving room for other wedding venue decoration ideas.
3. **Ask about rental options.** Many florists offer Real Touch rentals, which can be more affordable than buying fresh. You can often customize colours and styles to match your theme.
4. **Consider your photos.** Both fresh and Real Touch flowers photograph beautifully, but Real Touch flowers give you the peace of mind that every shot will look flawless—no wilting petals or drooping stems.
5. **Mix and match.** You don't have to choose one or the other. Many couples use fresh flowers for the bridal party and Real Touch for large installations like arches, pillars, and backdrops. This can save money while still giving you that fresh flower scent where it matters most.

## Conclusion: Which Should You Choose?

There's no right or wrong answer—it all depends on your priorities. If you love the natural fragrance and organic feel of fresh flowers and have the budget to accommodate them, go for it. But if you want affordable wedding flowers, a stress-free planning process, and the ability to create stunning wedding venue decoration ideas like a wedding flower wall backdrop, Real Touch flowers are a fantastic option. They offer the beauty of fresh flowers without the worry, and they can save you thousands of dollars. Talk to your florist about Real Touch rentals and see how they can transform your wedding day.
