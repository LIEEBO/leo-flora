# Wholesale Wedding Lights: What to Confirm Before Ordering

When you're planning a wedding, every detail matters—from the flowers to the lighting. While most wedding furniture is judged by how it looks, wedding lights are judged twice: once by how they look, and once by whether they actually switch on at 6 p.m. in a ballroom three time zones away from the factory. That second test is where wholesale buyers get caught off guard. If you're sourcing wedding lights for your big day or for your clients, here's what you need to confirm before placing that order.

## Why Wedding Lights Deserve Extra Scrutiny

Unlike a decorative arch or a table centerpiece, lighting has a functional job. It sets the mood, illuminates the dance floor, and highlights your faux floral arrangements. But if the lights fail, the entire ambiance falls flat. For DIY brides and wedding planners, ordering wholesale lights can save money—but only if you ask the right questions upfront. From voltage compatibility to plug types, the details you confirm today will determine whether your lighting works flawlessly on the wedding day.

## Key Questions to Ask Your Wholesale Supplier

Before you commit to a bulk order, run through this checklist with your supplier:

- **Voltage and plug type:** Will the lights work in your country? Confirm voltage (110V vs. 220V) and plug shape. Adapters can help, but they add clutter and cost.
- **Certification and safety:** Look for CE, RoHS, or UL certifications. This is non-negotiable for indoor venues.
- **Battery life and power source:** Are they battery-operated, USB, or plug-in? Battery life matters for reception longevity.
- **Durability and weather resistance:** If you're using them outdoors, ensure they're waterproof (IP rating).
- **Warranty and return policy:** What happens if a strand fails? A solid warranty protects your investment.
- **Minimum order quantity (MOQ):** Can you mix and match styles? Flexible MOQs help you stay within budget.
- **Lead times and shipping:** Confirm production and shipping timelines. You don't want lights arriving after the wedding.

## Pairing Lights with Affordable Wedding Flowers and Faux Florals

Lighting and flowers go hand in hand. A warm glow can make affordable wedding flowers look luxurious, while a harsh light can wash out even the most expensive blooms. When you're designing your décor, consider how your lights will interact with your floral choices.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

Here are some actionable tips to make your wholesale lighting purchase smoother:

1. **Order samples first.** Always test a sample strand before committing to a large order. Check brightness, color temperature, and build quality.
2. **Calculate your needs.** Measure your venue and determine how many strands or fixtures you'll need. Add 10% extra for backups.
3. **Test in similar conditions.** If possible, test the lights in a dim room to see how they'll look on the wedding day.
4. **Plan for power.** Map out where outlets are and bring extension cords and power strips. Battery-operated lights offer flexibility but check battery life.
5. **Mix light types.** Combine fairy lights, string lights, and uplights to create depth. Use them to highlight your faux floral arrangements and centerpieces.
6. **Store safely.** After the event, pack lights carefully to avoid tangles and damage for future use.
