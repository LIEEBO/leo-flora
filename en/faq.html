Frequently Asked Questions

Everything importers, event planners, and cross-border sellers need to know about MOQ, OEM customization, samples, and shipping from Yiwu.

1. What types of artificial flowers and plants does Leo Faux manufacture?
We offer 3,300+ wholesale SKUs covering Real-Touch Roses, Velvet Roses & Peonies, Austin Roses, Phalaenopsis & Thai Orchids, Hydrangeas, Lilies, Calla Lilies, Sunflowers, Daisies, Wisteria Vines, Eucalyptus, Pampas Reeds, Artificial Trees, Potted Plants, Flower Walls, Green Plant Walls, Synthetic Turf, Christmas Poinsettias, and Memorial/Cemetery Flowers.
2. What is your MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) for wholesale orders?
We support flexible MOQs tailored to importers, wedding planners, and cross-border e-commerce sellers. Standard in-stock styles can be mixed by carton or inner box. Contact us with your selected SKU list from our online catalog for exact carton packing details.
3. Do you provide OEM / ODM customization for colors, sizes, and packaging?
Yes. We provide full OEM/ODM services including custom Pantone petal dyeing, custom bouquet assembly, private-label tags, barcodes, and export retail boxes tailored to Amazon, Shopify, or local retail chains.
4. What materials do you use for realistic artificial flowers?
Depending on the product line, we use premium Real-Touch PU/latex coating, luxury velvet fabric, high-density silk/polyester cloth, and UV-resistant PE for outdoor greenery, plant walls, and cemetery arrangements.

6. What is the standard production lead time?
In-stock wholesale items can be prepared within 3–7 days. Bulk production orders typically take 10–25 days depending on season and order volume. Custom-dyed OEM orders are confirmed with a clear production schedule prior to deposit.
7. How do you handle international shipping and Yiwu forwarding agents?
We deliver directly to your designated Yiwu shipping agent/warehouse, or arrange international air freight, sea container shipping (FCL/LCL), and express courier (DHL/FedEx/UPS) worldwide with complete export documentation.

Yes, samples are available so you can inspect petal realism, real-touch feel, and color accuracy before confirming bulk orders. Sample fees and courier costs apply and can often be credited toward bulk orders.
