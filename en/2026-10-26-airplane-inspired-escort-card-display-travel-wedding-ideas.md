## Why an Airplane Escort Card Display Works

Escort cards do more than tell guests where to sit—they set the tone for your reception. An airplane-inspired display instantly communicates your love for travel and gives guests a conversation starter. Whether you use a vintage map backdrop, a departures board, or paper airplanes, the concept is flexible and photogenic. Plus, it's a natural fit for destination weddings or couples who bonded over a love of exploring.

The best part? You can adapt the idea to any budget. A simple foam board painted like a world map, adorned with **faux floral arrangements**, creates a stunning focal point without the cost of fresh flowers that wilt under venue lights.

## How to Create the Look with Silk Flowers and Faux Florals

Fresh flowers are beautiful, but they can be unpredictable—especially for outdoor or destination weddings. That's where **faux floral arrangements** shine. High-quality silk flowers mimic the look and feel of real blooms, but they hold up in heat, travel well, and last long after the big day.

For an airplane-themed display, consider these floral touches:

- **Garland accents:** Drape a soft garland of silk eucalyptus and roses along the top of your escort wall to soften the edges and add romance.
- **Mini arrangements:** Place small clusters of faux dahlias and garden roses at the base of the display or on a nearby welcome table.
- **Paper airplane florals:** Attach tiny silk flower buds to paper airplanes for a whimsical touch that ties the theme together.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

1. **Plan your layout first.** Sketch your escort wall design before buying supplies. Decide where flowers will go and how many stems you need. A mix of larger focal blooms (like dahlias) and filler greens creates balance.
2. **Choose the right base.** A foam board covered in fabric or paper works well for the backdrop. For a more dimensional look, use a wooden frame or a vintage window pane.
3. **Mix real and faux.** Use faux flowers for the escort display and fresh blooms for centerpieces if your budget allows. This keeps costs down while ensuring the display looks lush all night.
4. **Test your lighting.** Silk flowers can look different under venue lighting. Take a few stems to the venue or test them under warm and cool bulbs to ensure the colors read well.
5. **Personalize with travel details.** Use luggage tags as escort cards, stamp them with each guest's name, and pin them to the display. Add a small globe or vintage suitcase for extra charm.
6. **Secure your arrangements.** Use floral wire or zip ties to attach garlands and clusters to your backdrop. For table arrangements, use floral foam or weighted vases to prevent tipping.
