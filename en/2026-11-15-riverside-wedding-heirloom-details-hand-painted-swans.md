# Riverside Wedding with Heirloom Details & Hand-Painted Swans

When a bride grows up summering on Anderson Creek, her family's river home becomes the ultimate wedding venue. This real wedding, planned by Anna Wynn Event Designs, combined Nantucket charm with Alabama warmth for a day full of fireworks, swans, and a sailcloth tent. While the original celebration featured high-end details, you can recreate the look with affordable wedding flowers and smart DIY choices.

## The Charm of a Riverside Wedding

A riverfront setting offers natural beauty, but it also presents unique challenges: wind, moisture, and a relaxed vibe that calls for durable decor. Fresh flowers can wilt quickly in humid conditions, which is why many couples opt for wholesale silk flowers. These artificial blooms hold up beautifully outdoors and can be arranged hours before the ceremony without wilting.

For this wedding, the couple embraced the river theme with swan motifs and hand-painted details. You can achieve a similar look by incorporating swan figurines, painted signs, and lush floral arrangements that feel organic yet long-lasting.

## Heirloom Details That Tell Your Story

Heirloom touches add personality and meaning. Consider using family silver, vintage linens, or passed-down vases as vessels for your blooms. For a cohesive look, pair heirloom pieces with fake flowers centerpiece arrangements that mimic the look of fresh flowers but last beyond the big day.

## Creating a Sailcloth Tent Reception

A sailcloth tent provides an elegant, airy feel. To decorate, hang greenery from the poles, use string lights, and create a focal point with a floral chandelier. For tables, mix high and low centerpieces: tall branches with hanging orchids and low bowls filled with floating candles and blooms.

Using a fake flowers centerpiece allows you to prep ahead and avoid last-minute stress. You can arrange them days in advance and they'll still look fresh. Plus, they're budget-friendly—especially when you buy wholesale silk flowers in bulk.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides

- **Plan for wind:** Secure lightweight decor with weights or floral foam.
- **Mix real and faux:** Use real greenery for scent and faux blooms for durability.
- **DIY ahead:** Assemble centerpieces a week before and store them safely.

- **Personalize:** Add hand-painted elements like swan place cards or signage.
