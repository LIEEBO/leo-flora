## Creating Show-Stopping Fake Flowers Centerpieces

A fake flowers centerpiece can be just as breathtaking as a fresh one—if done right. Here are some ideas for using the 20" Open Rose Stems:

### 1. Classic Rose Centerpiece

Gather a cluster of 6–8 open rose stems in complementary colors (think cream and blush, or red and white) and arrange them in a low, wide vase. Add some eucalyptus or greenery to soften the look. The result is a romantic, garden-style centerpiece that guests will admire.

### 2. Mixed Floral Arrangement

Combine the open roses with other artificial flowers like cherry blossoms or hydrangeas for a textured, eclectic look. Vary the heights to add dimension, and use a metallic or mercury glass vase for a touch of elegance.

### 3. Rustic Mason Jar Display

For a rustic or barn wedding, place a single open rose stem in a mason jar tied with twine. Group several jars of different heights on each table for a charming, country-chic vibe.

**Pro Tip:** To make your centerpieces look more realistic, bend the stems slightly and fluff the petals. You can also spritz them with a light floral scent to mimic fresh flowers.

## Building a Wedding Flower Wall Backdrop

A wedding flower wall backdrop is a show-stopping element that doubles as a photo booth. Creating one with artificial flowers is easier and more affordable than you might think.

### How to Create a Flower Wall

1. **Choose a base:** Use a foam board, wooden pallet, or fabric-covered frame as your backdrop.
2. **Attach a mesh:** Cover the base with chicken wire or floral foam netting to hold the stems.
3. **Arrange your flowers:** Start with the largest blooms (like the 20" open roses) and fill in with smaller flowers and greenery. Use a mix of colors to match your wedding palette.
4. **Secure and display:** Use zip ties or floral wire to secure the stems. Place the wall behind the head table or as a photo backdrop.

**Pro Tip:** For a lush, cascading effect, let some roses trail down the sides. You can also incorporate fairy lights for a magical evening ambiance.

## Practical Tips for Using Artificial Flowers

- **Mix with real greenery:** Combine artificial roses with fresh eucalyptus or ferns for a more natural look.
- **Color coordination:** Order a sample pack first to ensure colors match your wedding palette.
- **Storage:** Store your flowers in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to prevent fading.
- **Cleaning:** Dust regularly with a soft cloth or use a hairdryer on cool setting to remove debris.
- **Transport:** Pack arrangements carefully in boxes with tissue paper to avoid crushing.
