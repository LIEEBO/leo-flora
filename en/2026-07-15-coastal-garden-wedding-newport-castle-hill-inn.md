When you think of a Newport summer wedding, what comes to mind? For many, it's the gentle breeze off Narragansett Bay, the scent of salt air, and the timeless elegance of a coastal garden party. The Castle Hill Inn, a historic 40-acre estate overlooking the water, provides the perfect backdrop for such a dreamy celebration. In this article, we'll break down the design elements of this romantic, airy wedding and show you how to recreate the look using wholesale silk flowers and other faux floral solutions that are practical for DIY brides and wedding planners alike.

## The Color Palette: White, Ivory, Green, and Pale Blue

The color scheme of this wedding is a study in understated elegance. White and ivory blooms create a crisp, clean foundation, while lush green foliage adds depth and texture. Pale blue accents—whether in the bridesmaids' dresses, table linens, or ribbon details—echo the sky and sea of Newport. This palette is not only timeless but also incredibly versatile for any coastal or garden-themed event.

For DIY brides, achieving this look with fresh flowers can be expensive and time-consuming. Instead, consider using realistic artificial flowers in shades of white and ivory. Pair them with high-quality greenery like eucalyptus or ferns, and add pale blue ribbon or hydrangea stems. You can source these from a supplier of wholesale silk flowers to keep costs low while maintaining a luxurious appearance.

## The Ceremony: Overlooking Narragansett Bay

The ceremony at Castle Hill Inn was set with a stunning view of the bay. The altar was framed by an abundance of white blooms and cascading greenery, creating a natural, airy archway. This focal point drew the eye toward the water, making the landscape part of the decor.

To recreate this look for your own wedding, consider building a ceremony arch using a combination of fresh and faux elements. A bridal bouquet artificial flowers arrangement in white and ivory can be used as a centerpiece for the arch, surrounded by realistic artificial flowers that mimic garden roses, peonies, and hydrangeas. Because faux flowers don't wilt, you can assemble the arch days in advance, reducing stress on the big day.

## The Reception: Garden-Party Elegance

The reception continued the garden-party theme with long tables adorned with low, lush centerpieces. White linens, delicate china, and pale blue glassware complemented the floral arrangements. The overall vibe was formal yet relaxed, inviting guests to enjoy the summer evening.

For a DIY-friendly approach, use wholesale silk flowers to create multiple centerpieces that match the ceremony palette. Mix in dried elements like pampas grass or preserved baby's breath for added texture. Since realistic artificial flowers look just like the real thing, you can achieve a high-end look without the worry of wilting or last-minute replacements. This is especially helpful for destination weddings or events where fresh flowers are hard to source.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

- **Plan Ahead:** Start sourcing your faux flowers at least two months before the wedding. Order samples to ensure colors and textures match your vision.
- **Mix Fresh and Faux:** Use fresh greenery for a natural scent and realistic artificial flowers for the main blooms. This saves money and extends the life of your decor.
- **Focus on Key Pieces:** Invest in a high-quality bridal bouquet artificial flowers arrangement, as it will be in many photos. For table centerpieces, you can use less expensive stems.
- **Storage and Transport:** Faux flowers are lightweight and easy to pack. Store them in airtight containers to keep dust-free, and assemble arrangements on-site if needed.
- **Budget-Friendly Sourcing:** Look for wholesalers that offer bulk discounts on wholesale silk flowers. Many online retailers specialize in wedding-grade faux florals.

A Newport summer wedding at Castle Hill Inn is the epitome of coastal romance.
