When planning a wedding, every detail matters—from the bridal bouquet artificial flowers to the table centerpieces. But fresh greenery can be expensive, high-maintenance, and short-lived. That's why real feel garlands and wreaths are becoming a go-to choice for DIY brides and wedding planners alike. These lifelike decorations offer the beauty of fresh foliage without the stress. Let's explore how you can incorporate them into your big day.

## Why Real Feel Garlands and Wreaths Are Wedding Must-Haves

Real feel garlands and wreaths—also known as real touch, PE, or realistic faux—are crafted to mimic the texture and appearance of fresh greenery. They've taken the wedding industry by storm, and for good reason.

### Lifelike Appearance

Guests won't believe these aren't fresh. The soft, natural-looking needles and leaves create a warm, inviting atmosphere that photographs beautifully. Whether draped over an arch or lining the aisle, they add elegance without the wilt-and-droop worry.

### Durability That Lasts

Unlike fresh greenery, these pieces won't dry out or turn brown. You can set them up days in advance—perfect for early venue decorating. They'll look just as stunning at the end of the night as they did during the ceremony.

### Allergy-Friendly and Low Maintenance

For couples or guests with pollen sensitivities, real feel options are a blessing. No sneezing, no watering, no pruning. Just pure beauty.

## Creative Ways to Use Garlands and Wreaths in Your Wedding Decor

### 1. Ceremony Backdrops and Arches

Drape a 10- or 15-foot real feel garland across a wooden arch or along a ceremony backdrop. Mix pine and cedar for depth. Add string lights for a magical evening vibe. The result? A stunning focal point that requires zero upkeep.

### 2. Reception Table Runners

Instead of traditional runners, lay garlands down the center of your reception tables. Weave in candles, lanterns, or even fake flowers centerpiece arrangements. This creates a lush, romantic tablescape that's both budget-friendly and reusable.

### 3. Wreaths as Wall Art or Chair Accents

Hang 24- to 36-inch wreaths on the venue's walls, or use smaller ones to decorate chair backs at the head table. They add a cohesive, natural touch that ties your theme together.

### 4. Bridal Bouquets and Boutonnieres

While garlands and wreaths are the stars, don't forget about bridal bouquet artificial flowers. Many real feel collections include stems and picks that match perfectly. You can craft a bouquet that looks fresh but won't droop during photos or the reception.

## Practical Tips for Shopping and Styling

- **Length and Size:** Measure your venue spaces first. Garlands come in 5 to 15 feet; wreaths in 24 to 36 inches. Choose based on where you'll place them.
- **Lighting:** Lit options add a warm glow—perfect for evening events. Unlit versions are more versatile for daytime or if you plan to add your own fairy lights.
- **Mix and Match:** Combine pine and cedar varieties for a more natural, layered look. Don't be afraid to mix with real flowers or other artificial flowers for wedding decor.
- **Storage and Reuse:** After the wedding, these pieces can be cleaned and stored for future events, or even repurposed as home decor. That's a win for your budget.
- **Test Before You Buy:** If possible, order a sample to check the feel and color in person. Photos can be deceiving.
