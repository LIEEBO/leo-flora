When a bride books a flower wall for her wedding, what do you do next? If you simply confirm the order and move on, you're leaving money on the table. ## Why Add-Ons Are the Secret to Rental Profitability

Acquiring a new customer costs five times more than selling to an existing one. Increasing your average order value by just 20% has the same revenue impact as increasing traffic by 20%—but costs almost nothing. Add-ons are the highest-leverage growth tactic in the rental business.

Consider this: a bride books a flower wall for $350. The average rental company says, "Great, see you Saturday." The profitable one says, "Great—would you also like columns to frame the ceremony, aisle markers to guide guests, and a cake table backdrop for photos?" That $350 booking becomes $1,100. Same client, same delivery truck, same setup crew—triple the revenue.

I had a Nashville client who tracked this for a year. Her average ticket without add-ons was $420. With add-ons, it was $890. She didn't acquire a single new customer that year. Her revenue doubled, and her profit tripled because add-on items carry higher margins and zero additional acquisition cost.

## The 5 Add-Ons That Actually Sell

Not all add-ons are created equal. Based on years of data from hundreds of rental companies, these five consistently perform:

1. **Ceremony Columns** – Frame the altar or archway with elegant floral columns. They create a stunning focal point and are easy to transport.
2. **Aisle Markers** – Guide guests down the aisle with small floral arrangements or petals. They add a romantic touch and are simple to set up.
3. **Cake Table Backdrop** – A mini flower wall or garland behind the cake table makes for gorgeous photos and is a natural upsell.
4. **Bridal Bouquet Artificial Flowers** – Offer a matching bouquet for the bride. Since they're artificial, they last forever and can be kept as a keepsake.
5. **Faux Floral Arrangements for Reception Tables** – Centerpieces, garlands, and table runners that tie the whole theme together.

These items are lightweight, reusable, and complement the flower wall perfectly. Plus, they're affordable for the client and highly profitable for you.

## How to Present Add-Ons Without Sounding Pushy

Timing and framing are everything. Don't overwhelm the client at the first inquiry. Instead, after they've committed to the flower wall, present add-ons as a way to enhance their vision. Use phrases like:

- "Many couples who book this wall also add aisle markers to create a cohesive look."
- "Would you like to see how columns would frame your ceremony?"
- "I can send you a few options for table arrangements that match your colors."

Focus on the benefit—beautiful photos, a cohesive theme, less stress—not the sale. When you position add-ons as helpful solutions, clients appreciate the guidance.
