Cultural traditions add depth and meaning to any wedding. In Stella and Jared’s case, the Nigerian reception was full of dance, music, and symbolic rituals. ## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

1. **Prioritize high-impact areas**: Use real flowers for the bridal bouquet and ceremony arch, but opt for affordable wedding flowers like silk roses or peonies for centerpieces and reception decor.
2. **Create a cohesive color story**: Choose two or three main colors and repeat them throughout the day. For a high-fashion look, stick to one neutral and one bold accent.
3. **Mix textures**: Combine matte and glossy finishes in your fake flowers centerpiece to mimic the look of fresh blooms. Add metallic accents (gold vases, silver candle holders) for a luxe feel.
4. **Don’t forget lighting**: Uplighting in your accent color can transform a simple room into a dramatic space. This is one of the most effective wedding venue decoration ideas for creating mood.
5. **Test your arrangements**: Before the big day, assemble one fake flowers centerpiece to ensure it matches your vision. Adjust colors or heights as needed.
