We are truly in awe of weddings that achieve that perfect balance between meaningful tradition and modern design, and this one, dear readers, really blew us away. Nhu + Long’s multi-event wedding in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam caught our attention at first because of its jaw-dropping contemporary ceremony and reception at Gem. The floral design was nothing short of spectacular—flowers floated overhead, creating a dreamy canopy that transformed the space. For DIY brides and wedding planners seeking inspiration, this real wedding offers a treasure trove of ideas for incorporating lush florals without breaking the bank. In this article, we’ll break down the key floral elements and share practical tips on how to recreate a similar look using affordable wedding flowers, bridal bouquet artificial flowers, and silk flowers wedding decor.

## The Magic of Floating Flower Installations

One of the most striking features of Nhu and Long’s wedding was the overhead floral installation. Hundreds of blooms seemed to float in mid-air, suspended from the ceiling in a cascading pattern. This effect creates a sense of intimacy and wonder, perfect for both ceremony and reception spaces. To achieve this look on a budget, consider using affordable wedding flowers such as baby’s breath, carnations, or even preserved greenery. For a more durable option that can be reused or repurposed, silk flowers wedding decor is ideal—they maintain their shape and color throughout the event and beyond. When planning your own floating installation, work with your venue to find secure hanging points and use fishing line or clear wire for an invisible effect.

## Bridal Bouquet: The Perfect Faux Finish

The bride’s bouquet was a lush mix of roses, orchids, and trailing vines, perfectly complementing the ethereal theme. For brides who want to keep their bouquet as a keepsake, bridal bouquet artificial flowers are a wonderful choice. High-quality silk flowers can mimic the texture and sheen of real blooms, and they won’t wilt under hot lights or during a long wedding day. When selecting artificial stems, look for ones with wired stems so you can shape them easily. Pair your faux bouquet with real greenery accents for a hybrid approach that feels fresh and authentic. This also helps you stay within a modest floral budget while still achieving a high-end look.

## Ceremony Arch and Aisle Decor

The ceremony arch at Gem was adorned with cascading florals that echoed the floating overhead installation. To recreate this, start with a sturdy arch frame and attach greenery garlands as a base. Then, add clusters of affordable wedding flowers like spray roses, chrysanthemums, or even dried flowers for texture. For a touch of luxury, incorporate silk orchids or peonies—these are often more cost-effective than their fresh counterparts and can be sourced in bulk. Line the aisle with low arrangements or lanterns filled with faux blooms to guide guests to the altar. This approach ensures your ceremony space looks lush and romantic without requiring last-minute flower care.

## Reception Tablescapes with Lasting Beauty

At the reception, centerpieces featured tall, airy arrangements that didn’t block sightlines—perfect for encouraging conversation. Use a mix of tall vases and low clusters to create visual rhythm. For the tall pieces, consider using affordable wedding flowers like eucalyptus, stock, or alstroemeria, and fill in with silk flowers wedding decor such as faux hydrangeas or roses for volume. Low centerpieces can be created with floating candles and a few artificial blooms in water—this technique is both elegant and budget-friendly. Since artificial flowers don’t require water changes or refrigeration, you can set up your tables the night before, saving precious time on the wedding day.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

- **Start with a color palette:** Choose 2-3 main colors and stick to them for a cohesive look. Artificial flowers come in every shade, so you can match your theme exactly.
- **Mix real and faux:** Use real flowers for focal points like the bridal bouquet and artificial flowers for large installations and centerpieces to save money.
- **Buy in bulk:** Purchase affordable wedding flowers from wholesalers or online retailers. Many offer discounts for bulk orders of silk flowers wedding decor.
- **Test your setup:** If using hanging installations, do a trial run with lightweight faux flowers to ensure safety and visual balance.
- **Repurpose after the wedding:** Artificial bridal bouquets and centerpieces can be given as gifts to family or used to decorate your home.
