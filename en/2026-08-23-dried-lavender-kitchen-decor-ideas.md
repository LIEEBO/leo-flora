Celebrity chef Clodagh McKenna’s recent kitchen renovation has set the design world abuzz, and for good reason. Her clever use of dried lavender hanging from ceiling beams has transformed her kitchen into a fairytale-like space that exudes rural charm. If you’ve been looking for rustic kitchen ideas that blend practicality with beauty, this is the trend to try. Not only does it look stunning, but it also brings a gentle fragrance and a low-maintenance alternative to fresh flowers. We spoke to interior experts to uncover how you can achieve this look in your own home—and how to adapt it for weddings and events.

## Why Dried Lavender Works in the Kitchen

Dried lavender is more than just a pretty decoration. According to Ntola Obazee, creative director at Kutchenhaus, it’s a versatile element that can elevate any space. “Dried lavender can be used across the entire home, and we often see it hung up in places such as the bathroom or on doors. However, what most people don’t automatically think of is using it in the kitchen,” she explains. “If you have a rail above the worktop, or at the end of your cabinet or island, then lavender, along with other dried herbs, is great at adding scent as well as visual interest to the space.”

Lavender’s gentle scent helps mask stale food odors, making it a practical choice for kitchens. Plus, unlike fresh flowers that wilt, dried lavender lasts for months, making it a cost-effective and sustainable option. This same principle applies to artificial flowers—whether you’re decorating a kitchen or planning a wedding, faux botanicals offer longevity and beauty without the upkeep.

## How to Get the Look: Hanging Dried Lavender

Clodagh’s approach is simple yet impactful: she hangs bunches of dried lavender from ceiling beams, spacing them between cookware. To replicate this, start by gathering several stems of dried lavender and tying them tightly with twine or ribbon. Hang them from hooks, beams, or a sturdy rail. For a cohesive look, alternate lavender with hanging pots, pans, or even dried herbs like rosemary and thyme.

If you don’t have exposed beams, consider hanging lavender from a wooden ladder leaned against a wall, or from a decorative wall-mounted rack. Another idea is to place small bundles in glass vases or mason jars on open shelves. The key is to create visual rhythm and texture, just as Clodagh did.

## Adapting the Trend for Weddings and Events

As a wedding decorator or DIY bride, you can borrow this rustic charm for your big day. Dried lavender is a beautiful addition to table settings, ceremony arches, and even bridal bouquets. For a long-lasting centerpiece, combine dried lavender with a **fake flowers centerpiece**—silk or faux blooms that mimic fresh flowers but won’t wither during the reception. This hybrid approach gives you the best of both worlds: the charm of dried botanicals and the durability of artificial flowers.

For a show-stopping backdrop, consider a **wedding flower wall backdrop** made of faux greenery and dried lavender accents. This not only photographs beautifully but also serves as a memorable focal point for your ceremony or photo booth. Artificial flowers are a practical choice for such installations because they can be prepared weeks in advance and reused for other events.

If you’re crafting your own **bridal bouquet artificial flowers**, mix silk roses, eucalyptus, and dried lavender for a romantic, rustic feel. The combination looks fresh and fragrant, yet the bouquet will stay perfect from the first look to the last dance. Artificial flowers also allow you to incorporate blooms that are out of season or have symbolic meaning without worrying about wilting.

## Practical Tips for Decorating with Dried Lavender

- **Choose quality lavender:** Look for dried lavender that retains its color and scent. Store it in a cool, dry place until ready to use.
- **Mix with other dried elements:** Combine lavender with dried wheat, statice, or eucalyptus for a fuller, more textured arrangement.
- **Hang with care:** Use strong twine or wire to ensure bundles stay secure. If hanging from beams, use hooks that won’t damage the wood.
- **Use in unexpected places:** Beyond the kitchen, lavender can grace bathroom vanities, entryway consoles, or even dining table centerpieces.
- **Create a wedding flower wall backdrop:** Attach faux flowers and dried lavender to a wooden frame or foam board for a stunning, reusable backdrop.
- **For bridal bouquets:** Choose artificial flowers that feel soft and look realistic. Pair them with dried lavender for a bohemian vibe.
