This thoughtful alternative offers the elegance of fresh flowers without the fragility, allowing you to focus on remembering and celebrating a life.

## Why Choose Temporary Artificial Flower Hire?

Fresh flowers are beautiful, but they are perishable. A funeral service often spans several days—viewing, ceremony, and burial or cremation. Fresh arrangements may not hold up, especially in unpredictable weather. Rain can bruise petals, wind can scatter them, and heat can cause rapid wilting. This means additional costs for replacements and the distress of seeing a tribute fade prematurely.

Temporary artificial flower hire solves these problems. You rent high-quality silk arrangements for the duration of the service, then return them afterward. This is both cost-effective and environmentally friendly, as it reduces waste. Plus, you have complete control over the look—choosing colors and styles that reflect the departed’s personality.

Another advantage is maintenance-free convenience. You don’t need to worry about watering, refrigeration, or clearing fallen petals. ## How Artificial Flower Hire Works

The process is straightforward and designed to reduce your stress. Most providers, like Stems From Heaven, offer an online catalog or consultation service. You select the arrangements you need—casket sprays, standing sprays, pedestal arrangements, or wreaths. Then, the company delivers and sets up the flowers at the funeral venue, whether that’s a church, crematorium, or family home. After the service, they collect the items, leaving you with only memories.

This service is available across the UK, with delivery times that fit your schedule. Some providers even offer same-day delivery for urgent needs. Because you’re renting, you can afford larger, more elaborate displays than you might if buying fresh flowers outright.

### Practical Tips for Choosing Artificial Funeral Flowers

- **Consider the venue:** Indoor venues allow for more delicate arrangements, while outdoor services may require sturdier displays that can withstand wind.
- **Match the color palette:** Choose flowers that reflect the deceased’s favorite colors or the season. Silks come in every shade imaginable.
- **Mix textures:** Combine different flower types and foliage to create depth and realism. High-quality silks often include subtle variations in color and texture.
- **Ask about customization:** Many providers allow you to add personal touches, such as a favorite photograph or a small memento.
- **Plan for the aftermath:** If you want keepsakes, ask if any flowers can be purchased separately.
