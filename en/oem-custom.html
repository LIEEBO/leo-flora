OEM WORKFLOW

From Brief to Container Loading

    Share Reference & Specs

    Send base SKU numbers from our catalog, reference images, Pantone codes, target dimensions, and estimated quantity.

    Global Logistics Handover

    Packed to your carton specifications and delivered directly to your Yiwu forwarding warehouse, port, or air courier.
