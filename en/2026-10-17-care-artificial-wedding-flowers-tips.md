## 1. Clean and Dust Regularly—But Don't Wash

One of the biggest mistakes people make with artificial plants and flowers is washing them. While it might seem like a good idea to give them a quick rinse, modern faux florals—especially those with real-touch textures—are designed to be dusted, not drenched. Washing can damage the delicate coatings that give them a realistic feel and appearance.

Instead, use a soft cloth or a feather duster to gently remove dust. For more stubborn dirt, a slightly damp cloth works well—just avoid rubbing too hard. This is especially important for bridal bouquet artificial flowers, where every detail matters. A gentle dusting will keep your bouquet looking as vibrant as the day you received it.

For potted arrangements that include moss or dried floral foam, avoid washing altogether. These materials can harbor bacteria and lose their appearance over time. Simply replace the moss layer periodically to keep everything looking fresh. Many suppliers, including yiwuleoflowers, offer replacement moss packets, making it easy to refresh your arrangements.

## 2. Keep Away from Direct Sunlight and Rain

Unlike real plants, artificial flowers don't thrive in direct sunlight—they fade. Prolonged exposure to UV rays can cause colors to dull and materials to become brittle. Similarly, while many faux flowers are water-resistant, repeated exposure to rain can take a toll.

If you're using artificial flowers outdoors, opt for sheltered areas like covered patios or pergolas. For indoor weddings, keep arrangements away from windows with harsh afternoon sun. By protecting your flowers from the elements, you'll ensure they remain stunning for multiple events—whether it's your wedding, a bridal shower, or a future anniversary party.

## 3. Prepare for Outdoor Conditions

Planning an outdoor wedding? Artificial flowers can absolutely work, but they need a little extra prep. If your venue is exposed to the elements, consider using UV-resistant sprays (available at craft stores) to protect colors. Also, secure arrangements with weights or floral foam to prevent wind from toppling them.

For bridal bouquet artificial flowers, keep them out of direct sun during photos and store them in a cool, dry place before the ceremony. This simple step will keep them looking picture-perfect all day long.

## 4. Store Properly Between Uses

After the wedding, you'll want to store your artificial flowers carefully to reuse them for future events or as home decor. Clean them thoroughly, then wrap them in tissue paper or place them in a storage box. Avoid crushing them—especially delicate blooms like roses or peonies. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

If you're a wedding planner, proper storage is key to maintaining your inventory. Label boxes by flower type and color to make future setups a breeze.
