One of the biggest stressors for DIY brides is the cost of fresh flowers—especially for a large estate venue like Castle Hill Inn. By opting for **affordable wedding flowers** in the form of high-quality artificial stems, you can create lush, voluminous arrangements at a fraction of the price. ## Design Tips for a Coastal Garden-Party Aesthetic
To capture that romantic, airy vibe, think in layers and textures. Start with a neutral base of white and ivory, then add touches of green and pale blue. Use **artificial flowers for wedding** garlands to frame the ceremony arch or line the aisle. For centerpieces, mix tall, clear vases with floating blooms and low clusters of silk flowers on mirrors. Don’t forget the power of greenery—faux ferns, ivy, and olive branches add volume and movement. The key is to choose flowers with realistic petals and stems, like those from trusted brands that specialize in **realistic artificial flowers**. This ensures your decor looks natural in photos and in person.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Wedding Planners
- **Order samples first:** Test the color and texture of your chosen artificial flowers before buying in bulk. Many suppliers offer sample stems for a small fee.
- **Mix high and low:** Use a few high-quality statement blooms (like garden roses) alongside more affordable filler flowers to balance cost and realism.
- **Consider the venue:** For outdoor weddings, choose artificial flowers that are UV-resistant if they’ll be in direct sunlight, and avoid delicate silks that may fray in wind.
- **Build your own arrangements:** Purchase stems and design your bouquet, boutonnieres, and centerpieces a few weeks ahead. This allows time to adjust and ensures everything matches your vision.
- **Store properly:** Keep your faux florals in a cool, dry place until the big day. A quick steam can remove any creases and restore their fresh look.
