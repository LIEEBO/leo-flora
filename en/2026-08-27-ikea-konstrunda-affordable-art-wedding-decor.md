# IKEA KONSTRUNDA: Affordable Art & Wedding Decor Inspo

Art has long been seen as a luxury reserved for galleries and the wealthy. But IKEA is changing that with its new limited-edition KONSTRUNDA collection, launching September 1st. With prices starting at just £19, these art objects, furniture, and soft furnishings are designed to make art accessible to everyone—and they're perfect for adding a creative, budget-friendly touch to your home or wedding decor.

As a wedding planner or DIY bride, you know that decor can make or break a venue—and your budget. The KONSTRUNDA collection offers a treasure trove of unique pieces that can double as stunning wedding accents, from statement vases to playful wall hangings. Let's dive into how you can use these affordable finds to elevate your wedding styling, alongside tips for incorporating affordable wedding flowers and realistic artificial flowers into your big day.

## Why KONSTRUNDA Is a Game-Changer for Wedding Decor

The KONSTRUNDA range, whose name means "art tour" in Swedish, features 22 objects created by seven designers and artists. It includes purely decorative pieces, playful furniture like stools and side tables, and soft furnishings such as cushions and rugs that can also be hung on walls. This versatility makes it a goldmine for wedding decorators who want to add artistic flair without breaking the bank.

Karin Gustavsson, the collection's creative leader, says, "Art should be available for all to enjoy, not a luxury reserved for galleries. Art evokes emotion, sparks conversation, and helps transform a house into a home." The same can be said for wedding decor—every element should tell your love story and make your guests feel welcome.

### KONSTRUNDA Decoration (28") – £22

This eye-catching piece is perfect for displaying trinkets, but it also works as a unique key holder or a standalone centerpiece on a sideboard. At a wedding, use it as a creative escort card holder or a quirky sign-in station accent.

Created by Swedish artist Lisa Reiser, this abstract, shadow-like vase is made from mouth-blown glass. It's a stunning vessel for affordable wedding flowers, whether you're using fresh blooms or realistic artificial flowers. The black-and-white design complements any color palette, making it a versatile addition to your wedding decor.

## Incorporating Affordable Wedding Flowers with KONSTRUNDA

Fresh flowers can eat up a huge chunk of your wedding budget. That's why many savvy brides are turning to affordable wedding flowers, including high-quality realistic artificial flowers. These faux blooms look just as beautiful as the real thing but can be reused for other events or even as home decor after the wedding.

Pair your KONSTRUNDA vases with realistic artificial flowers to create stunning centerpieces that won't wilt. You can also use these vases for bridal bouquets, ceremony arches, or even as part of your wedding flower wall backdrop. The key is to mix textures and heights for a lush, designer look.

## Creating a Wedding Flower Wall Backdrop on a Budget

A wedding flower wall backdrop is a showstopper, but hiring one can cost hundreds or even thousands of dollars. With a little creativity and the KONSTRUNDA collection, you can DIY your own for a fraction of the price. Use the rugs or wall hangings from the collection as a base, then attach realistic artificial flowers to create a lush, Instagram-worthy backdrop. Not only will it save you money, but you can also repurpose the flowers for other decor elements like table runners or aisle markers.

## Practical Tips for Using Art in Wedding Decor

- **Mix High and Low:** Combine KONSTRUNDA art pieces with thrifted finds or heirloom items for a curated, personal look.
- **Think Beyond the Table:** Use wall art as a photo booth backdrop or as a dramatic entrance to your ceremony space.
- **Repurpose After the Wedding:** These pieces are designed to be loved at home, so after the big day, they'll become cherished mementos of your celebration.
- **Coordinate Colors:** Choose KONSTRUNDA pieces that complement your wedding palette to create a cohesive aesthetic.
