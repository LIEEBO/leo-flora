# Stunning Indian Wedding Decor Ideas with Artificial Flowers

When Jaskaren and Sam tied the knot at Grand Velas Los Cabos, they blended their Punjabi and Persian heritages into a week-long celebration filled with love, tradition, and breathtaking décor. From an ocean-view mandap adorned with white, blush, and beige blooms to a starlit cocktail reception, their wedding was a masterclass in fusion design. But what truly made their décor unforgettable? The strategic use of flowers to create a romantic, inclusive atmosphere.

For DIY brides and wedding planners, Jaskaren and Sam's wedding offers endless inspiration—especially when it comes to using **artificial flowers for wedding** décor. Here's how to recreate their look with practical, budget-friendly tips.

## 1. Create a Dreamy Mandap with Artificial Blooms

Jaskaren envisioned a beautiful setting with white, blush, and beige flowers adorning the ocean-view mandap. To achieve this look without the worry of wilting blooms under the sun, consider high-quality artificial flowers. Silk and latex options from **leofaux** offer realistic textures and vibrant colors that last all day. Use them to drape the mandap pillars, create a floral canopy, or line the aisle. The best part? You can customize the color palette to match your outfit and theme perfectly.

**Practical tip:** Mix artificial flowers with real greenery for a natural look. Focus on key areas like the mandap entrance and the couple's seating to maximize impact.

## 2. Make a Statement with a Wedding Flower Wall Backdrop

A **wedding flower wall backdrop** is a must-have for modern Indian weddings. It serves as a stunning photo op and a beautiful focal point for the ceremony or reception. For their fusion wedding, Jaskaren and Sam could have used a flower wall to incorporate both Punjabi and Persian floral motifs. Artificial flowers are ideal for this because they hold their shape and color, even under event lighting.

**DIY tip:** Create a flower wall using a foam or wooden frame, then attach artificial flower stems in a dense, ombre pattern. Add fairy lights for a magical evening glow.

## 3. Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas for a Fusion Celebration

Jaskaren and Sam's wedding featured a starlit cocktail reception and a Persian Sofreh table. Here are some **wedding venue decoration ideas** that blend cultures seamlessly:

- **Table centerpieces:** Use low arrangements of artificial flowers in white, blush, and beige to complement both Punjabi and Persian aesthetics. Add candles and metallic accents.
- **Ceiling installations:** Hang artificial flower garlands or floating blooms above the dance floor for a whimsical touch.
- **Entryway décor:** Welcome guests with a floral arch or a flower-lined pathway using artificial flowers that stay fresh all day.
- **Sofreh table:** Enhance the traditional Persian spread with artificial flowers that won't interfere with food items.

**Practical tip:** Choose artificial flowers that match your cultural color symbolism. For Punjabi weddings, reds and golds are traditional; for Persian, whites and greens are popular. Artificial flowers let you combine both without compromise.
