## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

1. **Order samples first.** Colors can look different online. Always request a sample or visit a display to check quality and hue.
2. **Mix real and artificial.** Use artificial flowers for areas that need to last (arch, backdrop) and fresh flowers for accents like corsages.
3. **Plan ahead.** Artificial flowers can be arranged weeks in advance, so you're not scrambling the day before.
4. **Ask about rental terms.** If renting, check cleaning fees, delivery, and return policies.
5. **Think about storage.** If you buy, have a plan for storing and transporting arrangements.
6. **Work with a supplier who understands weddings.** Look for one that offers custom design services and a variety of configurations.
