Celebrity chef Clodagh McKenna recently unveiled her new kitchen renovation, and one small detail makes the space ooze rural charm: bunches of dried lavender hanging from the beams. It’s a fairytale look that has captured the hearts of interior enthusiasts—and it’s surprisingly easy to recreate, whether in your kitchen or as part of your wedding decor.

As a wedding planner or DIY bride, you know that creating a warm, inviting atmosphere doesn’t have to break the bank. Dried lavender offers an affordable, low-maintenance alternative to fresh flowers, and it pairs beautifully with faux floral arrangements to achieve that rustic, romantic aesthetic. In this article, we’ll explore how to get Clodagh’s look, why dried herbs are a smart choice, and how you can incorporate these ideas into your wedding styling.

## Why Dried Lavender Works in Kitchens and Weddings

Dried lavender is more than just a pretty decoration. Its gentle scent adds a calming, fresh aroma to any space, while its muted purple tones bring a touch of countryside charm. Interior experts like Ntola Obazee, creative director at Kutchenhaus, highlight that dried lavender can be used throughout the home—and it’s especially effective in kitchens where it helps mask old food smells.

For weddings, dried lavender is a versatile element. It can be used in bridal bouquet artificial flowers, as table scatter, or hung from ceremony arches. Unlike fresh blooms, dried lavender lasts for months, making it a cost-effective choice for couples who want to prepare decorations in advance. Plus, it aligns perfectly with the growing trend of eco-friendly, sustainable weddings.

## How to Get the Look: Styling Tips from Clodagh’s Kitchen

Clodagh’s kitchen features a Belfast sink, rich dark wood, flagstone floors, and open shelving. The standout detail? Bunches of dried lavender interspersed with hanging cookware. The result is a space that feels both functional and whimsical.

To recreate this in your own home or event, start by gathering small bunches of dried lavender. Tie them with twine or rustic ribbon, leaving a loop for hanging. Then, hang them from ceiling beams, shelf brackets, or a pot rack. You can also drape them over cabinet doors or place them in vintage jugs on open shelves.

For a wedding twist, consider using dried lavender in your bridal bouquet artificial flowers. Mix faux blooms with real dried lavender stems to create a long-lasting keepsake. Or, hang small lavender bunches from chair backs at the reception for a fragrant, visual detail that guests will love.

## Incorporating Faux Floral Arrangements for Longevity

While dried lavender is a star, combining it with faux floral arrangements ensures your decor stays vibrant throughout the event and beyond. High-quality silk flowers can mimic the look of fresh blooms without wilting, and they’re perfect for bridal bouquets, centerpieces, and ceremony backdrops.

One of the biggest advantages of faux florals is cost. Affordable wedding flowers are a top priority for many couples, and faux arrangements allow you to achieve a lush, full look without the premium price tag. You can also repurpose them after the wedding—use them to decorate your home, gift to guests, or sell to other brides.

When mixing dried lavender with faux flowers, aim for a balanced palette. Soft purples, creams, and greens create a cohesive, romantic feel. Consider adding eucalyptus, dusty miller, or faux baby’s breath to enhance texture and volume.

## Practical Tips for Using Dried Lavender in Your Wedding Decor

- **Preparation**: If you’re using fresh lavender, dry it by hanging bundles upside down in a cool, dark place for 2–3 weeks. For a quicker option, purchase pre-dried lavender from craft stores or online.
- **Storage**: Keep dried lavender away from direct sunlight to preserve its color. Store in airtight containers until you’re ready to decorate.
- **Placement**: Use lavender in areas where scent matters—near entrances, restrooms, or dining areas. It’s also lovely in bridal bouquets or as a boutonniere alternative.
- **Combining with Faux**: For bridal bouquet artificial flowers, wrap faux stems with dried lavender sprigs using floral tape. This adds a natural, fragrant element that won’t wilt.
- **Budget-Friendly**: Dried lavender is inexpensive, especially when bought in bulk. Pair it with faux florals to create stunning centerpieces that fit any budget.
