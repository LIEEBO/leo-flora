# Destination Wedding Planning Checklist: Your Complete Floral Decor Guide

Picture this: you're exchanging vows on a sun-drenched beach in Mexico or in a Tuscan vineyard, surrounded by your closest friends and family. Destination weddings make that dream a reality—and with about 25% of US couples now choosing to tie the knot away from home, you're in good company. But planning a wedding in another country comes with unique challenges, especially when it comes to floral decor. Fresh flowers can wilt in the heat, cost a fortune to import, and create logistical headaches. That's where a smart destination wedding planning checklist—with a floral twist—saves the day.

## Why Start Planning 12–18 Months Ahead?

The average destination wedding costs around $39,000 (The Knot, 2025), so early planning isn't just smart—it's essential. Giving yourself 12 to 18 months before the big day means better venue availability, stronger negotiating power on group travel rates, and ample time to research local vendors. But here's the floral secret: while you're booking the venue and flights, start thinking about your flowers too. Fresh blooms are perishable and often require local sourcing, which can be limited in remote destinations. Many savvy couples turn to **realistic artificial flowers** for their destination celebrations—they travel flat-packed, never wilt, and can be arranged days in advance.

## The Destination Wedding Planning Checklist

### 12–18 Months Out: Research and Book

- **Choose your destination** based on season, budget, and guest travel ease. Popular picks include Mexico (34% of international destination weddings), Europe (26%), and the Caribbean (21%).
- **Set your guest list**—destination weddings typically host 30 to 60 guests, making them more intimate.
- **Book your venue and hire a planner**—74% of couples marrying internationally hire a wedding planner. Ask about floral restrictions and setup times.
- **Start sourcing decor**—this is the perfect time to explore **wholesale silk flowers**. They offer huge savings over fresh blooms and let you preview your arrangements months ahead.

### 8–12 Months Out: Legalities and Logistics

- **Research legal requirements** for marrying abroad (residency periods, paperwork, translations).
- **Block hotel rooms** and negotiate group rates for guests.
- **Order your artificial flowers**—**fake flowers centerpiece** options can be customized to match your color palette and shipped well in advance. leoflowers in Yiwu offers a wide range of silk blooms perfect for destination weddings.

### 4–6 Months Out: Design and Details

- **Finalize your floral design**—create sample centerpieces using your artificial flowers. Realistic artificial flowers look stunning in photos and hold up in heat and humidity.
- **Plan your ceremony arch and aisle decor**—silk flower installations are lightweight and easy to transport.
- **Send invitations** with travel details and RSVP deadlines.

### 1–2 Months Out: Confirm and Pack

- **Confirm all vendor bookings** and create a detailed timeline.
- **Pack your decor**—artificial flowers pack flat and weigh almost nothing compared to fresh arrangements.
- **Assign a setup crew**—friends or family can help arrange centerpieces on-site.

### Week Of: Relax and Enjoy

- **Do a final walkthrough** with your planner and confirm delivery of any rentals.
- **Hand off your floral boxes** to your coordinator with labeled instructions.
- **Breathe**—you've got this!
