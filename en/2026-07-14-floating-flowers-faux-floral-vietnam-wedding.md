One of the most unforgettable elements of Nhu + Long’s wedding was the overhead floral installation. Flowers seemed to float above the ceremony space, creating a canopy of color and texture. This look is not only stunning but also surprisingly achievable with **faux floral arrangements**. ## Practical Tips for Recreating This Look

Want to bring Nhu and Long’s floating flower magic to your own wedding? Here are some actionable tips:

- **Choose lightweight faux blooms**: Opt for silk or polyester flowers that won’t pull down your suspension system. Peonies, orchids, and hydrangeas work beautifully.
- **Mix real and faux**: For budget-friendly impact, use faux floral arrangements for overhead installations and real flowers for bouquet or boutonniere accents.
- **Test your hanging method**: Whether using clear fishing line, wooden hoops, or a custom frame, always test the weight and balance before the big day.
- **Consider color continuity**: If your wedding spans multiple days, use a consistent color palette across all faux floral pieces to tie the events together.

## Why Faux Florals Are Perfect for Multi-Day Weddings

Multi-day celebrations like Nhu + Long’s require decor that lasts. For event decorators, this means you can set up the ceremony space days in advance and focus on other details as the celebration unfolds.
