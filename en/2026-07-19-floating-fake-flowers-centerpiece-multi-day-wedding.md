## Practical Tips for Using Artificial Flowers in Wedding Decor

1. **Choose high-quality silk flowers**: Invest in realistic **wholesale silk flowers** that mimic natural textures. Touch-test petals and leaves before purchasing—they should feel soft, not plasticky.

3. **Secure installations properly**: For overhead decorations, ensure your **artificial flowers for wedding** are firmly attached to the frame. Use zip ties or floral wire, and test the weight before the event.
