## How to Unpack and Arrange Artificial Plants for Weddings

So you've decided to use artificial flowers for wedding decor—smart choice! Not only are they budget-friendly, but they also stay fresh-looking from the first dance to the last toast. Whether you're styling a lush faux fern for the ceremony aisle or a potted topiary to frame the sweetheart table, the process of unpacking and arranging is similar for all. Here's how to get that 'nearly natural' effect every time, so your affordable wedding flowers look anything but cheap.

### Step 1: Unpack and Stand Your Plant Upright

Your artificial plant—whether it's a delicate orchid centerpiece or a grand topiary—will arrive carefully packaged in a sturdy box. Find a clean, open workspace and carefully open all taped corners (you may need scissors). Inside, your faux plant will be wrapped in protective plastic. Gently remove it and stand it upright on the floor or a table.

Most topiary trees come in a starter pot, which you can later slip inside a decorative planter for a more polished look. This also helps stabilize the plant, especially for taller designs that might be used as wedding aisle markers or backdrop accents.

### Step 2: Bend Down Branches for a Natural Spread

When you first unpack your artificial plant, you'll notice the foliage is tightly packed and folded upward against the stem. This is to protect it during shipping. Start at the bottom and carefully pull down each section or tier of greenery.

Because each branch has a hidden flexible wire, you can easily bend and separate them from the trunk. Take your time, working one branch at a time, to create a natural, full spread. This is especially important if you're using these plants as part of your bridal bouquet artificial flowers—you want each stem to have its own personality.

### Step 3: Fluff Out All the Leaves

Once the branches are separated, move through each tier and begin fluffing the leaves. Bend each one outward, alternating positions and directions to avoid a stiff or uniform look. For a lifelike touch, curve each leaf slightly along its spine—this gives your foliage a gentle, natural arch that mimics real growth.

This step is key when you're mixing these plants with other affordable wedding flowers. The more varied the leaf angles, the more organic and expensive the arrangement will appear.

### Step 4: Continue Styling and Fluffing Throughout

Work your way around the plant from the bottom up, using your fingers to shape the leaves into realistic positions. Real plants have natural variations in color and shape, and good-quality artificial flowers reflect this as well. Position the leaves to highlight these subtle details—some leaves might be darker, some lighter, some slightly curled.

Don't worry if some areas look less than perfect. This irregularity is exactly what makes the plant appear more real. If any branch looks uneven, simply re-bend it until you're happy with the result. This is your chance to customize the plant to fit your wedding theme, whether that's romantic, rustic, or modern.

### Step 5: Add Accompanying Setting to Base

While many artificial plants come stabilized in their own pot, some include decorative base materials like rocks or moss that you'll need to add yourself. If your plant arrives in a starter pot, place it inside your chosen decorative planter and fill the space around it with floral foam, gravel, or moss. This not only keeps the plant stable but also adds a finished, designer look.

For wedding use, you can even hide small LED lights or fairy lights in the base for an extra magical touch. This works beautifully for evening receptions or outdoor ceremonies.

### Final Tips for Wedding Planners and DIY Brides

- **Start early**: Unpack and arrange all your artificial plants at least a week before the wedding. This gives you time to fluff, adjust, and place them perfectly.
- **Use quality materials**: Invest in good-quality artificial flowers for wedding decor. They look better, last longer, and are worth the extra cost.
- **Mix and match**: Combine your faux plants with fresh flowers or other affordable wedding flowers to create a lush, layered look without breaking the bank.
- **Store properly**: After the wedding, gently clean your artificial plants with a soft cloth or a hairdryer on cool setting, then store them in a dry, dust-free box for future events.

Artificial greenery is a wedding decorator's best friend. With a little patience and these simple steps, your faux plants will look so real that even the most discerning guest won't be able to tell the difference. Happy decorating!
