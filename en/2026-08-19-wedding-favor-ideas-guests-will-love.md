## Why Guests Skip Favors (And How to Fix It)

Most favors end up left behind because they’re generic, bulky, or forgettable. Guests don’t need another engraved coaster or a bag of almonds they’ll never open. Instead, choose favors that serve a purpose or evoke a memory. Think edible treats from your hometown, mini succulents guests can plant, or a personalized candle that reminds them of your first dance. When you tie your favor to your story, it becomes something they’ll treasure—not something they’ll leave on the table.

## How to Display Favors with Your Decor

Favors don’t have to sit in a sad basket by the exit. Integrate them into your tablescape. Use a **fake flowers centerpiece** as the base—place favors around the stems or tuck them into the greenery. This not only saves space but also adds a pop of color. If you’re using **affordable wedding flowers** for the big arrangements, you can splurge on nicer favors without blowing your budget. For a cohesive look, match your favor wrapping to your color palette and add a small tag that ties into your wedding theme.

## Practical Tips for Making Favors Disappear

- **Make them visible:** Don’t hide favors at the back of the room. Put one at each place setting or on a prominent table near the exit.
- **Keep them small:** Guests are more likely to take something they can slip into a pocket or bag.
- **Add a personal note:** A handwritten thank-you tag makes any favor feel special.
- **Offer a choice:** Set up a “favor bar” with a few options—guests can pick what they love.
- **Pair with your decor:** When your favors double as place cards or table numbers, they’re impossible to miss.
