## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

- **Order extra stems.** It's better to have a few left over than to run short. A 10% buffer is a good rule of thumb.
- **Fluff and shape.** Artificial roses often arrive slightly compressed. Gently pull the petals outward to create a full, open bloom.
- **Mix textures.** Combine roses with faux hydrangeas, peonies, or greenery for a more natural look.
- **Store properly.** Keep unused stems in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to maintain their color.
- **Test your arrangements.** Do a mock-up of your centerpieces and bouquet at least two weeks before the wedding.
