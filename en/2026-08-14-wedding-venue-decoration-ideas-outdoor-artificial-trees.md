## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas: 7 Tips for Buying Outdoor Artificial Trees

Are you dreaming of a stunning outdoor wedding but worried about keeping flowers fresh and trees looking lush? Artificial trees are the perfect solution for wedding venue decoration ideas that last from ceremony to reception and beyond. But before you hit "add to cart," there's more to consider than just looks. Choosing the right outdoor artificial trees can be the difference between a magical backdrop and a wilted disaster.

In this guide, we'll walk you through everything you need to know to invest wisely in artificial trees for your wedding venue—whether it's a garden ceremony, a patio reception, or a courtyard vow exchange.

### 1. Must-Have Features for Outdoor Artificial Trees

The biggest mistake couples make is assuming all artificial trees are weatherproof. Not true! If you're using trees outdoors, you need two critical features:

- **Explicit outdoor suitability** – The product page should clearly state it's designed for outside use.
- **UV protection** – Look for trees with UV-resistant materials or a protective coating to prevent fading.

Without UV protection, a single sunny summer or a rainy winter can turn your beautiful tree into a faded, sad sight. Silk is gorgeous for indoor silk flowers wedding decor, but it doesn't hold up well outdoors. Instead, choose trees made from polyethylene or polyester blends that are built to withstand the elements.

**Pro tip:** Even with UV protection, you can extend the life of your outdoor trees by applying a UV protectant spray once a year.

### 2. Location: Plan Ahead for the Perfect Placement

Now that you know the basics, it's time to think about where your trees will live. Do you have a specific spot in mind? Maybe an entrance arch or a corner of the dance floor? Having a vision before you buy is key—artificial trees don't grow or change, so you can create your dream look instantly without waiting for nature.

Consider these placement ideas:
- **Flanking the aisle** – A pair of tall artificial trees adds symmetry and grandeur.
- **Behind the sweetheart table** – Creates a lush backdrop that looks great in photos.
- **Around the cake table** – Frames the moment with greenery.

Remember, you can even plant them directly in the ground for a more permanent look, but if you're renting a venue, opt for portable planters that you can move easily.

### 3. Size and Scale: Getting the Proportions Right

Size matters when it comes to artificial trees. A tree that's too small will get lost in a large venue, while one that's too tall can overwhelm a cozy space. Measure your venue's ceiling height (if indoors) or the open sky (if outdoors) and consider the distance from your seating areas.

For a wedding, you want trees that complement, not dominate. A good rule of thumb is to choose trees that are at least 6-8 feet tall for ceremony backdrops, and 4-5 feet for tabletop centerpieces. If you're mixing trees with a fake flowers centerpiece on each table, keep the heights varied for visual interest.

**Quick tip:** Use smaller artificial trees or branches in your centerpieces to tie the whole venue together. Pair them with silk flowers wedding decor for a cohesive look that's both elegant and practical.

### 4. Realism and Quality: Making It Look Authentic

The whole point of artificial trees is to mimic nature, so realism is non-negotiable. Look for trees with natural bark texture, varied leaf shapes, and realistic coloring. High-quality trees have multiple shades of green and even small imperfections that make them look alive.

Avoid trees that look shiny or plastic-like. Instead, choose ones with matte finishes and subtle color variations. Many premium brands now offer trees that are virtually indistinguishable from real ones—perfect for wedding venue decoration ideas that wow your guests.

**Don't forget the base!** A realistic trunk and a good-quality pot or planter make a huge difference. You can always dress up a plain pot with fabric or ribbon to match your wedding theme.

### 5. Budgeting and Long-Term Value

Artificial trees are an investment, but they pay off in the long run. Unlike real trees, they require no watering, pruning, or replacing. For wedding planners, this means you can reuse them for multiple events, making them a smart addition to your inventory.

Set a budget before you shop. Prices vary widely, but you don't have to break the bank for quality. Look for sales, or consider buying smaller trees and using them in pairs to create a fuller look. Remember, a well-chosen artificial tree can be the centerpiece of your decor for years to come.

**Money-saving idea:** If you're on a tight budget, use artificial trees as structural elements and fill in with fresh flowers for the ceremony. This gives you the best of both worlds—the permanence of faux and the fragrance of real.

### 6. Maintenance and Storage

Even artificial trees need a little TLC. Dust them regularly with a soft cloth or a gentle vacuum attachment. If they get dirty from outdoor use, wipe them down with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid harsh chemicals that could damage the finish.

When the wedding is over, store your trees properly. Keep them in a dry, cool place, ideally in their original packaging or covered with a breathable cloth. This will keep them looking fresh for your next event or a future family gathering.

### 7. Where to Buy and What to Avoid

Shop from reputable sellers who specialize in artificial plants. Read product reviews and look for detailed photos. Be wary of super-cheap options—they often look fake and fall apart quickly. Also, check the return policy in case the tree doesn't meet your expectations.

Now that you're armed with these tips, you're ready to choose outdoor artificial trees that will make your wedding venue unforgettable. Whether you're creating a romantic garden setting or a modern industrial backdrop, artificial trees offer the flexibility and beauty you need—without the stress of keeping them alive.
