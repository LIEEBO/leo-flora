## Why Real Feel Garlands and Wreaths Are Perfect for Weddings

Real feel (also called real touch, PE, or realistic) garlands and wreaths are designed to mimic the texture and appearance of fresh greenery. They’re a favorite among social media influencers and event stylists because they photograph beautifully and hold up throughout the entire day—from ceremony to reception. Unlike fresh greenery, which can droop under hot lights or start to brown by evening, real feel options stay pristine from the first dance to the last toast.

For weddings, this means you can prepare your decor days in advance. No more rushing to set up wilting garlands on the morning of your big day. You can hang them, fluff them, and forget about them—they’ll look just as fresh at midnight as they did at noon. Plus, they’re reusable, so you can repurpose them for bridal showers, anniversaries, or even as home decor after the wedding. That’s a win for your budget and the environment.

## Choosing the Right Garlands and Wreaths for Your Venue

Real feel garlands come in a variety of lengths, typically from 5 to 15 feet, making them ideal for draping over mantels, wrapping staircases, or framing an altar. You can choose from pine, cedar, or a mix of both, depending on the vibe you want. Pine offers a classic, crisp look, while cedar brings a softer, more textured feel. Mixing them can add depth and visual interest.

Wreaths, on the other hand, range from 24 to 36 inches in diameter and are available in oval or round shapes. They’re perfect for hanging on ceremony arches, entrance doors, or even as part of a **wedding flower wall backdrop**. A large wreath can serve as a focal point, while smaller ones can accent aisle chairs or the head table. Both garlands and wreaths come in lit or unlit versions, so you can add a warm glow to evening receptions without dealing with messy wires or battery packs.

## Creative Ways to Use Real Feel Greenery in Your Wedding

One of the best things about real feel garlands and wreaths is their versatility. Here are some practical ideas for incorporating them into your wedding decor:

- **Ceremony Arch**: Drape a 10-foot garland over a wooden arch and add a few cascading blooms for a romantic focal point. The greenery will frame you and your partner beautifully in photos.
- **Table Runners**: Instead of traditional flower arrangements, lay a long garland down the center of your banquet tables. Tuck in some **fake flowers centerpiece** elements like roses or peonies for a lush, full look that won’t break the bank.
- **Mantel and Staircase Decor**: If your venue has a fireplace or grand staircase, wrap garlands around the banister or drape them across the mantel. Add fairy lights for a magical evening ambiance.
- **Welcome Sign Backdrop**: Use a wreath as a backdrop for your welcome sign or seating chart. It’s a simple yet elegant touch that guests will notice immediately.
- **Bridal Suite Accents**: Hang a small wreath on the door of the bridal suite or place a garland on the vanity. It’s a lovely detail for getting-ready photos.

For the bride, real feel greenery can even be incorporated into your **bridal bouquet artificial flowers**—mix a few sprigs of faux cedar or pine with your chosen blooms for a textured, organic look that will last forever as a keepsake.

## Practical Tips for Buying and Styling Real Feel Greenery

When shopping for real feel garlands and wreaths, keep these tips in mind:

- **Check the Quality**: Look for pieces that use PE (polyethylene) materials, which feel more realistic to the touch. Bend a few leaves to see how they hold their shape.
- **Consider the Size**: Measure your space before buying. A 5-foot garland may look small on a large mantle, while a 15-foot one might overwhelm a small arch.
- **Fluff and Shape**: Real feel products often arrive compressed. Take time to fluff the branches and bend them into natural positions. This makes a huge difference in realism.
- **Mix with Fresh Elements**: For a hybrid look, combine real feel greenery with a few fresh flowers or herbs. This adds a pop of color and a lovely scent without sacrificing durability.
- **Storage**: After the wedding, gently clean the pieces and store them in a cool, dry place. They’ll be ready for your next event or as home decor.
