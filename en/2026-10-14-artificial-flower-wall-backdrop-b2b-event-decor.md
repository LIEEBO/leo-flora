## 5 Key Differences Between Wedding and Corporate Decor

Understanding these differences will help you tailor your offerings:

1. **Seasonality**: Weddings peak May–October; corporate events are year-round, with Q1 and Q4 being busiest.
2. **Budget**: Corporate clients spend more per event—$5,000–$25,000 vs. $2,500–$5,000 for weddings.
3. **Decision Cycle**: Corporate decisions take 2–8 weeks; weddings take 6–18 months.
4. **Repeat Business**: Corporate clients book annually and for multiple events; weddings are typically one-time.
5. **Design Needs**: Corporate decor must align with brand identity—think logo colors, modern aesthetics, and professional appeal.

For a **wedding flower wall backdrop**, you might focus on romance and softness. For corporate, you'll want sleek, branded designs using **faux floral arrangements** that can be easily reconfigured for different events.

## What Corporate Clients Actually Care About

Corporate clients prioritize three things: brand alignment, reliability, and impact. Your decor must reflect their brand colors and messaging. They need you to deliver on time and handle setup flawlessly. And they want Instagram-worthy moments that impress attendees.

To win them over, emphasize how your **faux floral arrangements** can be tailored to their brand. ## Pricing and Finding Corporate Clients

Corporate decor pricing should reflect the value you provide. Consider charging per square foot for backdrops, plus installation fees. A typical 8x8 ft flower wall might range from $1,500 to $3,500, depending on complexity.

Where to find clients? Start with your existing network—wedding clients often work for corporations. Attend industry events, join B2B groups, and send cold emails to event managers. One successful booking can lead to repeat business.

## Mistakes to Avoid

Don't treat corporate clients like wedding clients. They have different timelines, budgets, and expectations. Avoid overpromising on custom designs if you can't deliver. And never underestimate the power of professional presentation—your website and portfolio must look polished.
