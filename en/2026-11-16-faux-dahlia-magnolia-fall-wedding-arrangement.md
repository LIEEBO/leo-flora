# Faux Dahlia & Magnolia Fall Arrangement for Weddings

When it comes to wedding decor, seasonal simplicity often makes the most striking statement. A fall arrangement featuring artificial mauve dahlias and faux brown magnolia leaves proves that you don't need dozens of ingredients or complex techniques to create something unforgettable. This pairing of permanent botanicals is soft yet dramatic, moody yet approachable—perfect for autumn weddings and beyond.

Whether you're a DIY bride or a wedding planner looking for reliable, long-lasting decor, this simple arrangement delivers high impact with minimal effort. Best of all, using artificial stems means you can enjoy this look for years, reimagining it each season without water, care, or upkeep.

## Why Mauve Dahlias and Brown Magnolia Work Together

The magic of this combination lies in the contrast of shapes, colors, and textures. Round, petal-packed artificial dahlias serve as natural focal flowers, even in smaller sizes. Magnolia foliage, with its bold, larger leaves and two-tone texture, provides the perfect counterbalance.

The color palette is equally compelling: mauve offers a muted, soft, romantic feel, while the rich brown of magnolia leaves adds warmth. This cozy combination is ideal for fall, yet it also serves as a great base for transitioning into winter arrangements. Texture-wise, the layered petals of dahlias paired with glossy, earthy magnolia leaves create visual interest without needing extra fillers. This two-botanical recipe gives you a truly high-impact arrangement with minimal effort—a dream for busy couples and planners.

## What You'll Need

- 5–6 artificial mauve dahlias
- 2 brown magnolia leaf stems
- Vase or vessel of choice
- Floral wire cutters (optional)

## Step-by-Step Guide to Creating the Arrangement

### Step 1: Prep Your Vase

Choose a vase that complements your wedding theme. For added stability, place decorative rocks or vase stays inside. If using a clear vessel, you can cover the stems with moss or line the inside with a few magnolia leaves for a creative finish.

### Step 2: Create a Botanical Base

Begin with the artificial magnolia leaves. Be sure to shape the wire leaves! Fan them out slightly to create the base shape of your arrangement. Their broad, glossy leaves set a perfect backdrop for the mauve dahlias.

### Step 3: Add in the Faux Florals

Place 5–6 faux dahlias next, spacing them apart as focal pieces. Stagger the heights and shape the stems so some flowers rest low while others reach higher for natural variation. This technique mimics the organic look of fresh blooms and ensures a balanced composition.

### Step 4: Adjust and Fluff

Once all botanicals are in, adjust and weave in magnolia leaves to frame the dahlias, letting their warm tones make the pink flowers pop. Step back, turn the vase, and check for even balance. Angle a few magnolia leaves or dahlia blooms outward or downward to soften the edges and keep the look relaxed and organic.

**Optional Tip:** Floral cutters are optional! Most artificial stems can simply be bent to the height you want, making it easy to reuse and rearrange the arrangement for different events.
