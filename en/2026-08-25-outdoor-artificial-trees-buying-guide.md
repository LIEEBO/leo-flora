## 7 Things to Consider Before Buying Outdoor Artificial Trees

Can buying outdoor artificial trees be as simple as jumping online for 10 minutes and picking the first ones you like? Sure, but you might end up with regrets. Choosing the right artificial trees requires a little more forethought if you want long-lasting greenery that looks good day after day, year after year—whether for your driveway, back garden, or even as part of your wedding venue decoration ideas.

In this guide, we’ll walk you through everything you need to know before investing in outdoor artificial trees. And if you’re planning a wedding, these same principles apply to using faux florals for a stunning wedding flower wall backdrop or a fake flowers centerpiece that stays fresh all day.

### 1. Look for UV Protection and Outdoor Suitability

The biggest mistake people make is assuming all artificial trees are fine outside. They’re not. Without UV protection, the sun will fade colors quickly, and winter weather can damage materials.

Before you buy, check the product page for two things:
- It must say "suitable for outdoor use."
- It must mention UV protection or UV-resistant materials.

Silk leaves, common on indoor artificial plants, don’t hold up outdoors. A UV-protected tree has a special coating or additive that keeps pigments from fading. You can also buy UV protection sprays to reapply yearly, keeping your trees vibrant for seasons to come.

### 2. Choose the Right Location

Think about where you’ll place your trees before you order. Do you need a tall tree to frame a doorway, or a pair to flank a garden path? Artificial trees don’t grow, so you won’t have to wait—but you also can’t adjust their size later.

For weddings, location matters even more. A wedding flower wall backdrop can transform a plain ceremony space, but you need to consider wind, sun exposure, and how the backdrop will be secured. Similarly, a fake flowers centerpiece for outdoor tables should be heavy enough to stay put and made of weather-resistant materials.

### 3. Consider the Material Quality

Not all faux trees are created equal. Look for high-quality polyethylene or polyblend leaves, which mimic real foliage and withstand outdoor conditions. Cheap plastic looks shiny and fake, while premium materials have a natural matte finish.

If you’re mixing artificial trees with live plants or using them as wedding venue decoration ideas, quality matters. You want guests to do a double-take, not immediately spot the fake.

### 4. Think About Maintenance (Yes, There Is Some)

Outdoor artificial trees still need occasional cleaning. Dust, pollen, and bird droppings can dull their appearance. A gentle rinse with a hose and a soft cloth usually does the trick. UV sprays also need reapplication once a year.

For wedding decor, maintenance is even more critical. A wedding flower wall backdrop will be photographed up close, so make sure it’s clean and dust-free before the big day. Likewise, a fake flowers centerpiece might need a quick wipe-down if it’s been stored for months.

### 5. Decide Between Potted and Ground-Planting

Many outdoor artificial trees come with a pot or planter, but some can be planted directly into the ground. This is a great option if you want a permanent look without the maintenance of real trees. Just ensure the trunk is sturdy and the base is stable, especially in windy areas.

For weddings, potted artificial trees are more flexible—you can move them to create different layouts. They also work well as aisle markers or to frame an archway.

### 6. Budget for Quality

You get what you pay for. High-quality outdoor artificial trees cost more upfront, but they last for years. Cheap trees may fade, shed leaves, or break within one season.

If you’re a wedding planner or DIY bride, consider renting or buying reusable pieces. A wedding flower wall backdrop can be used for multiple events, and a fake flowers centerpiece can be repurposed for bridal showers or home decor.

### 7. Read Reviews and Ask Questions

Before purchasing, read customer reviews about color accuracy, durability, and how the tree looks in person. Don’t hesitate to contact the seller about UV protection, material composition, or assembly instructions.

If you’re buying for a wedding, ask if the vendor offers samples. Seeing and touching the material in person helps avoid surprises.

## Final Thoughts

Buying outdoor artificial trees doesn’t have to be overwhelming. By focusing on UV protection, location, material quality, and maintenance, you’ll find pieces that look beautiful for years.
