## The Magic of a Coastal Garden Palette

White and ivory blooms—like roses, hydrangeas, and peonies—form the base. ## Creating a Romantic, Floral-Forward Design

The design aesthetic of this wedding was classic and formal yet dreamy and floral-forward. Think lush centerpieces, cascading altar arrangements, and delicate touches on tables and chairs. To achieve this with artificial blooms, focus on texture and layering. Mix different sizes and types of **faux floral arrangements**—combine large statement flowers like garden roses with smaller filler blooms like baby's breath or waxflower.

For the ceremony backdrop, consider a freestanding arch adorned with greenery and pale blue flowers. Since **artificial flowers for wedding** don't wilt, you can build the arch days in advance. For table centerpieces, use low, lush arrangements in clear glass vases or ceramic pots. Add floating candles or scattered petals for extra romance. The key is to create depth and movement, just like the real thing.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

If you're planning a wedding with a similar coastal garden vibe, here are some actionable tips:

- **Invest in statement pieces**: Spend your budget on key items like the bridal bouquet, ceremony arch, and head table centerpiece. Use **wholesale silk flowers** for secondary decor like aisle markers or cocktail tables.
- **Mix fresh and faux**: If you love the scent of real flowers, combine fresh blooms with **faux floral arrangements** for volume and longevity. For example, use real greenery and artificial flowers.
- **Choose a cohesive palette**: Stick to 3-4 colors. White, ivory, green, and pale blue are timeless and easy to source in artificial flowers for wedding.
- **Prep early**: Faux flowers can be arranged weeks ahead. Store them in a cool, dry place and fluff them the day before the wedding.
- **Consider the venue**: For an outdoor wedding like Castle Hill Inn, ensure your arrangements are secure against wind. Use weighted bases or foam blocks.

Coastal venues come with unique challenges: salt air, wind, and fluctuating temperatures. Real flowers can wilt quickly or get damaged. **Artificial flowers for wedding** are resilient.
