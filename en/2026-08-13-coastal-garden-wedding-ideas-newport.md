When we think of a Newport summer wedding, we imagine ocean breezes, historic estates, and the effortless elegance of a coastal garden party. The Castle Hill Inn wedding captured this spirit perfectly, with a romantic and airy design that blended classic formality with a dreamy floral-forward aesthetic. If you're planning your own seaside celebration or simply love the idea of bringing that coastal charm to any venue, this real wedding is full of inspiration. Best of all, you can recreate this look with faux floral arrangements, a stunning wedding flower wall backdrop, and other affordable wedding flowers that keep your budget in check without sacrificing style.

## The Vision: A Coastal Garden Party by the Sea

The couple envisioned a celebration that felt both classic and fresh, set against the breathtaking backdrop of Narragansett Bay. The design was all about softness and airiness—think white, ivory, green, and pale blue hues that echoed the natural surroundings. The ceremony overlooked the water, and the overall vibe was that of an upscale garden party, but with a relaxed, welcoming feel. For DIY brides and planners, the takeaway is that you can achieve this look anywhere by focusing on a light color palette and incorporating natural textures like linen, wood, and plenty of greenery. A wedding flower wall backdrop can instantly transform an indoor space into a garden oasis, or frame an outdoor ceremony with a touch of romance.

## Floral Design: From Ceremony to Reception

Florals were the heart of this wedding's decor. The bridal party carried lush bouquets of white and ivory blooms, while the ceremony arch was adorned with cascading greenery and delicate flowers. At the reception, tables featured low, organic centerpieces that encouraged conversation and allowed guests to see across the room. To recreate this look with faux floral arrangements, choose high-quality silk flowers in shades of white, cream, and pale blue. Mix in eucalyptus, olive branches, and other greenery for that garden-fresh feel. Artificial flowers are a smart choice for coastal weddings because they won't wilt in the salt air or droop in the sun, and you can prepare them weeks in advance.

## Creating a Dreamy Backdrop on a Budget

One of the standout features of this wedding was the lush floral installations. But you don't need a celebrity budget to achieve that wow factor. A wedding flower wall backdrop can be made from faux blooms and greenery panels, which are reusable and cost-effective. You can build a DIY version using a wooden frame, chicken wire, or a foam base, then attach silk flowers in your chosen palette. This not only saves money but also gives you total creative control. If you're hiring a decorator, ask about faux floral options—many professionals now offer packages that include artificial installations at a fraction of the cost of real flowers. The result is a stunning focal point for your ceremony, photo booth, or head table that guests will remember.

## Practical Tips for a Coastal-Inspired Wedding

- **Choose a light color palette**: White, ivory, green, and pale blue are timeless and photograph beautifully. They also work well with faux florals, which can be color-matched easily.
- **Incorporate natural elements**: Use driftwood, seashells, or linen table runners to enhance the coastal theme without going overboard.
- **Mix real and faux**: If you love the scent of real flowers, use them for bouquets and boutonnieres, but opt for faux floral arrangements for large installations like the flower wall or hanging pieces. This balances budget and authenticity.
- **Plan for the weather**: Coastal venues can be windy or sunny. Faux flowers are sturdy and won't wilt, making them a practical choice for outdoor ceremonies.
- **Rent or DIY?** If you're handy, DIY your floral wall with affordable wedding flowers from craft stores or online wholesalers. If not, rent pre-made panels from a decor company—it's often cheaper than you'd think.

## Making It Your Own

And don't forget the details—place cards tucked into greenery, a flower crown for the flower girl, or a floral-covered arch for your vows. These touches add personality and charm without breaking the bank.
