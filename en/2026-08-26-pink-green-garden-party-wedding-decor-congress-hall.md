## A Pink and Green Garden Party Wedding at Congress Hall: Decor Inspiration

When it comes to wedding decor, few combinations feel as fresh and romantic as pink and green. The "Garden Party by the Sea" theme that this Cape May couple chose for their Spring celebration is a perfect example of how to blend natural beauty with elegant details. Whether you're planning a coastal wedding or simply love the idea of lush florals, this real wedding offers plenty of inspiration—and practical tips for bringing it to life, including how to use artificial flowers for wedding decor to achieve that same stunning look without the wilt-factor.

### Why Pink and Green Works for a Garden Party Wedding

Pink and green is a timeless palette that evokes the freshness of spring and the joy of a blooming garden. The bride herself shared, "I have always loved the color combination of pink and green florals," and it's easy to see why. Bright shades of green provide a lively backdrop, while pink adds a soft, romantic touch. Together, they create a vibe that feels both playful and polished—ideal for a venue like Congress Hall, with its historic charm and seaside setting.

For DIY brides and wedding planners, this color scheme is incredibly versatile. You can go bold with hot pink and lime green, or keep it subtle with blush and sage. The key is to balance the two so that neither overwhelms the other. Think of green as your canvas—think foliage, garlands, and greenery walls—and pink as your accents, from centerpieces to bridesmaid bouquets.

### Creating a Stunning Wedding Flower Wall Backdrop

One of the most memorable elements from this wedding was the floral backdrop. A wedding flower wall backdrop can transform any space, whether it's placed behind the head table, as a photo booth feature, or as the altar focal point. In this garden party theme, a wall covered in pink roses, peonies, and lush green leaves perfectly captured the "by the sea" elegance.

If you're on a budget or planning ahead, consider using high-quality artificial flowers for wedding backdrops. Modern faux florals look incredibly realistic and can be arranged weeks in advance. You can even build a modular flower wall with a wooden frame and zip ties, then attach silk flowers in your chosen shades. This not only saves money but also ensures your backdrop looks perfect all day—no wilting under the sun or drooping by evening.

### Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas to Steal

Congress Hall in Cape May provided a beautiful canvas, but you can recreate this look at any venue with the right wedding venue decoration ideas. Here are some practical tips inspired by this real wedding:

- **Entrance Arches:** Greet guests with a lush arch of pink and green florals. Use a mix of fresh and artificial stems to cut costs while keeping the look full.
- **Table Runners:** Instead of traditional centerpieces, line long tables with eucalyptus and scattered pink petals. Add candles for a warm glow.
- **Chair Accents:** Tie small posies of silk flowers to chairs along the aisle or at the reception. It's a simple touch that adds charm.
- **Ceiling Draping:** If your venue allows, hang greenery and pink blooms from the ceiling to create a canopy effect. This works especially well for tented receptions.

### The Smart Bride's Guide to Mixing Fresh and Faux

While fresh flowers are undeniably beautiful, they can be unpredictable and pricey. Many modern brides are turning to artificial flowers for wedding decor—not just for backdrops, but for bouquets, centerpieces, and even boutonnieres. Here are a few tips for mixing the two seamlessly:

- **Use faux for structural elements:** Walls, arches, and hanging installations are perfect for artificial flowers because they require a lot of material and need to last all day.
- **Reserve fresh for small accents:** If you love the scent of real blooms, use fresh flowers sparingly—like in the bridal bouquet or as table scatter—while using faux for the larger decor.
- **Invest in quality:** Not all silk flowers are created equal. Choose brands that offer realistic petals and stems. Touch-test them in store or read reviews online.
- **Consider the venue lighting:** Faux flowers can look stunning in natural light, but under harsh indoor lighting, they might look less realistic. Test a few stems in your venue beforehand.
