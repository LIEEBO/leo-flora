## The Power of a Cohesive Color Palette

Before you dive into decor, establish a color story that bridges both sides of your celebration. Stella and Jared used blush pink, ivory, soft white, and bold red—a palette that feels romantic and refined, yet allows for cultural pops of vibrancy. Your color choices set the tone for everything from bridesmaid dresses to table linens. For a high-fashion look, consider mixing soft neutrals with one or two saturated accent colors. These hues can be echoed in your floral arrangements, whether you’re using fresh blooms or opting for the longevity and versatility of **wholesale silk flowers**. Silk flowers come in every shade imaginable, making it easy to match exact Pantone colors without worrying about seasonal availability.

Once the ceremony ends, the party begins. For Stella and Jared, the reception was a celebration of Nigerian culture, full of energy, music, and color. Your reception decor can reflect your heritage through textiles, centerpieces, and lighting. Consider mixing traditional elements like Ankara prints or colorful beads with modern table settings. ## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

Whether you’re a DIY bride or a professional planner, these tips will help you execute a high-fashion, culturally rich wedding without the stress:

- **Start early with silk:** Order samples of **wholesale silk flowers** to check color and quality before committing. This also allows you to plan your arrangements with a clear vision.
- **Create a flower wall kit:** If you’re building a **wedding flower wall backdrop**, purchase a grid or foam base and attach silk flowers with zip ties or hot glue. Build it in sections for easy transport.
