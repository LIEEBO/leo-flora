## 3. Layering Fake Flowers Centerpiece with Greenery

When it comes to table decor, a fake flowers centerpiece can be just as stunning as fresh ones—if you know how to style it. The secret is to mix different textures and heights. Start with a base of faux greenery, like eucalyptus or ferns, then add larger blooms like roses or peonies, and finish with smaller filler flowers to create depth. Don't be afraid to mix in some of the moss mats or succulent panels as a base for your centerpiece—this adds that expensive, layered look without extra cost. And because artificial flowers don't wilt, you can prepare your centerpieces weeks in advance, saving you last-minute stress.

## Practical Tips for Styling Your Wedding with Artificial Flowers

- **Mix high and low:** Combine statement pieces like a moss wall with smaller accents like succulent panels to create visual interest.
- **Think about scale:** In large venues, use bigger panels and taller centerpieces to fill the space; in intimate settings, smaller pieces work better.
- **Lighting matters:** Artificial flowers look best in warm, soft lighting. Use fairy lights or candles to enhance the texture and color.
- **Reuse and repurpose:** After the wedding, your artificial flowers can be used to decorate your home or given to guests as favors—just another reason they're a smart investment.
