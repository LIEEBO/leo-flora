**Introduction**

You’re standing in the parking lot, pen in hand, tie or pantyhose in a twist, trying to come up with a warm and meaningful message for the happy couple’s wedding card. We’ve all been there. Whether you’re a last-minute scribbler or a thoughtful planner, finding the right words can feel as tricky as choosing the perfect centerpiece. But don’t worry—just like affordable wedding flowers can elevate a reception, a few heartfelt sentences can elevate your card. Below, we’ve gathered wedding wishes, writing tips, and inspiration to help you craft a message that’s as beautiful as the faux floral arrangements on the head table.

## Basic Wedding Wishes That Work for Anyone

If you’re short on time or don’t know the couple super well, stick with classic congratulations. These are the go-to messages that feel supportive and warm, no matter the relationship.

- “Heartfelt congratulations on your wedding day!”
- “Warmest wishes and love to you both.”
- “Congratulations! May your life together be filled with joy.”
- “So happy for you both—enjoy every moment!”
- “Wishing you a lifetime of love and happiness.”

These simple wishes pair perfectly with a bouquet of wholesale silk flowers—they’re timeless, elegant, and never wilt.

## Personalize Your Message Based on Your Relationship

A generic “congratulations” is fine, but adding a personal touch makes your card memorable. Think about your connection to the couple and let that guide your words.

**For close friends:**
- “You two are the best thing that ever happened to each other. Love you both!”
- “So many happy memories with you—now here’s to making new ones as a married couple!”
- “You guys are proof that soulmates exist. Congratulations!”

**For family members:**
- “Welcome to the family! We’re so thrilled to have you.”
- “Watching you grow up and find your person has been a joy. All my love.”
- “Now you’re officially stuck with us—and we couldn’t be happier!”

**For colleagues or acquaintances:**
- “Congratulations on your wedding! Wishing you both a bright future.”
- “So glad to celebrate this special day with you. Best wishes!”

When you’re stuck, think of a shared memory or a quality you admire in the couple—it’s like adding a personal flower to a faux floral arrangement: it makes the whole thing unique.

## When You Can’t Attend the Wedding

If you can’t make it to the ceremony, your card can still convey your love and regret. Here are a few ideas:

- “Wishing I could be there to celebrate with you. Sending all my love from afar.”
- “So sorry to miss your big day. Congratulations and best wishes for a wonderful marriage!”
- “I’ll be raising a glass to you from [location]. Cheers to your future together!”

Pair your card with a small gift like a vase of affordable wedding flowers delivered to their home—it’s a thoughtful gesture that says you care even from a distance.

## Quick Tips for Writing a Memorable Wedding Card

- **Start with a warm greeting:** “Dear [names]” or “To the happy couple.”
- **Be specific:** Mention something you love about them as a couple.
- **Keep it positive:** Avoid mentioning the stress of planning or any past relationships.
- **End with a blessing:** “Wishing you a lifetime of laughter and love.”
- **Sign with warmth:** “With all my love,” “Yours truly,” or “Hugs and kisses.”

If you’re still stuck, remember that the couple will treasure your words more than the perfect phrasing. Just like choosing wholesale silk flowers for your own wedding, it’s the thought that counts.
