## Practical Tips for Planning a Winery Proposal

If you're planning a winery proposal—whether in Kelowna or elsewhere—here are some actionable tips:

- **Choose a private spot**: Ask the winery about secluded areas, like a hillside overlook or a vine-lined patio, to ensure privacy.
- **Incorporate affordable wedding flowers**: You don't need to splurge on exotic blooms. Mixing **affordable wedding flowers** like roses, baby's breath, and eucalyptus can create a high-end look on a budget.
- **Use realistic artificial flowers for setup ease**: Artificial blooms can be arranged ahead of time, ensuring a flawless setup without last-minute scrambling.
- **Consider lighting**: String lights or lanterns add warmth and help photos pop as the sun dips below the horizon.
- **Plan for weather**: Have a backup indoor location in case of rain, and use weighted vases to keep arrangements stable in breezy conditions.
