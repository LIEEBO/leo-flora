## Practical Tips for Styling Your Wreath

Once you've picked your perfect wreath, here are a few tips to make it shine:

- **Don't limit it to the front door:** Hang wreaths on interior doors, above the mantel, or even on a kitchen cabinet with a ribbon.
- **Layer your decor:** Place your wreath on a mirror or a window to add depth and dimension to your space.
- **Mix and match:** Buy two smaller wreaths and hang them together for a symmetrical look, or pair a wreath with a matching garland to create a cohesive flow.
- **Consider the scale:** For a large, grand door, go for a 26-inch wreath. For smaller spaces or windows, an 18 or 20-inch size is perfect.
