When planning a wedding with multiple floral settings—ceremony arch, aisle, reception tables, and more—cost can escalate quickly. **Wholesale silk flowers** are a game-changer. 1. **Plan your color shift**: Like the Bastide du Roy wedding, decide on two palettes—one for ceremony, one for reception. Use **wholesale silk flowers** to create both sets of arrangements.
2. **Invest in a high-quality bridal bouquet**: **Bridal bouquet artificial flowers** are worth the splurge. Look for handcrafted options with realistic stems and mixed textures.
