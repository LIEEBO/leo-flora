# COVID Weddings: Smart Tips for Planning During a Pandemic

The global pandemic has changed the way we celebrate love. Weddings have been postponed, resized, and reimagined. As an event planner, I've navigated these uncharted waters firsthand—and I'm here to share practical advice to help you plan a beautiful, safe, and stress-free wedding, even during uncertain times.

## 1. Read Your Contracts Carefully

One of the biggest lessons from COVID-19 is the importance of reading every line of your vendor contracts. Many contracts are written to protect the vendor if you cancel, but rarely do they protect you in case of an emergency like a pandemic. Before signing, ask for a clause that covers postponements or cancellations due to force majeure. This simple step can save you thousands of dollars and endless headaches.

## 2. Get Your Marriage License—Virtually

You can still get married! Many counties now offer online or curbside marriage license applications. Check your local county website for updated procedures. If you can't meet a judge in person, consider having a friend or family member get ordained online to officiate. This adds a personal touch and ensures your ceremony happens, no matter the restrictions.

## 3. Embrace Smaller, Intimate Weddings

With limits on gathering sizes, many couples are opting for micro-weddings with just a handful of guests. This doesn't mean you have to sacrifice style. In fact, smaller weddings allow you to splurge on details that matter most. For example, you can invest in stunning faux floral arrangements that look just as beautiful as fresh flowers but last forever. Artificial flowers for wedding decor are a smart choice—they're budget-friendly, allergy-free, and can be prepared well in advance, reducing last-minute stress.

## 4. Choose Affordable Wedding Flowers That Wow

Speaking of flowers, one of the best ways to save money without compromising beauty is to choose affordable wedding flowers. Silk and high-quality faux blooms have come a long way. They photograph beautifully, can be arranged to match your exact color palette, and won't wilt in the heat or after hours of dancing. Plus, you can repurpose them as home decor after the big day—a lovely keepsake.

## Practical Tips for a COVID-Safe Celebration

- **Have a backup plan**: Whether it's a live-streamed ceremony or a rain date, prepare for the unexpected.
- **Communicate clearly with vendors**: Confirm their COVID policies and what happens if restrictions change.
- **Keep guest count flexible**: Send out save-the-dates early, but be ready to adjust numbers as your date approaches.
- **Consider outdoor venues**: They offer more space for social distancing and reduce the risk of transmission.

## Conclusion

We will get through this—and we can learn valuable lessons that make our weddings and businesses stronger. By reading contracts carefully, embracing intimate gatherings, and choosing versatile decor like faux floral arrangements, you can create a wedding that's both beautiful and resilient. Remember, the most important thing is the love you're celebrating. With a little flexibility and creativity, your special day will be unforgettable for all the right reasons.
