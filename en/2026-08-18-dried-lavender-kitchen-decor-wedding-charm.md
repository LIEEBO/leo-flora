## Why Dried Lavender Works in Any Space

'Dried lavender can be used across the entire home, and we often see it hung up in places such as the bathroom or on doors. However, what most people don't automatically think of is using it in the kitchen. If you have a rail above the worktop, or at the end of your cabinet or island, then lavender, along with other dried herbs, is great at adding scent as well as visual interest to the space,' commented Ntola Obazee, creative director for Kutchenhaus.

'It creates texture and provides a gentle scent, which is great for covering old food smells. It also has the added benefit of being a great low-maintenance alternative to flowers, which can be enjoyed for months to come.'

## Practical Tips for Using Dried Herbs and Faux Florals

Here are some practical ways to get this look for your wedding or event:

1.  **Hanging Installations:** Suspend bunches of dried lavender from wooden beams or shepherd's hooks to create a canopy effect. Mix in **artificial flowers for wedding** garlands to add volume and color.

2.  **Table Runners:** Instead of traditional floral runners, lay a line of dried lavender down the center of your tables. Intersperse with silk flowers and candles for a romantic, textured look that is also budget-friendly.

3.  **Budget-Friendly Bouquets:** For bridesmaids, consider using **affordable wedding flowers** like silk roses or peonies mixed with dried lavender stems. This not only cuts costs but also provides a unique, sensory experience for the wedding party.

4.  **Place Settings:** Tuck a small sprig of lavender into each napkin, paired with a single silk flower. This small detail adds a pop of color and a lovely scent that guests will remember.

5.  **Long-Lasting Favors:** Give guests a small bundle of dried lavender and a silk flower as a favor. It is a beautiful reminder of the day that will not die or fade, much like the memories of your celebration.
