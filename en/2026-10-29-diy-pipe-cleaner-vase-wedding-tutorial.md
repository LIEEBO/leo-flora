# How to Make a Pipe Cleaner Vase: Easy Woven Tutorial

If you're planning a wedding and looking for a creative, budget-friendly DIY project, a pipe cleaner vase is a charming addition to your decor. Not only is it easy to make, but it also adds a personal touch to your special day. In this tutorial, we'll guide you through creating a sturdy woven vase using pipe cleaners and a cardboard form. This versatile piece can be used as a standalone decoration or as part of a larger display, such as a wedding flower wall backdrop or a fake flowers centerpiece.

## Materials You'll Need

Before we dive into the steps, gather the following materials:

- Pipe cleaners (also known as chenille stems) in your chosen colors
- A cardboard tube (e.g., from a paper towel roll) or a cylindrical container as a form
- Scissors
- Glue (optional, for securing ends)
- Ruler (optional, for measuring)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Prepare Your Form

Start by selecting a cylindrical form that will give your vase its shape. A cardboard tube works well, but you can also use a plastic bottle or a jar. The size of the form will determine the size of your vase. For a standard vase, a paper towel roll is ideal.

### 2. Create Vertical Ribs

Cut pipe cleaners to a length that is slightly longer than the height of your form. These will serve as the vertical ribs of your vase. You'll need an even number of ribs, so count them as you go. For a small vase, 12-16 ribs are sufficient. Attach them to the top and bottom of the form using glue or by bending the ends to secure them. Ensure they are evenly spaced.

### 3. Start Weaving

Now comes the fun part: weaving! Take a pipe cleaner and secure one end to a vertical rib near the bottom. Then, begin weaving over and under the ribs, working your way around the vase. When you reach the starting point, twist the end of the pipe cleaner to the adjacent rib to secure it. Continue weaving with new pipe cleaners, pushing them down tightly against the previous row to create a dense, sturdy weave.

### 4. Finish and Secure

Once you've woven to the top, secure the final end by twisting it around a rib. If desired, add a rim by wrapping a pipe cleaner around the top edge. Allow any glue to dry completely.

## Creative Variations

- **Color Schemes:** Use pipe cleaners in colors that match your wedding theme. For a romantic look, try pastel shades; for a modern vibe, opt for metallic or bold colors.
- **Patterns:** Create stripes or chevron patterns by alternating colors in your weave.
- **Embellishments:** Add small faux flowers or ribbons to the vase for extra flair.

## Troubleshooting Tips

- **Weave Too Loose?** Push the rows closer together as you go. If needed, use a bit of glue to hold the pipe cleaners in place.
- **Vase Wobbly?** Ensure the vertical ribs are securely attached and evenly spaced. You can also add a base by weaving a flat circle of pipe cleaners at the bottom.
- **Pipe Cleaners Unraveling?** Twist the ends tightly and secure with a dab of glue.
