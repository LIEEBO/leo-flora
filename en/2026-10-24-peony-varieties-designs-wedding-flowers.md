# A Comprehensive Guide to Peony Varieties and Designs

Peonies are beloved for their lush blooms, captivating fragrance, and rich symbolism. They are a favorite in wedding floral design, but their short season and high cost can be challenging. This guide explores peony varieties and design ideas, with practical tips for incorporating them into your wedding using affordable alternatives like silk flowers.

## Why Peonies Are a Wedding Favorite

Peonies symbolize romance, prosperity, and good fortune, making them ideal for weddings. Their full, elegant blooms add sophistication to any arrangement. However, their brief blooming season (late spring to early summer) and premium price can be limiting. Many couples associate peonies with cherished memories, adding emotional value. ## Types of Peonies for Floral Designs

Understanding peony varieties helps you choose the right look for your wedding.

### Herbaceous Peonies
These are the most common, with bowl-shaped flowers in single, semi-double, or double forms. They die back in winter and re-sprout in spring. Available in soft pastels to vibrant hues, they suit bouquets and centerpieces. For DIY brides, herbaceous peonies are versatile and easy to work with.

### Tree Peonies
Tree peonies have woody stems and larger, more ruffled blooms. They bloom earlier than herbaceous types and offer a dramatic presence. They are excellent for statement arrangements but can be costly and delicate. Artificial tree peonies capture their grandeur without the fragility.

### Intersectional Peonies (Itoh Peonies)
A hybrid of herbaceous and tree peonies, intersectional peonies combine the best of both: large blooms, strong stems, and a longer bloom period. They are prized for their unique colors and disease resistance. In artificial form, they provide lasting beauty for wedding decor.
