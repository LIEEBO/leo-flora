## Practical Tips for Styling and Caring for Your Faux Lavender

To get the most out of your artificial lavender arrangement, here are a few tips:

- **Fluff and shape:** When you first unpack it, gently fluff the stems and leaves to give it a natural, full look.
- **Dust regularly:** Use a soft cloth or a gentle vacuum attachment to keep it looking fresh. A quick dusting once a month is all it needs.
- **Mix and match:** Combine it with other faux flowers or greenery to create a custom centerpiece or bouquet.
- **Use in unexpected places:** Think beyond the table—hang it on a wall, place it on a shelf, or use it as a focal point in a photo booth.

With just a little care, your arrangement will stay beautiful for years, making it a smart investment for both home and event use.
