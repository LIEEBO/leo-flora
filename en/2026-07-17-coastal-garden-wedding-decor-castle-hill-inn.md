One of the best ways to capture the spirit of this Newport wedding is by focusing on the floral details. The ceremony arch, for instance, can be a focal point. ## Practical Tips for a Floral-Forward Wedding

- **Choose a cohesive color palette:** Stick to 3-4 colors that complement your venue. White, ivory, green, and pale blue are foolproof for coastal settings.
- **Invest in a statement backdrop:** A wedding flower wall backdrop not only frames your ceremony but also serves as a stunning photo booth for guests.
- **Mix textures:** Combine different types of faux blooms—roses, peonies, eucalyptus, and ferns—to add depth and realism.
- **Consider the environment:** Outdoor venues like Castle Hill Inn benefit from realistic artificial flowers that withstand wind, sun, and humidity.
- **Plan for versatility:** Use the same floral pieces for both ceremony and reception to maximize your budget.
