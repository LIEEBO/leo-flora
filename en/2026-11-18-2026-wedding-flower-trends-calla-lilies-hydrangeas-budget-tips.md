# 2026 Wedding Flower Trends: Calla Lilies, Hydrangeas & Budget Tips

Wedding flowers are always a hot topic, and 2026 is no exception. This year, we're seeing a beautiful resurgence of calla lilies and hydrangeas in modern wedding design. But alongside these gorgeous trends, couples are facing rising floral costs and shortages. If you're a DIY bride or a wedding planner looking for smart solutions, this article is for you. We'll explore the top trends, share practical tips, and show how artificial flowers can help you achieve the look you want without breaking the bank.

## The Return of Calla Lilies and Hydrangeas

Calla lilies are back in a big way. Their sleek, sculptural shape makes them perfect for minimalist and modern weddings. They add a touch of elegance to bridal bouquets, ceremony arches, and table arrangements. Hydrangeas, on the other hand, bring lush volume and a romantic feel. They're ideal for creating full, abundant centerpieces and backdrop installations. Both flowers are highly requested for 2026 weddings, but they can be expensive and subject to seasonal availability.

## Rising Costs and Floral Shortages: What You Need to Know

If you've started planning your wedding flowers, you've probably noticed that prices are higher than ever. Factors like climate change, supply chain issues, and increased demand have led to floral shortages and inflated costs. Many couples are finding that their dream flower list doesn't fit their budget. This is where artificial flowers come to the rescue. High-quality faux florals look incredibly realistic and offer a cost-effective, reliable alternative. You can have calla lilies and hydrangeas in your wedding, regardless of the season or market conditions.
