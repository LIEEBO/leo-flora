## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

1. **Choose a cohesive color story.** Stick to 2-3 main hues for your floating flowers to keep the look elegant and intentional.
2. **Invest in quality faux stems.** Not all artificial flowers are equal. Look for realistic textures and subtle color variations.
3. **Use lightweight bases.** For overhead installations, opt for foam or wire frames covered with greenery to reduce weight.
4. **Mix fresh and faux strategically.** Use fresh blooms for small, close-up details (like the cake topper) and **bridal bouquet artificial flowers** for the main events.
5. **Order early.** **Wholesale silk flowers** often require lead time for custom colors or large quantities.
