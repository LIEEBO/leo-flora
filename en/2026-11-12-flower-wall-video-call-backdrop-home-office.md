## How Many Panels Do You Need?

You don't need a full wall. Your camera usually only sees the area directly behind your head and shoulders. Our panels are 60cm x 40cm, so:

- **4 panels** (2 across, 2 high) cover around 1.2m x 0.8m, enough for a tight camera frame.
- **6 panels** (2 across, 3 high) cover around 1.2m x 1.2m, our recommended size for most desks.
- **9 panels or more** create a full feature behind a wider desk or for standing presentations.

For wedding planners who want to use the backdrop for multiple purposes (e.g., photo booth or ceremony backdrop), consider a larger size like 12 panels (3m x 1.6m) to create a more impressive **wedding flower wall backdrop**.

## Choosing a Colour for Video

- **Green foliage:** Calm, natural, and suits almost any profession. Try the Green Foliage with Whites & Wisteria or Green Tropical.
- **Ivory:** Bright and clean, and bounces light onto your face. See the Ivory Foliage.

Avoid matching your clothes, so you don't blend into the background. For wedding planners, consider a color scheme that aligns with your brand or the weddings you plan. A **fake flowers centerpiece** can also be coordinated with your backdrop for a cohesive look.
