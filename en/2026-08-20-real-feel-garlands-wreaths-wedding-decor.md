# Real Feel Garlands & Wreaths: The Secret to Effortless Wedding Elegance

When planning a wedding, every detail counts—from the ceremony backdrop to the reception tables. Couples and planners alike are constantly seeking decor that looks breathtaking yet fits the budget and timeline. That's where **realistic artificial flowers** come in. Real feel garlands and wreaths (also known as real touch, PE, or true-to-life) have taken the event world by storm, and for good reason. They offer the lush, romantic look of fresh greenery without the worry of wilting or last-minute replacements.

Whether you're dreaming of a rustic barn wedding or a chic ballroom affair, these versatile pieces can transform any space. In this guide, we'll explore why real feel garlands and wreaths are a must-have for weddings and how to incorporate them into your decor.

## Why Choose Real Feel Garlands and Wreaths for Your Wedding?

Fresh greenery is beautiful, but it comes with challenges: it needs water, can wilt in heat, and often requires a florist to install it just hours before the event. Real feel options eliminate all that stress. Here’s why they’re a favorite among DIY brides and professional planners:

- **Lifelike Appearance**: Made with high-quality PE (polyethylene) materials, these garlands mimic the texture and color of real pine, cedar, and eucalyptus. Guests won’t be able to tell the difference—even up close.
- **Durability**: Unlike fresh foliage, real feel garlands won’t dry out or shed. You can hang them days in advance and they’ll look perfect from the first dance to the last toast.
- **Allergy-Friendly**: For couples or guests with pollen sensitivities, faux greenery offers the beauty without the sneezing. This is a huge plus for outdoor ceremonies where allergens are abundant.
- **Cost-Effective**: Fresh garlands can be pricey, especially for large venues. Real feel options are a one-time investment that can be reused for bridal showers, anniversaries, or even holiday parties.
- **Easy to Store**: After the big day, simply pack them away. No need to toss them in the compost—they’re ready for your next event.

## How to Use Garlands and Wreaths in Wedding Decor

The versatility of real feel garlands and wreaths is unmatched. Here are three inspiring ways to incorporate them into your wedding:

### Ceremony Backdrops and Arches

Create a stunning **wedding flower wall backdrop** using a mix of garlands draped over a wooden arch or a freestanding frame. Combine pine and cedar garlands with faux blooms like roses or peonies for a lush, romantic look. The best part? You can assemble this backdrop days before the wedding, saving you from last-minute panic.

### Table Runners and Centerpieces

Garlands make gorgeous table runners. Lay them down the center of long banquet tables, weaving in candles, fairy lights, or small bud vases. For round tables, create a wreath as a base for a centerpiece—place a lantern, a cluster of candles, or a vase of flowers in the middle. This adds depth and texture without overwhelming the table.

### Aisle and Entrance Decor

Line the aisle with small wreaths attached to chairs or shepherd’s hooks. At the entrance to your ceremony or reception, hang a larger wreath on the door or a welcome sign. This sets the tone for your theme and gives guests a warm, inviting first impression.

## Practical Tips for Choosing and Styling Real Feel Garlands

When shopping for real feel garlands and wreaths, keep these tips in mind:

- **Length and Size**: Measure your space first. Garlands come in lengths from 5 to 15 feet; wreaths range from 24 to 36 inches. For a grand backdrop, you’ll likely need multiple garlands—plan to overlap them for fullness.
- **Mix and Match**: Don’t be afraid to combine pine and cedar, or opt for a mixed garland. This adds visual interest and mimics how natural greenery grows.
- **Lighting Options**: Choose lit or unlit versions. Lit garlands are perfect for evening receptions—they add a warm, magical glow. If you prefer to use your own string lights, go unlit and DIY.
- **Shape Matters**: Wreaths come in round or oval shapes. Round is classic, while oval gives a more modern, elongated look—great for above a fireplace or on a rectangular arch.
- **Accessorize**: Add faux berries, pinecones, or small flowers to match your wedding palette. These little touches personalize the decor and make it uniquely yours.

## The Budget-Friendly Advantage: Affordable Wedding Flowers

Weddings are expensive, and flowers are often one of the biggest line items. Real feel garlands and wreaths offer a way to achieve that high-end floral look without breaking the bank. They qualify as **affordable wedding flowers** because they’re reusable and require no refrigeration or special care. You can even sell them after the wedding to recoup some costs—or keep them for your own home.

Imagine walking down the aisle lined with lush greenery, exchanging vows under a flower wall backdrop, and dancing the night away under garlands strung with fairy lights. All of this is possible within your budget, and with less stress.

From ceremony arches to table runners, they bring warmth and elegance to every corner of your celebration.
