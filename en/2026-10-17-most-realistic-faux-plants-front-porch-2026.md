## Practical Tips for Decorating with Faux Plants

- **Mix textures and heights:** Combine tall trees with low-lying succulents and hanging vines to create depth.
- **Use real-looking containers:** Choose pots that look like terracotta, stone, or metal to enhance realism.
- **Rotate seasonally:** Swap out plants for different seasons to keep your porch fresh.
- **Add lighting:** String lights or lanterns can highlight your faux plants and create a cozy evening ambiance.
- **Keep them clean:** Dust and rinse your faux plants periodically to maintain their appearance.
