When tradition meets modern design, the result can be truly breathtaking. That’s exactly what happened at Nhu and Long’s multi-day wedding in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. The jaw-dropping ceremony and reception featured flowers floating overhead, creating a dreamy, romantic atmosphere. If you’re a DIY bride or wedding planner looking for fresh wedding venue decoration ideas, this real wedding is packed with inspiration—and we’ll show you how to recreate the look with faux floral arrangements.

## Why Floating Flowers Steal the Show

One of the most striking elements of this wedding was the canopy of blooms suspended above the guests. Instead of traditional centerpieces, the couple chose to hang flowers from the ceiling, creating a sense of intimacy and wonder. This approach works beautifully for both indoor and outdoor venues. If you’re planning a wedding on a budget or want to reduce stress, consider using faux floral arrangements for these overhead installations. They won’t wilt, and you can assemble them weeks in advance. Plus, they’re reusable for future events or as home décor.

## How to Create a Wedding Flower Wall Backdrop

Another standout feature was the floral backdrop used for the ceremony and photo booth. A wedding flower wall backdrop is a versatile piece that can transform any space. For this Vietnamese wedding, the wall was lush with greenery and soft pastels. You can DIY your own by attaching artificial flowers to a lightweight frame or foam board. Use a mix of silk roses, hydrangeas, and eucalyptus to mimic the real thing. The best part? No watering or wilting worries. This is a perfect project for DIY brides who want a stunning focal point without the floral price tag.

## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas for Multi-Day Celebrations

Multi-day weddings require versatile décor that transitions from day to night. Here are some practical tips inspired by Nhu and Long’s event:

- **Layered lighting:** String lights, lanterns, and candles add warmth. Combine with faux floral garlands for a romantic glow.
- **Mix textures:** Combine silk flowers with real greenery for a natural look that lasts. Faux floral arrangements can be mixed with fresh accents for the best of both worlds.
- **Repurpose décor:** Use the same flower wall for the ceremony and reception. Move overhead installations from the dinner area to the dance floor later.
- **Color consistency:** Choose a palette that works for both daytime and evening. Pastels and neutrals are safe bets.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

Whether you’re planning your own wedding or helping a client, here’s how to incorporate these ideas without breaking the bank:

- **Start with a focal point:** Pick one or two big-impact elements, like a wedding flower wall backdrop or an overhead floral installation. Focus your budget there.
- **Go faux for high-up décor:** Overhead flowers are hard to water and maintain. Faux floral arrangements are lightweight, easy to hang, and look identical from a distance.
- **Test your setup:** If you’re using a venue with high ceilings, do a trial run. Make sure your hanging flowers won’t interfere with lighting or sightlines.
- **Involve your team:** Enlist friends or family for assembly day. Faux flowers can be prepped in advance, so you can enjoy the process together.
