## What Are Real Touch Wedding Flowers?

Real Touch flowers are high-quality faux florals designed to look and feel incredibly realistic. Unlike traditional silk flowers, modern Real Touch varieties have natural texture and movement, photograph beautifully, and mimic real blooms almost perfectly. Many brides are surprised they can’t tell the difference — especially in photos. These flowers are perfect for creating lush, luxurious arrangements that stay fresh-looking from ceremony to reception.

## Fresh Wedding Flowers: The Classic Choice

Fresh flowers have always been the traditional option for weddings. They offer natural fragrance, organic one-of-a-kind arrangements, and seasonal beauty. However, they come with limitations — especially in a place like the Okanagan, where heat, weather, and sourcing can affect quality and pricing. Fresh flowers are sensitive to temperature, must be prepared within days of the wedding, and are typically single-use. For outdoor weddings, they can wilt quickly, adding stress to your day.

## Real Touch vs Fresh: Pros and Cons

### Fresh Flowers
- **Best for:** Traditional weddings, couples who love natural fragrance, highly seasonal designs.
- **Considerations:** Sensitive to heat and weather, must be prepared within days, single-use.

### Real Touch Flowers (Pickadilly’s Specialty)
- **Best for:** Luxury designs on a realistic budget, outdoor weddings (heat-safe), brides who want consistency and peace of mind.
- **Benefits:** No wilting, no weather stress, you know exactly what your flowers will look like ahead of time, reusable and more sustainable. Ideal for large installations like floral pillars, arches, and aisle flowers.

## Practical Tips for Choosing Your Wedding Flowers

1. **Consider your venue and season.** If you’re having an outdoor summer wedding in the Okanagan, Real Touch flowers are a heat-safe choice that won’t droop.
2. **Think about your budget priorities.** If you want a grand floral installation but need to save elsewhere, mix fresh blooms for personal flowers and use silk flowers wedding decor for arches and centrepieces.
3. **Ask about rental options.** Many florists now offer Real Touch rentals, which can dramatically reduce costs. You can also buy wholesale silk flowers to create your own arrangements, perfect for DIY brides.
4. **Don’t forget about the photos.** Real Touch flowers photograph beautifully, so you won’t sacrifice visual impact.
5. **Check reusability.** After the wedding, you can repurpose Real Touch arrangements as home decor or sell them — something you can’t do with fresh flowers.
