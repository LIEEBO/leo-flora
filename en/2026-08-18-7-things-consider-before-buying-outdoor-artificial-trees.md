# 7 Things to Consider Before Buying Outdoor Artificial Trees

Imagine this: you’ve just finished setting up your outdoor wedding venue. The sun is shining, the guests are arriving, and you’ve placed beautiful faux trees along the aisle. But by the end of the season, those same trees look faded, brittle, and anything but fresh. Sound familiar?

Buying outdoor artificial trees can be as simple as jumping online for 10 minutes and grabbing the first ones you like—but you may end up with regrets. Choosing the right artificial trees requires a little more forethought if you want long-lasting pieces that look good day after day, year after year. Whether you’re decorating a backyard wedding, a corporate event, or a permanent garden space, these seven considerations will help you make a smart investment.

## 1. UV Protection and Weather Resistance Are Non-Negotiable

The biggest mistake people make? Not checking for UV protection or whether the materials can handle the outdoors. Not all artificial trees are suitable for outside use—yes, they’re artificial, but that doesn’t mean they’ll withstand rain, wind, or harsh sunlight.

Always look for two things on any product page:

- A clear statement that the tree is suitable for outdoor use
- UV protection or UV-resistant materials

Without these, one sunny summer or one winter will leave your tree looking like a forgotten artificial plant in a graveyard rather than a thriving focal point. Manufacturers that label their products for outdoor use carefully select materials that survive changing weather. Silk, for example, is a popular choice for indoor artificial plants, but it doesn’t hold up well outside.

A UV-protected tree has a special coating or additive that shields pigments from fading. While all artificial items fade over time, UV protection extends the life significantly. You can also buy UV protection sprays and apply them annually to keep your outdoor artificial plants vibrant.

For wedding planners and DIY brides, this is especially critical if you plan to reuse decor across multiple events. Investing in UV-resistant pieces means your bridal bouquet artificial flowers and trees will look fresh for every celebration—not just the first one.

## 2. Match the Tree to Your Location

Now that you know the basics, it’s time to think about where your trees will live. You can buy first and find a place later—and sometimes that works—but it’s best to have a vision and find the plants to match. Artificial trees don’t change over time, so you can bring your vision to life immediately without waiting for growth or worrying about a plant outgrowing its spot.

There really are no strict rules when it comes to location. You can even plant them in the ground in many circumstances. But go in with a plan. Browse Instagram and Pinterest for inspiration, and consider how the tree’s height, width, and overall silhouette will complement your space.

For a wedding ceremony outdoors, tall, elegant trees can frame the altar beautifully. For a cozy backyard reception, smaller trees in decorative pots add warmth without overwhelming the setting. Think about sightlines, guest flow, and photo backdrops—these are the details that make your decor memorable.

## 3. Quality Matters: Look for Realistic Details

Not all faux trees are created equal. When you’re buying for a special occasion, quality is everything. Look for trees with natural-looking trunks, varied leaf shades, and realistic textures. Cheap options often have shiny leaves, visible seams, or unnatural symmetry—all of which can ruin the illusion.

Read customer reviews and look for close-up photos. High-quality artificial trees often use polyethylene (PE) or polyblend materials that mimic real bark and foliage beautifully. These may cost more upfront, but they pay off in longevity and visual appeal.

If you’re a wedding planner sourcing wholesale silk flowers and trees for multiple events, investing in premium pieces means you can rent or reuse them, offering clients affordable wedding flowers without sacrificing style.

## 4. Size, Scale, and Proportion

One of the most common mistakes is misjudging size. A tree that looks perfect in a product photo might be far too small (or too large) for your venue. Always measure your space carefully and visualize the tree in context.

Consider the tree’s height from the base to the top, the spread of the branches, and the pot or planter size. A good rule of thumb: outdoor trees should feel proportional to the surrounding architecture and landscaping. For weddings, taller trees create a dramatic effect, while shorter ones work better as table centerpieces or aisle markers.

## 5. Maintenance and Storage

While artificial trees require less upkeep than live ones, they still need some care. Dust and pollen can accumulate, especially outdoors. A gentle rinse with water or a wipe with a damp cloth can keep them looking fresh. Check for loose branches or fading annually, and reapply UV protection spray as needed.

Storage is another consideration. If you’re using the trees for a single event, you’ll need a plan for after. Many artificial trees can be disassembled or compressed for storage, but some are bulky. Make sure you have space to keep them safe between uses.

## 6. Budget and Long-Term Value

Artificial trees are an investment, but they’re often more cost-effective than replacing live trees seasonally. Compare prices across retailers, and don’t forget to factor in shipping costs. For wedding professionals, buying wholesale silk flowers and trees in bulk can significantly reduce per-unit costs.

Consider how often you’ll use the trees. If it’s a one-time event, renting might be more economical. But if you plan to use them for multiple weddings, corporate events, or seasonal decor, buying is the smarter choice. High-quality artificial trees can last for years with proper care.

## 7. Aesthetic Versatility

Finally, think about how the trees fit into your overall design theme. Do they match your color palette? Can they be dressed up with fairy lights, ribbons, or floral accents? Versatile trees can adapt to different styles—from rustic garden weddings to modern minimalist events.

Some trees come with removable leaves or interchangeable pots, giving you even more flexibility. Don’t be afraid to mix and match with other artificial elements like bridal bouquet artificial flowers or hanging greenery to create a cohesive look.

## Final Thoughts

Buying outdoor artificial trees doesn’t have to be overwhelming. By focusing on UV protection, location, quality, size, maintenance, budget, and versatility, you can find pieces that elevate your space and stand the test of time. Whether you’re a DIY bride planning your dream wedding or a professional event decorator, these tips will help you make a confident purchase.

Remember: the right artificial trees are more than just decor—they’re an investment in beauty and practicality. With a little planning, you’ll enjoy lush, realistic greenery for years to come.

Happy decorating!
