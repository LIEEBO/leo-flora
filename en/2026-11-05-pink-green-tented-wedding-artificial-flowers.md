# Pink & Green Tented Wedding: Artificial Flower Decor Ideas

When Olivia and her partner decided to host their wedding on her family land in Waterford, they envisioned a celebration that felt both personal and immersive. With the help of A Charming Fête, they transformed the outdoor space into a tented wonderland of layered patterns and bold colors. The result? A pink and green wedding that feels fresh, fun, and utterly romantic. In this article, we’ll break down the key decor elements and show you how to recreate the look with artificial flowers for wedding—no wilting, no stress, just lasting beauty.

## The Magic of Layered Pattern and Custom Upholstery

One of the standout features of this wedding was the use of custom upholstery. Banquette seating was wrapped in a bespoke fabric, and the same green pattern was carried over to a circular pink bar that wrapped around a tree in the cocktail tent. This cohesive approach to pattern and color created a sense of unity and surprise around every corner.

For DIY brides and planners, the takeaway is clear: don’t be afraid to mix patterns and carry a motif throughout your venue. Consider using fabric backdrops, custom cushions, or even patterned linens to tie your wedding venue decoration ideas together. And when it comes to florals, faux floral arrangements can echo the same palette without the need for constant watering or refrigeration.

## Whimsical Fabric Chandeliers and Floral Accents

Scalloped pink fabric chandeliers floated above the tables, adding a soft, romantic texture to the tent. These fabric installations are a great way to add volume and color without overwhelming the space. Pair them with hanging faux floral arrangements—think trailing greenery and pink blooms—to create a dreamy canopy.

Artificial flowers for wedding are perfect for hanging installations because they’re lightweight and won’t shed petals on your guests. You can use silk wisteria, faux roses, or even artificial cherry blossoms to achieve the look. ## Bringing the Outdoors In with Faux Florals

Since the wedding took place on family land, the couple wanted to bring the natural surroundings into the tent. They used lush greenery and pink blooms to soften the structure and create a garden-like atmosphere. But instead of relying solely on fresh flowers, you can achieve the same effect with faux floral arrangements.

Faux flowers have come a long way in recent years. High-quality artificial flowers look incredibly realistic and can be reused for multiple events or even as home decor after the wedding. They’re also a smart choice for outdoor weddings where heat or wind might damage fresh blooms. Plus, you can prep them days in advance without worrying about wilting.

## Practical Tips for Recreating This Look

1. **Choose a bold color palette**: Pink and green is a classic combination that feels both vibrant and elegant. Use different shades of each color to add depth.
2. **Incorporate custom fabric elements**: Whether it’s upholstered seating or draped fabric, textiles add warmth and texture. Look for patterned fabrics that can be repeated in small doses.
3. **Mix real and faux**: Use artificial flowers for large installations and high-traffic areas, and supplement with fresh flowers for table centerpieces if desired.
4. **Lighting matters**: Add string lights or lanterns to enhance the ambiance, especially in a tent.
5. **Source smartly**: When buying faux florals, choose a reputable supplier. leofaux offers a variety of artificial flowers that are perfect for wedding decor, from roses to greenery.
