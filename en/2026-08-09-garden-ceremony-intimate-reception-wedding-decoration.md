# Garden Ceremony to Intimate Reception: Wedding Decoration Ideas

When planning a wedding, the journey from a vibrant outdoor ceremony to a cozy indoor reception can be a beautiful narrative. The wedding featured on Style Me Pretty—a garden ceremony at Bastide du Roy that transitioned to an intimate evening—showcases how thoughtful design can transform a space. For DIY brides and wedding planners, this real wedding offers a treasure trove of inspiration. Let's dive into how you can recreate this magic with budget-friendly strategies and practical tips.

## The Two-Act Color Palette: From Joyful to Elegant

One of the most striking aspects of this wedding was its two-act color palette. The ceremony embraced a vibrant and joyful spectrum—shades of pink, coral, blue, and fresh green—creating a lush, expressive garden atmosphere. This is a perfect example of how color can set the emotional tone for your event. For your own wedding, consider using bold, saturated hues for the ceremony to energize the space and reflect the excitement of the moment.

As the evening progressed, the palette shifted to a more restrained composition of ivory, cream, and soft neutrals. This transition not only matched the intimate mood of the reception but also created a sense of sophistication. For a practical takeaway: you don't have to stick to one palette throughout the day. Use color to tell a story—bold for the celebration, subtle for the more intimate moments. This approach is a smart wedding venue decoration idea that works in any setting.

## Affordable Wedding Flowers: The Key to a Lush Look

One of the biggest concerns for any couple is the floral budget. The lush greenery and blooms at Bastide du Roy might look expensive, but there are ways to achieve a similar effect without breaking the bank. The secret lies in choosing **affordable wedding flowers** that still make a statement. For the ceremony, focus on greenery—ferns, eucalyptus, and ivy—which are cost-effective and create a full, garden-like feel. Mix in a few high-impact blooms like garden roses or peonies in your accent colors, but let the greenery do the heavy lifting.

For the reception, opt for fewer, larger arrangements rather than many small ones. A single dramatic centerpiece on a long table can be more impactful and budget-friendly than several small vases. Also, consider repurposing ceremony arrangements for the reception—a classic trick that saves money and time. This is a win-win for DIY brides who want a high-end look on a realistic budget.

## Fake Flowers Centerpiece: A Smart Choice for Evening Events

When it comes to the reception, especially an evening event, **fake flowers centerpiece** options are a game-changer. Not only are they cost-effective, but they also allow for a level of control that fresh flowers can't offer. You can design your centerpieces weeks in advance, ensuring they look perfect on the day. For the intimate evening at Bastide du Roy, faux florals in ivory and cream would have been ideal—they mimic the soft elegance of the palette without wilting under the evening lights.

Modern fake flowers are incredibly realistic, and when mixed with fresh greenery or textured elements like berries and branches, they're nearly indistinguishable from the real thing. Another advantage? You can repurpose them after the wedding—use them to decorate your home or gift them to guests. This not only reduces waste but also adds lasting value. For a DIY bride, creating your own centerpieces with high-quality faux flowers is a satisfying and budget-savvy project.

## Practical Tips for a Seamless Transition

Transitioning from an outdoor ceremony to an indoor reception requires planning. Here are some practical tips to ensure a smooth flow:

- **Designate a helper**: Assign a friend or planner to move key elements (like ceremony flowers) to the reception space.
- **Use lighting**: Soft, warm lighting can transform a room and highlight your centerpieces. String lights or candles create an intimate ambiance.
- **Layer textures**: Mix linens, wood, and metal to add depth to your tablescape.
- **Consider the flow**: Ensure that the transition is logical—guests should move naturally from one space to the next, and the decor should feel cohesive.

By following these steps, you can create a wedding that feels both grand and personal, without the stress or the hefty price tag.

## Conclusion

The garden ceremony at Bastide du Roy is a testament to the power of thoughtful design. By embracing a two-act color palette, choosing affordable flowers, and using fake flowers for centerpieces, you can achieve a stunning wedding that reflects your style and respects your budget. Whether you're a DIY bride or a seasoned planner, these wedding venue decoration ideas are sure to inspire your next project. Remember, the best weddings are those that feel authentic to you—so take these tips and make them your own.
