A Rhode Island wedding at Castle Hill Inn captures the essence of a Newport summer: breezy, elegant, and deeply romantic. The historic 40-acre estate overlooking Narragansett Bay sets the stage for a coastal garden-party wedding that blends classic formality with a dreamy floral-forward design. For DIY brides and wedding planners looking to replicate this look without the hefty price tag of fresh blooms, incorporating bridal bouquet artificial flowers and silk flowers wedding decor offers a practical, stunning alternative.

## The Vision: Romantic Coastal Garden Party

The original wedding featured white, ivory, green, and pale blue hues—a palette that evokes seaside serenity and timeless elegance. The ceremony took place with the bay as a backdrop, creating an airy, open feel. To achieve this aesthetic with artificial flowers, focus on soft, natural textures. Choose silk flowers in shades of cream, blush, and dusty blue, paired with eucalyptus or ivy for greenery. Wholesale silk flowers allow you to buy in bulk for large installations like ceremony arches or table runners, keeping costs down while maintaining a high-end look.

## Key Elements to Recreate with Artificial Flowers

### 1. Bridal Bouquet Artificial Flowers

The bride's bouquet is the centerpiece of her look. For a Newport-inspired bouquet, select a mix of silk roses, peonies, and hydrangeas in ivory and pale blue. Wrap the stems with a soft ribbon or lace for a romantic touch. Artificial flowers offer the advantage of lasting forever—a perfect keepsake from the big day.

### 2. Silk Flowers Wedding Decor for the Ceremony

Create a stunning ceremony arch using silk flowers and greenery. Drape white and green blooms along the structure, with pops of pale blue for contrast. Since silk flowers don't wilt, you can set up the arch hours before the event, reducing stress on the wedding day. For the aisle, line with potted artificial greenery or small silk flower arrangements.

### 3. Reception Tablescapes with Wholesale Silk Flowers

For the reception, design centerpieces that mimic the coastal garden vibe. Use low, lush arrangements of silk flowers in glass vases or ceramic pots. Mix in candles and seashells for a beachy touch. Buying wholesale silk flowers in bulk allows you to create uniform centerpieces for every table without breaking the budget.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

- **Mix textures**: Combine smooth petals with fuzzy lamb's ear or wispy ferns for depth.
- **Use high-quality silk**: Invest in realistic stems that don't look plastic. Touch and bend them to ensure they feel natural.
- **Store properly**: Keep silk flowers in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to prevent fading.
- **Add fragrance**: Spritz a light floral scent on artificial blooms to mimic fresh flowers.
- **Scale up**: For large events, order samples first to check color and quality before committing to wholesale quantities.
