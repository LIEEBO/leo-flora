## How to Use Artificial Flowers with KONSTRUNDA Pieces

One of the best ways to incorporate these art objects into your wedding is by combining them with faux florals. A **wedding flower wall backdrop** is a must-have for photo booths and ceremony arches, and you can create your own using IKEA’s vases and decorative objects as accents. Here are some practical tips:

- **Create a focal point:** Place a large KONSTRUNDA vase on a pedestal and fill it with artificial blooms in your wedding colors. This works beautifully as an altar or entrance piece.
- **Mix textures:** Combine the smooth glass of the vase with the soft petals of silk flowers for a rich, layered look.
- **Think beyond centerpieces:** Use the multi-colour block decoration as a quirky table number holder or as part of a guest book display.
- **Wall art that doubles as décor:** The KONSTRUNDA rugs and cushions can be hung as wall art, adding a bohemian vibe to your venue. Pair them with a **wedding flower wall backdrop** for a cohesive, Instagram-worthy setting.
