# Revive Old Plastic Pots: A Clever Hack for Stunning Floral Displays

If you're a DIY bride or wedding planner constantly on the lookout for budget-friendly decor ideas, this TikTok hack is about to become your new best friend. It’s a brilliant way to repurpose old plastic plant pots into a professional-looking flower frog, perfect for creating gorgeous centerpieces without spending a fortune. Whether you're working with fresh blooms or silk flowers wedding decor, this trick will elevate your arrangements.

## The Hack That’s Taking Over TikTok

The viral video from creators @jeffandlaurenshow demonstrates a simple yet genius technique: place a plastic plant pot upside down inside a clear vase, then push flower stems through the drainage holes. This creates a sturdy grid that holds each stem in place, allowing you to build a full, lush bouquet with ease. It's essentially a DIY flower frog—a tool florists use to keep stems arranged perfectly.

What makes this hack so appealing is its accessibility. You likely already have an old pot lying around from a previous plant purchase. Instead of tossing it, you’re giving it a second life while solving the age-old problem of flowers flopping over in a vase. The result? A stunning display that looks like it came straight from a florist.

## Why This Works for Wedding Decor

For weddings, where every detail matters, this hack is a game-changer. It allows you to create beautiful faux floral arrangements for tables, aisles, or even the ceremony arch. Because the pot holds stems securely, you can transport your centerpieces without worrying about them falling apart. Plus, you can customize the look by choosing pots with different hole patterns—circular arrangements work best in wide bowls, while linear patterns suit narrower vases.

If you're using artificial flowers, this method is even more beneficial. Faux stems often have wire inside, making them flexible yet sturdy—perfect for inserting into the holes. You can pre-arrange your fake flowers centerpiece days in advance, saving you precious time on the big day. And unlike fresh flowers, they won’t wilt, so your decor will look perfect from the first dance to the last.

## Tips for Perfecting the Technique

To get the most out of this hack, consider the following tips:

- **Choose the right pot**: Look for pots with drainage holes that are large enough to accommodate your stem thickness. If the holes are too small, you can gently enlarge them with a drill or a heated screwdriver.
- **Match vase to pot**: A wide-necked vase or a low bowl works best, as it gives you room to spread stems out naturally. For a more structured look, use a taller vase with a narrower opening.
- **Mix heights and textures**: Combine different flower types and greenery to create depth. This is especially easy with faux stems, as you can bend and position them exactly how you want.
- **Add extra holes if needed**: If your pot’s hole pattern is too restrictive, don’t hesitate to add a few more. This gives you more flexibility for arranging.
