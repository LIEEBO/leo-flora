## Flower Care for Weddings: How to Keep Fresh Blooms Perfect (and Why Silk Flowers Are a Game-Changer)

Whether you're a DIY bride or a seasoned wedding planner, you know that flowers are one of the most important (and stressful) parts of the big day. Fresh flowers are gorgeous, but they need serious TLC to stay perfect from the ceremony to the last dance. And let's be honest—sometimes they just don't cooperate.

That's where smart flower care and a little backup planning come in. In this guide, we'll share practical tips to keep your fresh blooms looking their best, and we'll also introduce you to the world of **wholesale silk flowers** and **faux floral arrangements**—the secret weapon of savvy wedding pros. Plus, we'll show you how to create a stunning **wedding flower wall backdrop** that lasts all day (and beyond).

## 1. Prep Like a Pro: Vase, Water, and Food

Before your flowers even think about wilting, give them a fresh start. Choose a clean vase (scrub it with hot water and soap if it's dusty) and fill it two-thirds with cool, clean water. Add the flower food that comes with your bouquet—it contains nutrients, a pH balancer, and a sterilizing agent to keep bacteria at bay. If you're using a hydration pouch (common with courier deliveries), remove it over the sink and rinse the stems gently.

**Top tip:** Leave the flowers tied in their original arrangement—it keeps the florist's design intact and saves you time.

## 2. Trim, Strip, and Place: The Golden Rules of Stem Care

To help flowers drink up, trim about 2cm off the stems at an angle using a sharp, clean knife or secateurs. An angled cut prevents crushing and keeps stems from sitting flat on the vase bottom. Strip away any leaves that would be below the waterline—they'll rot and shorten vase life.

Place your arrangement away from direct sun, drafts, radiators, and windowsills. Also keep it out of reach of pets and little ones (lilies, for example, are toxic to cats).

**Top tip:** Change the water every two days and re-trim stems to maximize freshness.
