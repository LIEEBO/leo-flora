When Southern elegance meets timeless architecture, the result is a wedding that feels both grand and intimate. Imagine a historic domed ceiling, soft candlelight, and a 16-foot floral tree as the centerpiece of your reception. This New Orleans wedding, captured by Style Me Pretty, showcased just that—a breathtaking floral installation that set the tone for the entire evening. In this article, we'll break down how you can recreate this high-impact look for your own event, whether you're working with fresh blooms or opting for the practicality of artificial flowers.

## The Magic of a Statement Floral Tree

The focal point of this wedding was a towering 16-foot manzanita tree, its branches gracefully draped with light blue wisteria. Placed under the historic dome of the Board of Trade, it instantly drew the eye upward, creating a sense of wonder. The neutral palette of white, cream, and gold, accented with French blue, allowed the tree to stand out without overwhelming the space.

For planners and DIY brides, a floral tree is a bold choice that delivers maximum visual impact. It works beautifully as a backdrop for the head table, a photo opportunity, or even as an alternative to a traditional ceremony arch. The key is to scale it appropriately to your venue—what works in a grand ballroom might need to be downsized for a cozier space.

## Why Choose Artificial Flowers for Large Installations?

While fresh flowers are stunning, large-scale installations like this one come with practical challenges. Cost, availability, and the need for last-minute hydration can be stressful. That's where artificial flowers shine. High-quality silk and faux blooms mimic the look of fresh petals with none of the wilting worries.

For a project like a wedding flower wall backdrop, artificial flowers are a game-changer. You can pre-assemble panels, transport them easily, and reuse them for multiple events. Similarly, a bridal bouquet artificial flowers option allows you to have your exact dream bouquet months in advance, without worrying about seasonal availability or bruising. And for table decor, a fake flowers centerpiece can be crafted to match your theme perfectly, then kept as a keepsake long after the wedding day.

## Design Tips for a Grand Floral Installation

Creating a floral tree or wall that looks professionally designed doesn't require a floral degree—just a bit of planning. Here’s how to get started:

- **Choose a sturdy base**: Manzanita branches are popular for their organic shape and strength. If you're using a fake tree, look for one with a solid trunk and multiple branches that can hold weight.
- **Layer your blooms**: Mix different sizes and shades of flowers for depth. In the New Orleans wedding, the light blue wisteria contrasted beautifully with white and cream blooms. For a faux arrangement, use wired stems to attach flowers securely.
- **Consider lighting**: Uplighting can make a floral tree glow against a dark ceiling, while string lights woven through the branches add a romantic touch.
- **Think about balance**: If you have a tall tree, balance it with lower centerpieces or a wedding flower wall backdrop on the opposite side to create visual symmetry.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

If you're ready to tackle a project like this, here are some practical, budget-friendly tips:

1. **Start early**: Begin collecting your artificial flowers months in advance. Look for sales and wholesale options to save money.
2. **Use a grid system**: For a flower wall, use a wooden or PVC frame with chicken wire or a floral foam grid to attach stems easily.
3. **Make it modular**: Build your tree in sections that can be assembled on-site. This makes transportation and setup a breeze.
4. **Test your design**: Set up a small mock-up to see how the colors and textures work together. Adjust as needed before the big day.
5. **Hire a pro for the heavy lifting**: If you're a planner, consider hiring a floral designer to handle the installation, while you source the faux materials to keep costs down.
