## How to Make Fake Garland Look Even Better

Even the best fake garland can benefit from a few tweaks. Here are practical tips:

### 1. Fluff and Shape

Take time to separate leaves and bend stems to create a natural, organic shape. Avoid a flat, uniform look.

### 2. Mix in Real Elements

Add a few real sprigs of eucalyptus or pine for scent and texture. The contrast will fool the eye.

### 3. Use Lighting

Fairy lights or warm-toned bulbs woven through the garland add warmth and distract from any artificial sheen.

### 4. Combine with Flowers

Incorporate **fake flowers centerpiece** arrangements or **affordable wedding flowers** like silk roses or peonies. This adds color and romance.

### 5. Hide the Base

Wrap the stem with floral tape or ribbon, or tuck in moss to cover any plastic parts.

### 6. Weather the Look

For an outdoor wedding, lightly dust leaves with a bit of matte spray or use a touch of brown paint on edges to mimic natural wear.
