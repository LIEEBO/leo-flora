# True Touch Flowers Wholesale: A Complete Buying Guide

If you're planning a wedding or sourcing décor for events, you've likely come across the term "True Touch flowers." These realistic artificial blooms are a favorite among DIY brides and wedding planners because they offer the beauty of fresh flowers without the wilting, watering, or last-minute stress. But what exactly are True Touch flowers, and how are they made? In this guide, we'll break down everything you need to know, from the manufacturing process to smart wholesale buying tips.

## What Are True Touch Flowers?

True Touch flowers (also called Real Touch, Natural Touch, or Soft Touch) are high-end artificial flowers designed to mimic the look and feel of fresh blooms. Unlike standard silk flowers, True Touch petals are made with special materials like latex, polyurethane (PU), coated fabric, or silicone-like soft-touch finishes. This gives them a natural, almost velvety texture that's hard to distinguish from real petals at a glance.

For wholesale buyers—whether you're an importer, wedding supplier, or event decorator—understanding the material is key. A listing that says "true touch" might actually be ordinary silk unless the supplier proves otherwise. Always request samples to verify the material and hand-feel before placing a bulk order.

## How True Touch Flowers Are Made

The manufacturing process of True Touch flowers involves several steps to achieve that realistic look:

1. **Molding**: Each petal and leaf is cast from a mold based on a real flower. This captures fine details like veins and natural curves.
2. **Material Application**: Latex or PU is applied to fabric or foam-core bases. This coating gives the soft, rubbery texture that feels like real petals.
3. **Coloring**: Dyes and paints are hand-applied or airbrushed to create gradients, shading, and natural imperfections.
4. **Assembly**: Petals, stems, and leaves are assembled by hand or with semi-automated tools, ensuring each stem looks organic.
5. **Quality Control**: Finished flowers are inspected for color consistency, petal durability, and overall appearance before packing.

Because of this detailed process, True Touch flowers are more expensive than basic silk, but the realism is unmatched—perfect for close-up shots and intimate wedding settings.

## Wholesale Buying Tips for Wedding Planners and DIY Brides

When sourcing True Touch flowers wholesale, keep these practical tips in mind:

- **Verify the material**: Ask for samples and test the petals. Latex and PU coatings should feel soft and slightly tacky, not plastic-like.
- **Check MOQs and lead times**: Typical minimum order quantities (MOQs) range from 500–576 pieces per style. Samples usually take about 15 days, while bulk production can take 60–90 days. Plan your wedding timeline accordingly.
- **Understand shipping terms**: Most Chinese suppliers quote FOB Xiamen, with sea freight via FCL or LCL. Confirm carton dimensions and packing to avoid damage during transit.
- **Ask about OEM/ODM**: If you need custom colors, stem lengths, or packaging, make sure your supplier can accommodate your specifications.

## Creative Ways to Use True Touch Flowers in Weddings

Once you have your wholesale blooms, the possibilities are endless. Here are some **wedding venue decoration ideas** using faux floral arrangements:

- **Aisle markers**: Line the aisle with lush True Touch rose petals or small arrangements attached to chairs—no wilting under hot lights.
- **Table centerpieces**: Mix True Touch hydrangeas, peonies, and greenery for a full, romantic look that stays fresh all night.
- **Ceremony arch**: Create a stunning **wedding flower wall backdrop** using a mix of True Touch blooms. The soft texture looks incredible in photos and can be reused for the reception.
- **Bouquets and boutonnieres**: Since they're durable, you can assemble bouquets weeks in advance and even toss the bouquet without worry.
