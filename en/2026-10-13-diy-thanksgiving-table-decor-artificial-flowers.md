## 3 DIY Thanksgiving Table Decor Ideas

### 1. Rustic Harvest Centerpiece with Sunflowers and Maple Leaves

**Perfect for:** Farmhouse-style tables, outdoor gatherings, or cozy family dinners.

Nothing says "fall" like sunflowers and maple leaves. For this fake flowers centerpiece, combine:

- **Real Touch Artificial Sunflowers Bouquet** (5 stems, 13" tall) – Their latex-coated petals mimic the soft texture of fresh sunflowers.
- **Real-Touch Artificial Maple Leaf Stems** (3-pack, 14" branches) – These adjustable stems come in warm yellow, orange, and red hues for a natural gradient.
- A **wooden tray or low ceramic vase** (fill with dried corn kernels or acorns for extra texture).

**How to style it:** Arrange the sunflowers in the center, then tuck maple leaves around the base to create a full, organic shape. Add a few miniature pumpkins or pinecones for a harvest feel. This arrangement works beautifully as a **silk flowers wedding decor** accent if you’re planning a fall wedding.

### 2. Elegant Eucalyptus and Berry Garland

**Perfect for:** Long rectangular tables or buffet stations.

A garland adds instant elegance without blocking conversation. Use artificial eucalyptus stems and faux berry sprays to create a lush runner.

**What you’ll need:**

- 6–8 artificial eucalyptus stems (silver or green)
- 4–5 faux berry sprays (red or burgundy)
- Floral wire or twine

**How to style it:** Lay the eucalyptus along the center of the table, overlapping the stems. Weave in berry sprays for pops of color. Secure with floral wire if needed. Add candlesticks or small votives for a warm glow. This garland also doubles as a stunning **bridal bouquet artificial flowers** alternative—simply gather a small section and tie with ribbon for a rustic bridal look.

### 3. Mini Pumpkin and Succulent Place Settings

**Perfect for:** Intimate dinners or assigned seating.

Mini pumpkins make charming place card holders. Hollow out a small pumpkin (or use a faux one) and insert a small artificial succulent or a few faux flower stems.

**What you’ll need:**

- Mini pumpkins (real or faux)
- Artificial succulents or small faux blooms
- Hot glue gun
- Place cards

**How to style it:** Glue a succulent to the top of each pumpkin. Tuck a place card into the leaves. These double as party favors guests can take home. For a more formal look, use white pumpkins and silver-toned artificial greenery.

## Practical Tips for Styling Your Thanksgiving Table

- **Mix textures:** Combine smooth leaves, fuzzy berries, and metallic accents for depth.
- **Stick to a color palette:** Warm oranges, deep reds, and earthy greens create a cohesive fall look.
- **Use odd numbers:** Groupings of three or five stems look more natural than even numbers.
- **Add height:** Use cake stands or small crates to elevate centerpieces and create visual interest.
- **Light it up:** Candles or string lights add warmth and make artificial flowers glow.
