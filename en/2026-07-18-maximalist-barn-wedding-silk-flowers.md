## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

### 1. Buy in Bulk and Mix Heights

Ordering **wholesale silk flowers** allows you to experiment without breaking the budget. Create tall, dramatic centerpieces using a mix of tall grasses and large blooms, and shorter clusters for the tables. Varying heights adds visual interest and fills the space beautifully.

### 2. Use Color Blocking for Impact

Instead of scattering colors evenly, group them. Place a cluster of hot pink peonies on one side of the altar and a burst of orange dahlias on the other. This technique makes each color stand out and gives your **silk flowers wedding decor** a curated, intentional look.

### 3. Don't Forget the Ceiling

Barns have gorgeous wooden beams—use them! Hang garlands of **fake flowers centerpiece** elements from the ceiling to draw the eye upward. Mix in greenery and fairy lights for a magical, maximalist canopy.

### 4. Layer Textures

Combine smooth petals with fuzzy lamb's ear, shiny leaves with matte berries. The tactile variety will make your arrangements feel rich and expensive, even when using **wholesale silk flowers**.
