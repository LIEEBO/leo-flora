## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

1. **Mix textures for depth**: Combine matte silk petals with velvet leaves or metallic accents. This mimics the richness of real blooms and adds high-fashion drama.
2. **Use a fake flowers centerpiece as a focal point**: For each table, create a centerpiece that blends blush, ivory, and red silk flowers. Add candles or beads for cultural touches.
3. **Pre-assemble and store**: Faux florals can be arranged weeks before the wedding. Store them in cool, dry places to keep dust-free.
4. **Incorporate cultural elements**: Wrap stems in traditional fabric or add wooden beads to reflect Nigerian heritage. This personal touch makes the decor meaningful.
5. **Scale for impact**: For a high-fashion look, go big. Oversized silk flower arches or cascading centerpieces create wow factor without extra cost.
