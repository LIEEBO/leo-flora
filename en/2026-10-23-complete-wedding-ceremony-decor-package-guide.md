## Arch & Backdrop Size Guide: Get the Proportions Right

The arch and backdrop are the focal point of your ceremony. Getting the proportions right is crucial for photographs. Here are the standard sizes that work beautifully:

- **Arch:** A height of 7–8 feet and width of 5–6 feet is ideal for most ceremonies. It frames the couple without overwhelming the space.
- **Backdrop:** For a backdrop behind the arch, aim for at least 8 feet wide and 7 feet tall. This creates a lush, immersive feel.
- **Aisle:** The aisle should be wide enough for the couple and their attendants—typically 4–6 feet. A runner or petals can define the path.

When using **silk flowers wedding decor**, you can easily adjust the size by adding or removing floral clusters. This flexibility is a huge advantage over fresh flowers.

## The Flower Math: How Much Coverage Do You Actually Need?

One of the biggest questions is: how many flowers do I need? The answer depends on the density you want. For a lush, full look on an arch, plan for 60–80% coverage. That means roughly 20–30 large blooms (like roses or peonies) plus filler flowers and greenery per arch.

For a backdrop, you'll need more—aim for 50–70% coverage, which could mean 50–100 blooms depending on the size. Aisle markers typically use smaller clusters of 5–7 blooms each. The beauty of artificial flowers is that you can buy in bulk and arrange them to your desired fullness without worrying about wilting.

## 7 Costly Package Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Underestimating flower coverage**—always buy 10–20% extra.
2. **Ignoring proportions**—measure your space before ordering.
3. **Choosing low-quality silk**—cheap flowers look fake in photos.
4. **Forgetting about setup time**—artificial flowers need arranging.
5. **Not planning for weather**—silk flowers are durable but can be weighted down.
6. **Skipping a trial run**—set up your arch before the big day.
7. **Overcomplicating the design**—simplicity often photographs best.
