You’ve finally made the leap into artificial greenery—hooray! Whether you’re crafting a lush wedding flower wall backdrop, designing fake flowers centerpieces for reception tables, or styling a potted faux fern for your ceremony aisle, the process is similar for all. Now that you’ve purchased your artificial plants, you might be wondering: how do you unpack and arrange them so they look realistic and stay secure? Today we’re sharing expert tips to help you get that nearly natural effect every time—perfect for DIY brides and wedding planners alike.

## Unpack and Stand Your Artificial Plant Upright

Your faux floral arrangements—whether a tabletop orchid or a tall potted topiary—will arrive carefully packaged in a secure box. Upon delivery, find an open workspace and carefully open all taped corners (you may need scissors). Inside, your artificial plant will be wrapped in protective plastic. Gently remove it and stand it upright on a floor or table surface.

Most topiary trees arrive in a starter pot, which you can later place inside a decorative plant pot for a more finished look. This also helps stabilize the plant, especially for taller designs. If you’re planning a wedding flower wall backdrop, you might want to keep the starter pot for easy mounting or weighing down.

## Bend Down Branches for a Natural Spread

When you first receive your artificial plant, you’ll notice that most of the foliage is tightly packed and folded upward against the stem or trunk. This is to protect it during shipping. Start at the bottom and carefully pull down each section or tier of greenery.

Because each branch contains a hidden flexible wire, you can easily bend and separate them from the trunk. Take your time, working one branch at a time, to create a natural spread. This is especially important for fake flowers centerpieces—you want each stem to have its own direction and personality.

## Fluff Out All the Leaves

Once the branches are separated, move through each tier of foliage and begin fluffing the leaves. Bend each one outward, alternating positions and directions to avoid a stiff or uniform look. For a lifelike touch, curve each leaf slightly along its spine—this gives your foliage a gentle, natural arch.

If you’re building a wedding flower wall backdrop, fluffing every leaf and bloom ensures the wall looks lush and full, not flat or sparse. The same goes for faux floral arrangements: fluffing is the secret to a professional, polished appearance.

## Continue to Style and Fluff Throughout

Work your way around the plant from the bottom up, using your fingers to shape the leaves into realistic positions. Real plants have natural variations in color and shape, and our artificial plants reflect this as well. Position the leaves to highlight these subtle details.

Don’t worry if some areas look “less perfect.” This irregularity actually makes the plant appear more real. If any branch looks uneven, simply re-bend it until you’re happy with the result. For wedding decor, this step is crucial—whether you’re styling a centerpiece or a backdrop, the goal is to mimic nature’s effortless beauty.

## Add Accompanying Setting to the Base

While many artificial plants come stabilized in their own plant pot, some include decorative base materials like rocks or moss that you’ll need to add yourself. If your plant arrives in a starter pot, place it inside your chosen decorative plant pot and fill the space around it with floral foam, gravel, or moss. This not only keeps the plant stable but also adds a finished, designer touch.

For wedding planners, this is a great opportunity to coordinate your faux floral arrangements with your theme. Use moss for a rustic look, white gravel for a modern vibe, or even fairy lights for a magical evening reception.

## Final Thoughts

Unpacking and arranging artificial plants doesn’t have to be a chore. With a little patience and these simple techniques, you can transform your faux greenery into stunning decor that rivals the real thing. Whether you’re creating a wedding flower wall backdrop, designing fake flowers centerpieces, or building lush faux floral arrangements for an event, the key is to fluff, bend, and style with intention. Happy decorating!
