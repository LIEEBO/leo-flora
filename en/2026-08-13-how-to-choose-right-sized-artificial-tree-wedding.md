# How to Choose the Right Sized Artificial Tree for Your Wedding Venue

Choosing the perfect artificial tree or plant for your wedding venue can feel overwhelming. You fall in love with a lush faux olive tree or a dramatic fiddle leaf fig, only to discover it's too tall for the entrance or too wide for the altar. Whether you're a DIY bride planning your own décor or a wedding planner curating multiple events, getting the size right is essential. In this guide, we'll walk you through how to measure for artificial trees, where to place them for maximum impact, and how to incorporate them into your wedding venue decoration ideas—plus tips for using bridal bouquet artificial flowers and fake flowers centerpiece arrangements.

## Step 1: Decide Where Your Tree or Plant Will Live

Before you start shopping, think about the role your artificial tree will play. Will it be a focal point at the ceremony, or a subtle accent in the reception? Consider the sightlines: will guests see it from seated or standing eye level? For example, a tall, slim faux olive tree in a foyer creates a grand entrance, while a compact boxwood topiary works perfectly as a table centerpiece.

- **Ceremony backdrop:** A large faux fiddle leaf fig or bird of paradise can anchor the altar or archway, adding a touch of nature without wilting.
- **Reception entrance:** Place a tall, slim faux bamboo or olive tree to welcome guests and set the tone.
- **Cocktail tables:** Use small faux palms or snake plants to add greenery without taking up too much space.
- **Head table:** A pair of matching artificial trees can frame the bridal party beautifully.

**Pro tip:** Faux plants soften harsh lines and bring a calming, natural balance to your décor. They're also perfect for venues with no natural light or strict no-live-plant policies.

## Step 2: How to Measure for an Artificial Tree or Plant

Getting the size right is crucial. Here's a simple method to ensure your artificial tree fits perfectly:

1. **Measure the height:** From the floor to the desired top of the tree. Remember to account for the pot or planter. A 6-foot tree in a 2-foot pot will be 8 feet total.
2. **Measure the width:** Check the canopy spread at its widest point. Make sure it won't block walkways or doorways.
3. **Consider the scale:** A tree should be about 2/3 the height of the ceiling or backdrop it's placed against. For a 10-foot ceiling, a 6-7 foot tree works well.
4. **Test the location:** Use painter's tape on the floor to outline the tree's footprint. This helps you visualize the space it will occupy.

For wedding venue decoration ideas, think about using multiple trees of varying heights to create depth. For example, a 7-foot tree behind the head table, flanked by 4-foot trees on either side, creates a beautiful layered look.

## Step 3: Match the Style to Your Theme

Your artificial greenery should complement your wedding theme. Whether you're going for minimalist, rustic, or glam, there's a faux tree or plant for you.

- **Rustic:** Olive trees, eucalyptus stems, and boxwood topiaries add a natural, earthy feel.
- **Modern:** Clean lines with snake plants or fiddle leaf figs in sleek ceramic pots.
- **Romantic:** Cherry blossom branches or wisteria trees in soft pastels.

When using bridal bouquet artificial flowers, choose blooms that echo the colors of your trees. For a cohesive look, match the greenery in your centerpieces to the foliage of your artificial trees.

## Step 4: Practical Tips for Placement and Safety

- **Safety first:** If you have candles, fairy lights, or small children at the venue, ensure trees are placed in safe spots away from open flames and high-traffic areas.
- **Pets:** Some artificial plants look so real that pets may try to nibble them. Keep them out of reach or discourage pets from getting close.
- **Balance:** Mix different sizes and shapes to avoid a monotonous look. Use a tall tree in a corner, a medium plant on a sideboard, and a small succulent on each table.

For fake flowers centerpiece ideas, pair a small artificial tree with a cluster of faux blooms in a vase. This adds height and interest without overwhelming the table.

## Final Thoughts

Choosing the right sized artificial tree and plant for your wedding doesn't have to be a guessing game. By measuring carefully, considering placement, and matching your theme, you can create stunning wedding venue decoration ideas that wow your guests. Whether you're using bridal bouquet artificial flowers for the bridal party or crafting fake flowers centerpiece for each table, artificial greenery offers endless possibilities—without the worry of wilting or allergies. Happy decorating!
