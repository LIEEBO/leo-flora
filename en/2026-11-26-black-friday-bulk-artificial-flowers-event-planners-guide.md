## Why Bulk Buyers Should Care About Black Friday

Black Friday offers a rare chance to stock up at reduced prices, but the key is to focus on versatile, durable products that will serve multiple themes and seasons.

- **Cost efficiency:** Buying in bulk during sales lowers per-unit costs, especially for high-turn items like roses, peonies, and greenery.
- **Inventory readiness:** With wedding season ramping up, having a deep stock of artificial wedding centerpieces and accent stems ensures you never scramble for last-minute orders.
- **Quality assurance:** Reputable suppliers use Black Friday to move volume, but you must verify materials and construction to avoid false economies.

## Material Selection: The Key to Repeat Business

Not all artificial flowers are created equal. For B2B buyers, material quality directly impacts client satisfaction and your reputation. Here’s what to prioritize:

- **Real-Touch PU:** Polyurethane petals mimic the softness and flexibility of fresh flowers. They resist crushing and hold up well in shipping.
- **Silk & Velvet:** High-quality silk offers a natural sheen, while velvet adds a luxurious texture for upscale events. Ensure fabrics are colorfast and won’t fade under venue lights.
- **UV-Resistant PE:** For outdoor installations or artificial green plant walls, polyethylene with UV inhibitors prevents fading and brittleness. This is essential for long-term outdoor displays.
- **Construction Details:** Check for reinforced stems, secure petal attachments, and realistic color gradients. Flimsy stems or glued petals will fail during setup.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What’s the difference between real touch and silk flowers?
Real touch flowers are made from PU (polyurethane) that mimics the soft, supple feel of fresh petals, while silk flowers use fabric for a more traditional look. Real touch is generally more durable and realistic for high-end events.

### How many stems do I need for a wedding centerpiece?
It depends on the arrangement size and flower type. As a rule of thumb, a standard 10-inch centerpiece uses 8–12 stems of mixed blooms and greenery. Oversized statement stems may require only 3–5.

### Can artificial flowers be used outdoors?
Yes, but choose UV-resistant materials like PE for artificial green plant walls and outdoor installations. Real touch and silk are better suited for indoor or covered areas.

### What is the typical MOQ for bulk artificial flowers?
MOQs vary by supplier and product. Factory-direct sources like Leo Faux often offer low MOQs (e.g., 10–50 pieces per style) to accommodate small businesses and event planners.

### How do I ensure color consistency across batches?
