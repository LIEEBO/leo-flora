WHO WE ARE

Craftsmanship Meets Factory-Direct Scale

Founded in Yiwu — the world's largest hub for floral and decorative trade — Leo Faux combines dedicated factory production with a modern wholesale showroom at the Global Digital Trade Center (District 6, Yiwu International Trade City).

From Real-Touch Phalaenopsis Orchids, Velvet Roses, Austin Peonies, and Hydrangeas to Commercial Artificial Trees, Green Plant Walls, Wedding Flower Walls, and Seasonal Memorial/Christmas Florals, every stem is quality-checked for natural color gradients, petal realism, and export-ready packaging.

Whether you are a wholesale distributor, wedding & event planner, hotel interior contractor, or cross-border e-commerce seller (Amazon / Shopify / Etsy), our team responds within 24 hours with clear quotations and flexible MOQs.
