- **Mix real and faux florals**: Use real blooms for the focal pieces and **fake flowers centerpiece** options for areas that need to last all day. High-quality silk flowers look just as beautiful in photos and are more budget-friendly.
- **Create a focal wall**: A **wedding flower wall backdrop** is a stunning addition to any proposal. Whether you choose a lush greenery wall or a cascading floral arrangement, it provides a gorgeous backdrop for photos and adds a wow factor.

- **Consider the wind**: If your setup is outdoors, secure lightweight items and choose sturdy vases or containers.
