## Why Dried Lavender Works in the Kitchen

Dried lavender isn’t just for bathrooms or entryways—it can transform your kitchen into a fragrant, visual delight. According to Ntola Obazee, creative director at Kutchenhaus, “Dried lavender can be used across the entire home, and we often see it hung up in places such as the bathroom or on doors. However, what most people don’t automatically think of is using it in the kitchen. If you have a rail above the worktop, or at the end of your cabinet or island, then lavender, along with other dried herbs, is great at adding scent as well as visual interest to the space.”

Lavender creates texture and provides a gentle scent, which is great for covering old food smells. It also has the added benefit of being a great low-maintenance alternative to fresh flowers, which can be enjoyed for months to come. Plus, its muted purple tones blend beautifully with rustic wood, stone, and neutral palettes—perfect for a country-style kitchen.

## How to Get the Look: Hanging Lavender Bunches

To recreate Clodagh’s fairytale kitchen, start by sourcing high-quality dried lavender. You can find it at craft stores, online, or even dry your own from your garden. Once you have your bunches, tie them with twine or ribbon, leaving a loop for hanging. Then, hang them from ceiling beams, open shelving, or a pot rack—just like Clodagh did with her cookware. Space them out evenly between pots and pans for a cohesive, curated look.

If you don’t have beams, consider installing a simple wooden rail or using adhesive hooks on the underside of cabinets. The key is to keep the lavender away from direct heat and moisture, so avoid placing it right above the stove or sink. This will help preserve its color and scent for longer.

## Practical Tips for Decorating with Dried and Faux Botanicals

- **Mix textures**: Combine dried lavender with silk flowers, eucalyptus, or even dried orange slices for a rich, layered look.
- **Use vertical space**: Hang bunches from beams, shelves, or a pegboard to draw the eye upward and make the room feel taller.
- **Consider scent**: Dried lavender naturally perfumes the air, but if you use faux flowers, add a few drops of lavender essential oil to a cotton ball and tuck it among the stems.
- **Keep it fresh**: Dust faux arrangements regularly and replace dried lavender every few months to keep it looking vibrant.
- **Think beyond the kitchen**: These decor ideas work beautifully in dining rooms, entryways, and even wedding reception spaces.
