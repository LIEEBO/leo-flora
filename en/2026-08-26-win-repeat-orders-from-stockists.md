Getting that first order from a new stockist is a thrill. But the real win? The second order, the third, and building a relationship that turns a one-time buyer into a loyal partner who reorders every season—without you having to chase them.

Yet this is exactly where many wedding and floral brands get stuck. You land the first order, deliver beautifully, and then... wait. And hope. And feel awkward about following up because you don't want to seem pushy or desperate.

Here's the truth: the discomfort is real, but the fear of being pushy is almost always bigger than the actual risk. Reaching out to a stockist to see how your realistic artificial flowers are selling isn't pushy—it's professional. In most cases, it's exactly what they expect you to do.

## Why Reorders Don't Happen Automatically

It would be lovely if stockists who loved your products just... reordered when they needed more. Sometimes they do. But often, they don't.

Retail buyers are busy. Running a shop involves a constant stream of decisions, suppliers, customers, admin, and logistics. Your brand is one of many they work with, and unless you stay visible, you're not front of mind when they think about restocking.

They also don't always know when to reorder. This sounds odd, but many independent retailers lack sophisticated stock management systems. They might not realize they're running low until they're almost out—and by then, it's too late to reorder in time. A timely check-in from you can be genuinely helpful, not just for you but for them.

And sometimes a product sells through faster than expected, and the buyer simply hasn't had a chance to reach out. They're not avoiding you; they just haven't gotten around to it yet.

## Pushy vs. Professional: Know the Difference

Pushy is contacting someone multiple times a week, ignoring their cues, applying pressure, or making them feel guilty for not buying. That's not what we're talking about.

Professional is staying in touch at a reasonable cadence, giving your stockists useful and relevant information, making it easy for them to reorder when they're ready, and being the kind of supplier they actually want to hear from.

The key difference is whether you're showing up with something of value—or just showing up. A check-in that includes how a product is performing elsewhere, a heads-up about an upcoming wedding season, or a note about a new product that fits their range is something a buyer is glad to receive. An email that just says "just checking in to see if you need anything" is harder to respond to and easier to ignore.

## Practical Tips to Win Repeat Orders

### 1. Build a Check-in Schedule

The brands that get consistent reorders are almost always the ones who have a system—even a simple one—for staying in touch with their stockists. It doesn't need to be complicated. Set a regular check-in with every active stockist, follow up one week after a first order, and plan a seasonal touchpoint.

For example, before peak wedding season (spring and early summer), reach out to stockists who carry your wedding flower wall backdrop or affordable wedding flowers. Remind them that brides are actively looking for these items and offer to send more stock or a fresh display idea.

### 2. Share Performance Insights

Buyers love to know how products are selling. If your realistic artificial flowers are trending in another shop, tell them. If a particular color or style is flying off shelves, share that data. This positions you as a partner who helps them make smarter buying decisions—not just a salesperson.

### 3. Make Reordering Effortless

Don't make your stockists work to place an order. Send a simple reorder form, a link to your wholesale portal, or even a one-click reorder option. The easier it is, the more likely they'll do it.

### 4. Offer Seasonal Promotions or Bundles

Give stockists a reason to reorder sooner. Offer a small discount on bulk orders of affordable wedding flowers, or bundle your wedding flower wall backdrop with matching garlands or centerpieces. Make it a no-brainer for them to stock up.

### 5. Ask for Feedback (and Act on It)

When you check in, ask how the products are selling and if there's anything they'd like to see improved. Then actually listen. If they suggest a new color or a smaller size option, consider it. This builds trust and shows you value their input.

## Conclusion

Winning repeat orders from stockists isn't about being pushy—it's about being proactive, professional, and genuinely helpful. Build a simple check-in system, share valuable insights, and make reordering easy. When you show up with value, stockists will see you as a trusted partner, and those reorders will start to come naturally.

So go ahead and reach out. Your stockists (and your future orders) will thank you.
