## Practical Tips for Using Boxwood Troughs in Your Wedding

### 1. Line the Aisle

Place trough planters along both sides of the aisle for a grand, garden-like entrance. Their uniform height and dense foliage create a natural, elegant border that guides guests' eyes to the altar. Add a few silk flower stems tucked into the foliage for a pop of color.

### 2. Frame Key Areas

Use them to flank the entrance of your venue, the head table, or the cake table. The stone-effect planter adds a touch of sophistication, while the greenery softens architectural lines. For an outdoor ceremony, position them in front of the seating area to define the space.

### 3. Mix and Match Heights

If you have multiple sizes (the product comes in four), mix them to create visual interest. Place taller troughs near the back and shorter ones in front for a layered look. This works beautifully for stage backdrops or photo booths.

### 4. Weather-Proof Your Decor

Don't let a sudden drizzle ruin your setup. These planters are designed to handle the elements, so you can relax and enjoy your day. They're also easy to clean—just wipe down with a damp cloth and they're ready for the next event.
