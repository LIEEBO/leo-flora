# Coastal Garden Party Wedding Ideas from Castle Hill Inn

Imagine a summer wedding on a historic 40-acre estate overlooking the Narragansett Bay. The air is salty, the breeze is warm, and the scenery is pure New England charm. That’s exactly the vibe of the stunning Rhode Island wedding featured at Castle Hill Inn. With its classic yet romantic design, this celebration is a dream for anyone planning a coastal garden party wedding.

If you’re a DIY bride or a wedding planner looking for inspiration, this real wedding is full of ideas you can adapt—especially when it comes to floral-forward decor. And the best part? You don’t need fresh flowers to achieve the same look. With realistic artificial flowers, you can capture the same airy, romantic aesthetic while staying within budget and reducing stress.

## The Design Vibe: Romantic, Airy, and Coastal

The wedding at Castle Hill Inn was a perfect blend of classic formality and dreamy garden-party charm. The color palette—white, ivory, green, and pale blue—echoed the natural beauty of the seaside setting. The ceremony overlooked the bay, with the ocean as a breathtaking backdrop.

To recreate this vibe, focus on soft, flowing lines and natural textures. Think linen table runners, wooden chairs, and lots of greenery. For your floral decor, choose arrangements that feel loose and organic rather than stiff and structured. This is where **faux floral arrangements** shine—they can be styled to look effortlessly wild, just like fresh flowers, but they’ll stay perfect all day long.

## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas: Bring the Outdoors In

One of the biggest takeaways from this Newport wedding is how the decor complemented the venue rather than competing with it. For your own wedding venue decoration ideas, consider these tips:

- **Use the landscape as your backdrop.** If you’re near water, mountains, or gardens, let nature do the heavy lifting. Keep your decor minimal in those areas.
- **Incorporate natural elements.** Driftwood, seashells, and potted greenery can tie your theme together without overwhelming the space.
- **Create focal points with florals.** A lush arch, a hanging installation, or a long table runner made of blooms can instantly elevate a plain space.

For a coastal garden party, consider using **realistic artificial flowers** in your centerpieces and ceremony arch. They won’t wilt in the sun or wind, and they’ll look gorgeous in photos from every angle.

## Why Choose Faux Florals for Your Wedding?

Many brides worry that artificial flowers will look fake or cheap. But today’s high-quality silk and latex flowers are incredibly realistic—often indistinguishable from fresh blooms. Here’s why they’re a smart choice for weddings like this one:

- **Budget-friendly:** You can save up to 60% compared to fresh flowers, especially for large installations.
- **Stress-free:** No last-minute wilting, no water needed, no allergy worries.
- **Reusable:** After the wedding, you can repurpose them for your home or sell them to another bride.
- **Perfect for destination weddings:** If you’re traveling to a venue like Castle Hill Inn, you can ship your florals ahead without worrying about freshness.

When shopping for faux florals, look for options with realistic touch and subtle color variations. Mixing different textures—like velvet roses, silk peonies, and eucalyptus sprays—will give your arrangements depth and dimension.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

If you’re planning a coastal garden party wedding, here are some actionable tips to bring your vision to life:

1. **Start with a color palette.** Stick to 3-4 colors that complement your venue. The Castle Hill wedding used white, ivory, green, and pale blue—a palette that’s both classic and refreshing.
2. **Mix high and low.** For centerpieces, use a mix of tall and short arrangements to create visual interest on the table.
3. **Add fragrance separately.** Since faux flowers don’t smell, use scented candles or essential oil diffusers to evoke a garden atmosphere.
4. **Plan for wind.** If your ceremony is outdoors, secure your arrangements well. Faux florals are easier to anchor than fresh ones.
5. **Think about lighting.** Soft, warm lighting (like string lights or lanterns) will make your florals glow in the evening.

## Final Thoughts: Capture the Spirit of Your Own Summer

The Castle Hill Inn wedding is a beautiful example of how a venue and floral design can come together to create an unforgettable day. Whether you’re planning a coastal garden party or a more formal affair, you can achieve a similar look with thoughtful decor and **realistic artificial flowers**.

By choosing faux florals, you’re not sacrificing beauty—you’re gaining flexibility, durability, and peace of mind. So go ahead, dream big, and design a wedding that feels like a breath of fresh sea air. Your guests will remember the romance, the style, and the joy—not whether the flowers were real or not.
