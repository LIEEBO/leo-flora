## Practical Tips for Recreating This Look

1. **Invest in Quality Faux Florals:** Not all artificial flowers are created equal. Choose realistic artificial flowers that are made from high-quality materials like silk or latex. They should have a natural sheen and feel soft to the touch.
2. **Mix Fresh and Faux:** For a hybrid approach, use fresh flowers for the bridal bouquet and boutonnieres, and faux for larger installations like the arch and centerpieces. This balances authenticity with practicality.
3. **Buy in Bulk:** To save money, purchase wholesale silk flowers for your DIY projects. You'll get a better price per stem, and you can create abundant arrangements that look luxurious.
4. **Consider the Setting:** For outdoor weddings, faux flowers are a lifesaver. They won't wilt, droop, or attract bees, and they'll look perfect in photos from start to finish.
5. **Repurpose and Reuse:** Plan to move ceremony florals to the reception. An arch can become a backdrop for the sweetheart table, and aisle arrangements can be placed on buffet tables. This maximizes your investment.
