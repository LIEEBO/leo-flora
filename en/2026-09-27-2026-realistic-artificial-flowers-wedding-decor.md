## Practical Tips for Using Faux Botanicals in Wedding Decor

1. **Mix textures:** Combine real touch petals with silk leaves and dried elements for a natural, layered look.
2. **Play with scale:** Use large statement blooms like bougainvillea alongside delicate cosmos for visual interest.
3. **Focus on color palette:** Stick to 2–3 complementary hues to keep arrangements cohesive.
4. **Think beyond bouquets:** Use faux stems in ceremony arches, aisle runners, centerpieces, and even cake flowers.
5. **Store and reuse:** After the wedding, clean your blooms gently and store them for future events or home decor.
