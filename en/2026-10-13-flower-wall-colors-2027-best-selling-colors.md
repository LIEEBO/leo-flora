## Seasonal Color Strategy: What Rents When

Timing is everything. Here's a quick seasonal breakdown:

- **Spring**: Blush pink, lavender, and butter yellow are in high demand.
- **Summer**: White, greenery, and coral tones dominate.
- **Fall**: Burgundy, rust, and deep orange are popular.
- **Winter**: White, ivory, and metallics like silver and gold.

For wedding planners, offering seasonal color options can attract more clients. For rental companies, rotating inventory based on season maximizes bookings. If you're a bride, choose a color that aligns with your wedding date for a cohesive look.

## Color by Event Type: Wedding vs. Corporate vs. Party

Different events call for different palettes:

- **Weddings**: Soft, romantic colors like blush, white, and greenery. These are the most rented.
- **Corporate Events**: Neutral tones like white, ivory, and navy blue for a professional vibe.
- **Parties**: Bold colors like emerald, burgundy, or even black for a dramatic effect.

If you're planning a wedding, affordable wedding flowers in classic colors will give you the most bang for your buck. For corporate planners, investing in neutral walls ensures repeat use.

## 5 Color Mistakes That Kill Rental Revenue

1. **Buying too much of a trendy color** – Like my dusty lavender mistake. It rented twice in a year.
2. **Ignoring seasonality** – Stocking fall colors in spring.
3. **Not offering neutrals** – White and blush are always in demand.
4. **Overlooking versatility** – Colors that only work for one event type limit bookings.
5. **Forgetting maintenance** – Faded or dirty walls don't rent. Choose high-quality realistic artificial flowers that hold up.
