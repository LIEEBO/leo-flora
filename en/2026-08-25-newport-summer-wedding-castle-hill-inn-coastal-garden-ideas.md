## A Newport Summer Wedding at Castle Hill Inn: Coastal Garden Inspiration

Imagine saying your vows overlooking the sparkling Narragansett Bay, the salty breeze mingling with the scent of fresh blooms. That was the dreamy reality for one couple at the historic Castle Hill Inn in Newport, Rhode Island. Their wedding perfectly captured the essence of a Newport summer—romantic, airy, and effortlessly elegant. For brides and planners seeking that coastal garden-party vibe, this real wedding is a treasure trove of inspiration. And the best part? You can recreate this look beautifully and budget-friendly with high-quality artificial flowers.

## The Vision: Romantic, Airy, and Floral-Forward

Set on a 40-acre estate, the wedding design blended classic formality with a dreamy, floral-forward aesthetic. The color palette was a breath of fresh air: crisp whites, soft ivories, gentle greens, and pale blues. This combination echoes the natural surroundings—the ocean, the lawns, and the sky—creating a seamless indoor-outdoor flow.

The ceremony, held with the bay as a backdrop, was framed by lush floral arrangements that felt both wild and refined. The reception continued the theme with tablescapes adorned with low centerpieces and tall, airy arrangements. The overall effect was a garden party that felt both polished and welcoming.

For DIY brides, achieving this look with fresh flowers can be costly and stressful, especially with a specific color palette. This is where artificial flowers shine. Modern faux blooms are incredibly realistic, allowing you to design with the exact shades of ivory, green, and pale blue you envision, without worrying about wilting or seasonality.

## Recreating the Look with Artificial Flowers

### 1. The Ceremony Backdrop

The ceremony arch at Castle Hill was a showstopper. To recreate a similar focal point, consider a **wedding flower wall backdrop**. A lush, mixed-flower wall in your palette creates an unforgettable photo opportunity and frames your vows beautifully. You can build one using a sturdy frame, chicken wire or floral foam, and a mix of artificial greenery and blooms. It's a weekend project that pays off in stunning photos and can be repurposed for the reception or even your home afterward.

### 2. Centerpieces That Wow

For the reception tables, mix tall and low centerpieces. For low centerpieces, use a **fake flowers centerpiece** in a classic glass vase or a vintage silver urn. Combine ivory roses, pale blue hydrangeas, and soft green eucalyptus for that coastal garden feel. For tall arrangements, use clear cylinders with floating candles and a few stemmed flowers above a base of greenery. This creates visual interest without blocking guests' conversations.

### 3. Ceremony Aisle and Reception Details

Don't forget the aisle! Line it with small arrangements of artificial flowers and greenery attached to shepherd's hooks. These are easy to make ahead of time and can be moved indoors for the reception. Also, consider adding floral touches to your cake table, bar, and even the restroom—these small details make a big impact.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

- **Choose Quality:** Invest in high-quality **artificial flowers for wedding** use. Look for blooms with realistic texture and color variation. Silks and high-end polyblends are your best friends.
- **Mix and Match:** Combine faux flowers with dried elements (like pampas grass or dried palm leaves) for added texture and a more organic feel.
- **Plan Ahead:** Create your centerpieces and bouquets weeks in advance. This reduces last-minute stress and allows you to perfect your design.
- **Consider Weight:** A large **wedding flower wall backdrop** can be heavy. Ensure you have a sturdy stand or wall support. Practice assembling it before the big day.
- **Store with Care:** After the wedding, gently clean your artificial flowers with a soft brush or a hairdryer on cool, and store them in airtight containers. You can sell them or repurpose them for future events.
