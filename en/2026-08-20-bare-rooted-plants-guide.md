# Bare Rooted Plants: What They Are and How to Plant Them

Have you ever received a plant that looks like nothing more than a bundle of roots? That’s a bare-rooted plant. While they might seem unusual at first, they’re actually a traditional, sustainable, and highly effective way to grow healthy plants. For DIY brides and wedding planners, bare-rooted plants offer an affordable and eco-friendly option for creating beautiful garden weddings or even as inspiration for **fake flowers centerpiece** designs. Let’s dive into what makes them so special.

## What Are Bare-Rooted Plants?

Bare-rooted plants are simply plants that are sold without soil around their roots. When the plant is one year old, growers remove it from the ground, shake off the soil, and sell it with bare roots exposed. This method is nothing new—it’s how plants were traditionally sold before plastic nursery pots became the norm. Because these plants have already flowered once and spent a year developing strong roots, they are proven to be healthy and vigorous.

## Why Choose Bare-Rooted Plants?

There are several compelling reasons to choose bare-rooted plants over potted ones, especially if you’re planning a garden wedding or adding greenery to your event decor.

### Stronger Roots and Faster Growth

Bare-rooted plants have a head start. Their roots are stronger because they’ve had an extra year to grow in the nursery. Once planted, they root much faster than potted plants because the roots are in direct contact with the soil. This means you’ll see vigorous growth from the very first season—perfect for creating lush wedding backdrops on a budget.

### Resilient and Sustainable

These plants are less sensitive to dry, cold, or wet periods, making them more forgiving for beginner gardeners. Plus, they’re incredibly sustainable: no plastic pots, no excess packaging, and a higher survival rate. For eco-conscious wedding planners, this aligns perfectly with the growing trend toward sustainable weddings.

## How to Plant Bare-Rooted Plants: Step-by-Step

Planting bare-rooted plants is easy, even if you’re new to gardening. Follow these simple tips for the best results.

1. **Choose Your Spot**: You can plant them in flower pots or directly in the open ground. Ensure the location has well-draining soil and adequate sunlight.
2. **Dig the Right Hole**: Make a hole that is twice the diameter of the root spread. This gives every root plenty of room to establish.
3. **Position Correctly**: Always point the roots downward. If the root has a branch or green sprout, make sure it sticks out above the soil. If it’s a single large root, place it ‘nose’ up, just below the surface.
4. **Cover with Fresh Soil**: Use fresh soil that contains about 60 days of plant food. Gently firm the soil around the roots.
5. **Water Wisely**: After planting, water only if the soil is dry. Keep the soil slightly moist—if it doesn’t rain, water as needed. Avoid overwatering.
6. **Fertilize at the Right Time**: Once the growth and flowering period begins, use organic garden fertiliser or food pellets to boost health.
