## What Are Real Touch Wedding Flowers?

Real Touch flowers are high-quality faux florals designed to look and feel incredibly realistic. Unlike traditional silk flowers, modern Real Touch florals have natural texture and movement, photograph beautifully, and mimic real blooms almost perfectly. Many clients are surprised they can't tell the difference—especially in photos. This makes them a fantastic option for brides who want the look of fresh flowers without the worry of wilting or weather damage.

## Fresh Wedding Flowers: The Classic Choice

Fresh flowers have always been the traditional option for weddings. They offer natural fragrance, organic one-of-a-kind arrangements, and seasonal beauty. However, they also come with limitations—especially in a place like the Okanagan, where heat and sourcing can affect quality and pricing. Fresh flowers are sensitive to temperature, must be prepared within days of the wedding, and are typically single-use. For outdoor weddings, they can wilt quickly, which is a major concern for summer brides.

## Real Touch vs Fresh: What's the Difference?

### Fresh Flowers

Fresh flowers are best for traditional weddings, couples who love natural fragrance, and highly seasonal designs. However, they are sensitive to heat and weather, must be prepared within days of the wedding, and are typically single-use. If you're planning an outdoor summer wedding, fresh flowers may not hold up well.

### Real Touch Flowers (Pickadilly's Specialty)

Real Touch flowers are best for luxury designs on a realistic budget, outdoor weddings (they're heat-safe), and brides who want consistency and peace of mind. Benefits include no wilting, no weather stress, and you know exactly what your flowers will look like ahead of time. They're also reusable and more sustainable, making them a great choice for eco-conscious couples. They're ideal for large installations like floral pillars, arches, and aisle flowers.

## Practical Tips for Choosing Your Wedding Flowers

- **Consider your venue**: For outdoor weddings, Real Touch flowers are a safer bet. They won't wilt in the sun or droop in the heat.
- **Think about your budget**: If you want a high-end look without the high-end price, Real Touch rentals offer significant savings.
- **Look at photos**: Ask to see real wedding photos of Real Touch arrangements. You'll likely be amazed at how realistic they look.
- **Mix and match**: Some couples choose fresh flowers for bouquets and Real Touch for large installations like arches or backdrop walls. This can balance cost and fragrance.
- **Plan ahead**: With Real Touch, you can book early and know exactly what you're getting. No last-minute surprises.
