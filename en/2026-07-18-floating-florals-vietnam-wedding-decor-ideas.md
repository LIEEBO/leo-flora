## Practical Tips for Recreating This Look

- **Choose the Right Materials**: Opt for silk or latex flowers that have a natural sheen and soft touch. Avoid cheap plastic—they look shiny and fake.
- **Lighting Matters**: Overhead florals look best with warm, directional lighting. Use uplights or fairy lights to highlight the textures.
- **Scale and Balance**: Don’t overcrowd the space. Floating flowers should feel airy, not heavy. Test your installation with a small mock-up first.
- **DIY Hanging Techniques**: Use clear fishing line or thin wire to suspend flowers from hooks or grids. Make sure your venue allows ceiling attachments.
- **Mix Real and Faux**: For a hybrid approach, use faux flowers for hard-to-reach spots and real blooms for close-up areas like the bouquet or cake table.
