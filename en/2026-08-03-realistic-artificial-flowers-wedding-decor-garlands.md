When you're planning a wedding, every detail matters—especially the flowers. Fresh blooms are beautiful, but they come with a hefty price tag and a short lifespan. That's why more DIY brides and wedding planners are turning to realistic artificial flowers. From real feel garlands to lifelike wreaths, faux florals have evolved into stunning, versatile decor that can transform any venue into a dreamy space.

If you've ever searched for wedding venue decoration ideas, you've likely seen the magic of real touch greenery. These products go by many names—real feel, PE, realistic, true-to-life, or faux—but they all share one thing: they look incredibly real. In this guide, we'll explore how you can use these decorations to create a warm, inviting atmosphere for your big day, without the stress of wilting petals or constant maintenance.

## Why Choose Realistic Artificial Flowers for Your Wedding?

Realistic artificial flowers have come a long way from the stiff, plastic-looking blooms of the past. Today's real feel garlands and wreaths are designed to mimic the look and texture of fresh greenery—so closely that your guests might not even notice they're faux. This makes them a perfect choice for couples who want the beauty of nature but with the practicality of a long-lasting keepsake.

One of the biggest advantages is durability. Unlike fresh greenery, these garlands won't wilt or dry out, even if your wedding is outdoors in the summer heat or indoors under bright lights. You can set them up days in advance, saving you last-minute stress. Plus, they're reusable—after the wedding, you can repurpose them for your home, bridal shower, or anniversary celebration.

Another major benefit is allergy-friendliness. Many people suffer from pollen allergies, and the last thing you want is a guest sneezing through your ceremony. Realistic artificial flowers offer the beauty of greenery without the allergens, ensuring everyone can enjoy the day comfortably.

## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas with Garlands and Wreaths

When it comes to wedding venue decoration ideas, garlands and wreaths are incredibly versatile. They can be used in countless ways to add elegance and warmth to any space. Here are some of our favorite ideas:

- **Archways and Ceremony Backdrops**: Drape a lush, 5 to 15-foot real feel garland over an archway or create a stunning backdrop for your vows. The lifelike texture will frame your moment perfectly.
- **Mantel and Table Runners**: Lay a garland along a fireplace mantel or down the center of a banquet table. Mix in candles, lanterns, or silk flowers for a romantic tablescape.
- **Wreath Place Settings**: Use smaller wreaths as unique placemats or hang them on chairs for a whimsical touch. You can even add a personalized monogram or ribbon.
- **Welcome Signs and Photo Booths**: Hang a wreath on your welcome sign or create a photo booth backdrop with a mix of garlands and wreaths. Your guests will love the photo opportunities.

### Sizing and Styles for Every Venue

Garlands come in lengths from 5 to 15 feet, giving you flexibility to cover large spaces or create intimate accents. You can choose from pine, cedar, or a mix of both—each brings its own character. Cedar has a softer, more delicate look, while pine is fuller and more traditional. For a truly custom feel, mix them together.

Wreaths range from 24 to 36 inches in diameter and are available in oval or round shapes. Round wreaths are classic, while oval wreaths offer a modern twist. You can also select lit or unlit versions. If you're planning an evening event, lit garlands and wreaths add a magical glow without the need for extra lighting fixtures.

## Practical Tips for Using Faux Florals in Your Wedding Decor

Using realistic artificial flowers is all about strategy. Here are some practical tips to get the most out of your investment:

1. **Mix Textures**: Combine real feel greenery with silk flowers wedding decor like roses, peonies, or hydrangeas. The contrast between the soft petals and the lifelike leaves creates depth and visual interest.
2. **Think Beyond the Ceremony**: Use garlands to line the aisle, drape over staircases, or frame doorways. Wreaths can be hung on the back of the bride and groom's chairs or used as table centerpieces.
3. **Plan for Reuse**: After the wedding, donate your decor to a local church, community center, or sell it online. Many brides recoup a portion of their costs this way.
4. **Lighting Matters**: If you choose unlit garlands, consider adding fairy lights or candles to create a warm ambiance. The right lighting can make faux florals look even more realistic.
5. **Store Carefully**: After the event, gently pack your garlands and wreaths in storage bins to keep them dust-free. They'll be ready for your next celebration or to brighten your home.
