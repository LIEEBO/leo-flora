## Introduction
There's something undeniably magical about a rustic kitchen—warm wood, open shelving, and the gentle scent of herbs hanging from the beams. Celebrity chef Clodagh McKenna recently unveiled her dreamy kitchen renovation, and the standout detail? Bunches of dried lavender suspended from the ceiling, adding a fairytale-like charm. As a wedding and event decorator, you know that the right floral touches can transform any space, and this trend is perfect for bringing that same enchantment to your own home or event venue.

But is dried lavender a practical choice for your kitchen? We spoke to interior experts to find out—and the answer is a resounding yes. Plus, we'll show you how to incorporate faux floral arrangements and wholesale silk flowers for a low-maintenance, long-lasting look.

## Why Dried Lavender Works in the Kitchen
Dried lavender isn't just pretty—it's functional. According to Ntola Obazee, creative director at Kutchenhaus, lavender adds both scent and visual interest to a kitchen. 'It creates texture and provides a gentle scent, which is great for covering old food smells,' she explains. 'It also has the added benefit of being a great low-maintenance alternative to flowers, which can be enjoyed for months to come.'

This makes it an ideal choice for busy homeowners or event spaces where you want a natural touch without constant upkeep. Plus, the soft purple hues bring a calming, rustic vibe that pairs beautifully with wood and stone.

## How to Get the Look: Styling Tips from Experts
To recreate Clodagh's look, start by hanging bunches of dried lavender from ceiling beams, pot racks, or open shelving. She spaced them between pots and pans for a cohesive, curated feel. If you don't have beams, consider draping lavender over a cabinet door or along a rail above your worktop.

For a more permanent solution, consider mixing dried lavender with faux floral arrangements. Realistic artificial flowers can mimic the look of fresh herbs without the worry of wilting or dust. Choose high-quality silk flowers that blend seamlessly with your decor.

## Mixing Dried Herbs with Faux Florals for Lasting Beauty
While dried lavender is beautiful, it won't last forever. To extend the life of your decor, combine it with wholesale silk flowers. You can create stunning displays that look fresh all year round. For example, pair a bunch of dried lavender with realistic artificial eucalyptus or olive branches for a rich, textured arrangement.

When sourcing materials, look for wholesale silk flowers to save money if you're decorating multiple spaces or events. Many suppliers offer bulk pricing, making it easy to stock up on stems for centerpieces, wreaths, or wall hangings.

## Practical Tips for DIY Decorators and Wedding Planners
- **Choose your placement wisely:** Hang lavender where it won't be disturbed, like above a sink or on a high shelf. Avoid areas with high moisture, as it can cause mold.
- **Mix textures:** Combine dried lavender with faux floral arrangements that include berries, leaves, or grasses for depth.
- **Use command hooks:** If you're renting or can't drill into beams, use adhesive hooks that can hold light bunches.
- **Refresh scent:** To revive the lavender's fragrance, gently crush the buds or add a few drops of lavender essential oil to the bunch.
- **Consider longevity:** For events, use realistic artificial flowers as the base and add fresh or dried herbs for fragrance—guests will appreciate the beauty without the mess.
