# Coastal Garden Wedding Decor Ideas from Castle Hill Inn

If you’re dreaming of a wedding that feels like a breezy summer afternoon by the sea, look no further than the timeless elegance of a Newport coastal garden party. One recent celebration at the historic Castle Hill Inn perfectly captured this spirit—with a romantic, airy design that blended classic formality with a lush floral-forward aesthetic. The ceremony overlooked the sparkling Narragansett Bay, setting the stage for a day that felt both grand and intimate.

For DIY brides and wedding planners, this real wedding is a goldmine of inspiration. In this article, we’ll break down the key decor elements and share practical tips for recreating the look—whether you’re working with fresh blooms or exploring budget-friendly alternatives like artificial flowers for wedding decor.

## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas for a Coastal Garden Theme

The Castle Hill Inn’s 40-acre estate provided a stunning natural backdrop, but the decor truly elevated the experience. The wedding colors—white, ivory, green, and pale blue—echoed the surrounding seascape and created a serene, sophisticated palette.

To bring this look to your own venue, focus on:

- **Layered textures**: Mix linens, lace, and natural wood to add depth.
- **Greenery-heavy installations**: Think garlands along ceremony arches or draped over reception tables.
- **Soft, airy florals**: Use white and ivory blooms (like roses, hydrangeas, and peonies) accented with pale blue delphinium or thistle.
- **Natural elements**: Incorporate driftwood, seashells, or smooth stones for a subtle nod to the shore.

For planners, these wedding venue decoration ideas work beautifully in ballrooms, tents, or outdoor spaces. The key is to let the landscape guide your choices—just as this wedding did.

## Using Artificial Flowers for Wedding Decor on a Budget

One of the biggest challenges for DIY brides is staying within budget while achieving a lush, high-end look. That’s where artificial flowers for wedding decor come in. High-quality silk or faux flowers can mimic the elegance of fresh blooms without the worry of wilting or seasonal availability.

For a coastal garden theme, consider:

- **Faux white roses and peonies** for centerpieces and bouquets.
- **Faux greenery** like eucalyptus or ivy for arches and table runners.
- **Pale blue faux hydrangeas** to add that pop of color without the cost.

Artificial flowers are especially practical for destination weddings or outdoor venues where weather can be unpredictable. They also allow you to prep decor weeks in advance—a huge time-saver for planners.

## Creating a Stunning Fake Flowers Centerpiece

Centerpieces are the heart of reception decor. For a coastal garden look, a fake flowers centerpiece can be just as show-stopping as fresh ones. Here’s how to design one:

- **Choose a low, wide container** in glass, ceramic, or even a wooden box to keep the arrangement airy.
- **Start with a base of faux greenery**—ivy, ferns, or olive branches.
- **Add focal flowers** like large white silk roses or peonies.
- **Tuck in smaller accents** like pale blue faux thistle or baby’s breath.
- **Finish with a few natural elements**—a piece of driftwood or a single seashell for coastal charm.

Pro tip: Mix real and faux elements if you’re on the fence. For example, use real greenery (which is often inexpensive) with faux flowers to cut costs while keeping the look fresh.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

Recreating a Castle Hill Inn–inspired wedding doesn’t require a historic estate or a six-figure budget. Here are actionable tips:

1. **Prioritize one or two statement pieces**—like a ceremony arch or head table backdrop—and keep the rest simple.
2. **Rent or buy pre-made faux arrangements** from online retailers specializing in wedding decor. Many offer rental options for centerpieces and bouquets.
3. **Test your color palette** with fabric swatches and flower samples before committing. Natural light can change how colors appear.
4. **Consider the guest experience**: Coastal garden weddings often feature outdoor cocktails or lawn games. Make sure your decor doesn’t obstruct views or movement.
5. **Work with your venue’s strengths**. If you have a water view, keep centerpieces low so guests can see the scenery.

## Conclusion

The magic of a Newport summer wedding lies in its effortless blend of nature and elegance. By borrowing design cues from the Castle Hill Inn celebration—soft colors, lush florals, and coastal touches—you can create a day that feels both timeless and personal. And with smart choices like artificial flowers for wedding decor and a well-designed fake flowers centerpiece, you can achieve that dreamy look without breaking the bank.

Whether you’re a DIY bride planning your own big day or a wedding planner seeking fresh wedding venue decoration ideas, remember: the best decor tells a story. Let the sea, the sky, and your love guide the way.
