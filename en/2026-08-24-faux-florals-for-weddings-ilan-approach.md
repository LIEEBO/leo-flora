## Practical Tips for Using Faux Florals in Your Wedding

### 1. Mix Textures and Heights

Don't just stick to one type of flower. Combine large statement blooms (like peonies or hydrangeas) with delicate fillers (like baby's breath or eucalyptus). Vary the heights of your centerpieces—tall arrangements for reception tables, low clusters for intimate dinners. This adds depth and visual interest.

### 2. Choose a Cohesive Color Palette

Select 2-3 main colors and one accent. For a classic look, go with white, cream, and greenery. For a bold vibe, try jewel tones like burgundy and emerald. Keep the palette consistent across your bridal bouquet, centerpieces, and ceremony arch to create a unified aesthetic.

### 3. Invest in Quality for Key Pieces

Your bouquet and the couple's table centerpiece are the most photographed items. Splurge on high-quality faux floral arrangements for these. Use more budget-friendly options for less visible areas like restrooms or hallway accents.

### 4. Prep in Advance

One of the biggest advantages of fake flowers is you can assemble everything days or even weeks before the wedding. This frees you up on the big day to focus on getting ready, not on flower logistics.

### 5. Reuse and Repurpose

After the wedding, don't let your faux florals gather dust. Use them to decorate your new home—they'll last for years, reminding you of your special day every time you see them.
