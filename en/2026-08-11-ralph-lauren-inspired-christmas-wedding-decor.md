# Ralph Lauren-Inspired Christmas Décor: The Look Everyone’s Talking About

This holiday season, classic elegance is making a comeback—and it’s all about that timeless, cozy-meets-refined aesthetic often seen in the world of Ralph Lauren. Think rich plaids, shimmering gold accents, and lush greenery layered with red velvet ribbon and candlelight. It’s sophisticated, warm, and perfectly festive—and it’s taking over holiday décor trends everywhere.

But this trend isn’t just for the living room. For wedding planners and DIY brides, this designer-inspired style offers a gorgeous, cohesive theme for winter weddings. And the best part? You can achieve it with **affordable wedding flowers** and **artificial flowers for wedding** designs that look just as lush as fresh blooms—without the wilting or the hefty price tag.

## What Is “Ralph Lauren–Inspired” Wedding Décor?

This look blends classic American heritage style with luxurious textures and color palettes. Picture dark navy and hunter-green tartan ribbons, gleaming gold nutcrackers, velvet bows, and accents of red and metallic finishes. It’s equal parts rustic lodge and Park Avenue sophistication—perfect for a winter wedding that feels both grand and intimate.

Key features of this trend include:

- **Tartan Plaids:** Green, navy, and red patterns that evoke equestrian and country-club charm.
- **Gold Accents:** Metallic nutcrackers, reindeer, and candle holders that elevate your tablescape.
- **Velvet & Ribbon:** Deep, rich textures that add warmth and dimension to bouquets and centerpieces.
- **Natural Greenery:** Lush garlands, wreaths, and cedar sprays bring balance to the glamour.
- **Classic Candlelight:** Etched glass or metallic votives cast a golden, nostalgic glow.

## Why This Trend Is Perfect for Weddings

It’s more than just a color scheme—it’s a feeling. The Ralph Lauren–inspired look blends nostalgia with refinement, offering a sense of tradition and comfort that feels right at home in today’s cozy-luxury interiors. It photographs beautifully (hello, Pinterest-ready!), mixes seamlessly with heirloom ornaments, and works in both modern and traditional venues.

This year, designers and couples alike are embracing:

- **Statement ceremony backdrops**—think a **wedding flower wall backdrop** in deep greens and tartan ribbons.
- **Mantels and head tables** draped in pine and cedar, accented with gold deer and velvet bows.
- **Oversized nutcrackers** standing guard at the entrance or beside the cake table.
- **Candlelight** reflecting off mirrored trays and brass décor for that signature glow.

## How to Incorporate the Look with Artificial Flowers

One of the smartest ways to achieve this luxurious style on a budget is by using **artificial flowers for wedding** décor. High-quality silk and faux florals mimic the look of fresh greenery and blooms perfectly, and they can be prepared weeks in advance—saving you stress and money.

Here are some practical ways to weave this trend into your wedding:

### 1. Build a Wedding Flower Wall Backdrop

A **wedding flower wall backdrop** is a showstopper for your ceremony or photo booth. Use faux greenery panels, then layer in red velvet bows, gold picks, and tartan ribbon. Because it’s artificial, you can install it days ahead and reuse it for multiple events.

### 2. Create Lush Table Runners and Centerpieces

Instead of expensive fresh garlands, opt for pre-lit faux pine garlands. Weave in gold bells, velvet ribbon, and berry picks. Add candle holders with mercury glass votives for that warm, designer glow.

### 3. Design Bridal Bouquets with a Vintage Twist

Carry a bouquet of faux red roses, navy ranunculus, and eucalyptus, wrapped in a plaid ribbon. It will look stunning in photos and last forever as a keepsake.

### 4. Style the Ceremony Aisle

Line the aisle with faux cedar sprays and gold lanterns. You can rent or buy these pieces, and they won’t wilt during a long ceremony.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

- **Mix textures:** Layer plaids, velvet, and metallics for depth. Start with a base of greenery, then add ribbon, picks, and ornaments.
- **Invest in quality faux flowers:** Look for “real-touch” or “silk” varieties that have natural veining and color variation.
- **Use affordable wedding flowers strategically:** Splurge on the bouquet and focal arrangements; save on aisle décor and backdrops with artificial options.
- **Lighting is key:** Warm fairy lights, candles, or lanterns will elevate even the simplest décor.
- **Plan for reuse:** After the wedding, repurpose the flower wall as a backdrop for your thank-you photos or holiday parties.
