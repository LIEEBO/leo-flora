## DIY Bridal Bouquet with Artificial Flowers: A Step-by-Step Guide

Your wedding bouquet is one of the most photographed details of your big day. Making your own bouquet not only saves money but gives you the satisfaction of creating something beautiful and personal. With artificial flowers, your bouquet can last forever as a keepsake. In this guide, we'll show you how to craft a stunning bridal bouquet using sola wood flowers, preserved greenery, and dried fillers.

## Why Choose Artificial Flowers for Your Wedding?

Artificial flowers offer incredible advantages for weddings. They don't wilt, they're budget-friendly, and you can create them well in advance without worrying about freshness. ## Supplies and Tools Needed

Before you start, gather these essentials:

- Sola wood flowers
- Preserved eucalyptus
- Dried star flowers
- Dusty Miller
- Leather fern
- Bouquet grass
- Cutting shears
- Glue gun
- Floral tape
- Floral wire
- Burlap twine

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Dye the Sola Wood Flowers

Pour water into a bowl and add sola softener, stirring thoroughly. Add sola dye and mix well. Dip-dye the flowers and let them dry for at least 24 hours. This step allows you to customize colors to match your wedding palette perfectly.

### Step 2: Stem the Sola Wood Flowers

Apply hot glue to the tip of a floral wire and carefully push it into the base of each flower. Hold for a moment until the glue sets. Stem all flowers before arranging to streamline the process.

### Step 3: Start Arranging the Bouquet

Pick three or four blooms to form the base of your bouquet. Add more flowers around the original blooms, turning the bouquet as you go to build layers from the center out. Wrap with floral tape as you work for added support.

### Step 4: Add Greenery and Fillers

Start with eucalyptus leaves to add height, scattering them evenly. Add preserved leather ferns and dusty miller leaves. For volume, insert a generous bunch of dried star flowers. Once you're happy with the size, wrap the stems tightly with floral tape.

### Step 5: Finish the Handle

Surround the handle with bouquet grass for a natural look and secure with floral tape. Then apply glue at the top and wrap burlap twine around the handle until fully covered. Secure the ends with hot glue.

### Step 6: Trim the Stems

Cut any unruly ends to make the bottom even. The handle should be comfortable to carry—not too long, not too short.

## Tips for Success

- **Plan your color palette**: Dye sola flowers in colors that complement your wedding theme.
- **Work in layers**: Build your bouquet gradually, turning it as you add stems.
- **Mix textures**: Combine different greenery and fillers for a lush, professional look.
- **Secure everything**: Use floral tape generously to keep stems in place.
