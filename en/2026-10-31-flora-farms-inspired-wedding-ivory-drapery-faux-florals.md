## Introduction

When Alex and Patrick celebrated their black-tie wedding at Flora Farms in San José del Cabo, the venue was transformed like never before. Amy Abbott Events wrapped the open-air reception in sweeping ivory drapery, paired with serpentine tables, dark wood chairs, and candlelight. Cristina Gonzalez kept the calla lilies and hydrangeas sculptural, allowing the gardens to shine. This stunning celebration is full of inspiration for DIY brides and wedding planners looking to create a similar look—without the high cost of fresh flowers.

In this article, we’ll break down the key elements of this reimagined Flora Farms wedding and show you how to achieve the same effortless elegance using affordable wedding flowers, faux floral arrangements, and a show-stopping wedding flower wall backdrop.

## The Magic of Ivory Drapery and Candlelight

Ivory drapery is a timeless choice for open-air receptions. It softens the space, adds a sense of romance, and reflects candlelight beautifully. To recreate this look, consider using lightweight, flowing fabrics like chiffon or voile. Drape them from pergolas, tent frames, or even trees to create a dreamy atmosphere. Pair with plenty of candles—votives, pillars, and tapers—to cast a warm glow as the sun sets.

**Tip:** Use battery-operated candles if your venue has restrictions on open flames. They look just as lovely and are safer for drapery.

## Sculptural Florals with Calla Lilies and Hydrangeas

Cristina Gonzalez’s approach to florals was all about structure and simplicity. Calla lilies and hydrangeas were arranged in a sculptural way, letting their natural shapes take center stage. To mimic this with faux flowers, choose high-quality artificial calla lilies and hydrangeas that have realistic textures and colors. Group them in clusters of three or five stems for maximum impact, and use a mix of heights to create visual interest.

**Tip:** For a modern look, place single stems in sleek ceramic vases or use them to line the center of serpentine tables. Faux floral arrangements can be prepared days in advance, saving you time and stress on the wedding day.

## Creating a Wedding Flower Wall Backdrop

A wedding flower wall backdrop is a stunning focal point for ceremonies and receptions alike. For a Flora Farms-inspired look, consider a backdrop of ivory drapery with a floral installation of calla lilies and hydrangeas. You can create this using a foam or wire frame covered with faux flowers, or hire a professional to install a rental wall.

**Tip:** If you’re DIY-ing, start with a backdrop stand and attach a fabric panel. Then, use zip ties or floral wire to secure clusters of faux flowers in a cascading or asymmetrical pattern. This backdrop works beautifully behind the sweetheart table or as a photo booth background.
