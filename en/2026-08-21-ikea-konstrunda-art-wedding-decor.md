## Why IKEA KONSTRUNDA is a Game-Changer for Wedding Decor

The KONSTRUNDA range, whose name means 'art tour' in Swedish, features 22 objects designed by seven different artists and designers. From decorative pieces to functional furniture with a playful twist, this collection is all about making art accessible. Karin Gustavsson, the collection's creative leader, emphasizes that 'art should be available for all to enjoy, not a luxury reserved for galleries.' For wedding planners, this means you can source unique, artistic elements that will make your event stand out, all while staying within budget.
