When you're planning a wedding, every detail matters—especially the flowers. But fresh greenery can be expensive, high-maintenance, and short-lived. That's why savvy DIY brides and wedding planners are turning to **real feel garlands and wreaths**. These lifelike beauties offer the lush look of fresh foliage without the stress. In this guide, we'll explore how these versatile pieces can transform your wedding decor while keeping your budget happy.

## Why Choose Real Feel Garlands and Wreaths?

Real feel (also called real touch, PE, or realistic) garlands and wreaths are crafted to mimic the texture and appearance of fresh greenery. They've become a favorite among influencers and wedding pros alike. Here's why:

- **Lifelike Appeal**: They look and feel like the real thing, bringing warmth and elegance to any setting.
- **Durability**: Unlike fresh greenery, they won't wilt or dry out. Your decor stays perfect from the rehearsal dinner to the last dance.
- **Allergy-Friendly**: Perfect for guests with pollen sensitivities—beauty without the sneezes.
- **Easy Storage**: After the big day, pack them away for anniversaries or future events.
- **No Maintenance**: No watering, pruning, or worrying about drooping petals.

These features make them ideal for **affordable wedding flowers**—you get the high-end look without the high-end price tag.

## Garlands: Versatile Decor for Every Setting

Garlands are the ultimate multitaskers in wedding decor. Available in lengths from 5 to 15 feet, they can be draped, wrapped, or hung to create stunning visual impact.

- **Style Choices**: Pine, cedar, or a mix of both—select the vibe that matches your theme.
- **Lighting Options**: Choose lit or unlit versions. Lit garlands add a magical glow to evening receptions or outdoor ceremonies.

Use garlands to:

- Adorn the head table or sweetheart table
- Frame an arch or backdrop
- Line the aisle or staircase
- Accent a fireplace mantel (perfect for winter weddings)

For a cohesive look, pair your garlands with **silk flowers wedding decor**—think faux roses, peonies, or eucalyptus sprigs tucked into the greenery.

## Wreaths: Elegant Accents That Wow

Wreaths aren't just for front doors! They make stunning focal points in wedding decor.

- **Sizes**: From 24 to 36 inches—choose based on where you'll place them.
- **Shapes**: Round is classic, oval adds a modern twist.
- **Styles**: Pine, cedar, or mixed—pick to match your garlands.
- **Lighting**: Again, lit or unlit options available.

Get creative with wreaths:

- Hang them on ceremony chairs or pew ends
- Use as table centerpieces (lay flat and place a candle inside)
- Create a photo backdrop wall with multiple wreaths
- Adorn the gift table or guest book station

Because they're lightweight and easy to hang, wreaths are perfect for **faux floral arrangements** that you can set up ahead of time and reuse for other events.

## Practical Tips for Using Real Feel Greenery in Weddings

### 1. Mix Textures for Realism

Combine real feel garlands with other faux elements like velvet ribbons, berries, or metallic accents. This adds depth and makes the greenery look even more authentic.

### 2. Plan Your Layout

Measure your spaces before buying. Know how many feet of garland you need for each table or arch. For wreaths, consider the diameter and where they'll hang.

### 3. Consider the Season

While pine and cedar are perfect for winter weddings, you can also find eucalyptus or olive leaf versions for spring and summer. Choose greenery that complements your color palette.

### 4. Light It Up

If your reception is in the evening, opt for lit garlands and wreaths. They create a romantic ambiance and save you from buying extra string lights.

### 5. Store and Reuse

After the wedding, gently clean and store your greenery in a cool, dry place. You can repurpose them for baby showers, birthdays, or even your own home decor.
