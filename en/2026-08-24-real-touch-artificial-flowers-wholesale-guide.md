## What Are Real Touch Artificial Flowers?

Real Touch artificial flowers are premium faux blooms made with soft-touch coatings—like latex or polyurethane (PU)—that mimic the feel of real petals. Unlike standard silk flowers, these have a slightly rubbery, velvety texture that makes them convincing up close. For weddings, where guests may touch the decor, this realism is a game-changer.

### How They're Made

- **Latex coating**: Gives a soft, petal-like feel—perfect for roses, tulips, and orchids.
- **PU coating**: Slightly firmer, offering more durability for high-traffic areas.
- **Foam or fabric core**: Helps the flower hold its shape and recover after being packed.
- **Mixed materials**: Some stems combine Real Touch petals with silk leaves or plastic stems for balance.

Understanding these materials helps you choose the right flowers for your project and ensures you're not overpaying for something that won't hold up.

## Why Choose Artificial Flowers for Weddings?

- **Durable**: No wilting, no water needed, and they can be prepped weeks in advance.
- **Reusable**: After the wedding, you can repurpose them for your home or sell them to recoup costs.

## Wholesale Buying Tips for Real Touch Flowers

If you're buying for a wedding or event business, wholesale is the way to go. Here are practical tips to ensure you get the best value:

### 1. Understand MOQs and Lead Times

Most suppliers require a minimum order quantity (MOQ) of 500–576 pieces per style. Samples typically ship in about 15 days, and bulk production takes 60–90 days after approval. Plan accordingly!

### 2. Ask About the Coating

Don't assume all Real Touch flowers are the same. Ask your supplier if they use latex, PU, or a blend. This affects not only the feel but also how they hold up in shipping and handling.

### 3. Check the Packing and Shipping

Because these flowers are soft-touch, they can be crushed in transit. Ensure your supplier uses protective packing and carton marks. Sea freight (FCL or LCL) is standard, with FOB Xiamen being a common trade term.

### 4. Request Samples First

Always order samples before committing to a large order. This lets you check the petal feel, color accuracy, and stem strength. A reputable supplier will be happy to send them.
