## How to Choose the Right Size Artificial Tree for Your Space

Choosing the perfect artificial tree or plant for your home—or your wedding venue—can feel overwhelming. You fall in love with a gorgeous faux fiddle leaf fig, order it online, and wait with excitement… only to discover it's too tall, too wide, or just doesn't fit the vibe. Whether you're decorating a cozy corner or planning a grand event, this guide will help you measure, place, and style artificial greenery like a pro.

### Step 1: Decide Where Your Tree or Plant Will Live

Before you start browsing, pick the exact spot. Is it a focal point or a subtle accent? Will it be seen from seated or standing eye level? Here are some ideas for different spaces—from your living room to your wedding altar.

**Living Room** – A large faux fiddle leaf fig or bird of paradise makes a striking anchor beside a sofa or in a corner. For weddings, consider using these as aisle markers or backdrop accents.

**Foyer or Entrance** – Tall, slim faux olive or bamboo trees create a grand first impression. In a wedding venue, they can line the ceremony aisle or frame the entrance.

**Home Office** – Small faux palms or snake plants add a touch of greenery that boosts creativity. For a wedding planning workspace, they keep the vibe fresh.

**Kitchen Nook** – A colorful faux arrangement in a terracotta planter adds cheer without taking up space. These also work as charming table accents for a rustic wedding.

**Small Spaces** – Even a dorm or studio can benefit from compact faux plants with slim bases. For wedding favors or table decor, small potted faux succulents are perfect.

> **Pro Tip:** Faux plants soften harsh lines and bring a calming, natural balance to any décor—home or event.

### Step 2: How to Measure for an Artificial Tree or Plant

Measuring correctly prevents the dreaded “too big” or “too small” mistake. Here’s a simple method:

- **Height:** Measure from the floor to the ceiling, then subtract about 12–18 inches for breathing room. For a tabletop plant, measure from the table surface upward.
- **Width:** Measure the widest part of the plant (usually the leaves). Ensure it won’t block walkways or doorways.
- **Pot Size:** The pot should be proportional—about 1/3 the height of the plant for tall trees, or 1/2 for bushy plants.

For wedding decor, think about sightlines. A tall artificial tree behind the sweetheart table can frame the couple beautifully, but it shouldn’t block guests’ view.

### Step 3: Match the Style to Your Décor

Your artificial greenery should blend with your existing style—or your wedding theme. Here’s how to match:

- **Minimalist:** Choose clean, sculptural plants like a single stem monstera or a sleek bamboo.
- **Rustic:** Opt for olive trees, eucalyptus bundles, or boxwood topiaries in natural pots.
- **Glam:** Go for glossy fiddle leaf figs or white orchids in metallic planters.
- **Eclectic:** Mix different styles—a modern snake plant next to a vintage-inspired rose bush.

### Step 4: Practical Tips for Placement and Care

- **Center of Attention:** Some faux plants are subtle, while others—like large arrangements—command attention. Decide if you want a statement piece or a background accent.
- **Furniture:** Make sure the tree or plant doesn’t crowd your furniture. Leave at least 6 inches of space between the plant and a wall or sofa.
- **Safety:** Keep artificial trees away from fireplaces, candles, or areas where small children might pull them over. Secure tall trees with a sturdy base.
- **Pets:** Some realistic faux plants might tempt your pets to nibble. Discourage them from getting too close.
- **Theme:** Whether your wedding is boho, classic, or modern, there’s a faux tree to match. Stay consistent with your color palette and textures.
