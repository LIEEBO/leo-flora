## What Does a Wedding Stylist Do? A Guide for DIY Brides and Planners

Planning a wedding is exciting, but it can also feel overwhelming. Between choosing the venue, the dress, and the menu, there's a whole world of décor decisions that can make or break the look of your big day. That's where a wedding stylist comes in. But what exactly does a wedding stylist do? And if you're a DIY bride or a wedding planner, how can you achieve that polished look without hiring one? Let's break it down.

### The Role of a Wedding Stylist

A wedding stylist is responsible for the overall aesthetic of your wedding. They don't just place a few vases on tables—they create a cohesive design that tells your love story. Their tasks typically include:

- **Concept development:** Understanding your vision and translating it into a design plan.
- **Ceremony décor:** Designing the altar, aisle, and entrance to set the tone.
- **Reception styling:** Tablescapes, centerpieces, lighting, and lounge areas.
- **Floral design:** Selecting and arranging flowers—fresh or artificial—to match the theme.
- **Logistics:** Coordinating delivery, setup, and pack-down so you don't have to lift a finger.

For couples in Brisbane and beyond, a stylist ensures everything looks picture-perfect from the moment guests arrive until the last dance.

### How to Style Your Wedding with Silk Flowers

Whether you're a DIY bride or a professional planner, here are some practical tips for using artificial flowers in your wedding design:

1. **Mix textures and sizes.** Combine different types of **realistic artificial flowers**—roses, peonies, eucalyptus—to create depth and interest.
2. **Focus on key areas.** Prioritize the ceremony backdrop, aisle markers, and reception centerpieces. These are the most photographed spots.
3. **Use lighting to your advantage.** Warm string lights or candles can make silk flowers glow and look even more lifelike.
4. **Reuse and repurpose.** Move ceremony arrangements to the reception to save money and reduce waste.

### What to Ask Before Hiring a Wedding Stylist

If you do decide to hire a stylist, make sure you ask these questions:

- What's included in your package? (Décor, flowers, setup, pack-down?)
- Do you work with artificial flowers? (Some stylists specialize in fresh only.)
- Can you work within my budget? (Be upfront about your limits.)
- How many weddings do you take per weekend? (Ensure you get their full attention.)
- Can I see a mood board or sample setup? (Visuals help avoid misunderstandings.)

A good stylist will be transparent and collaborative. If you're a DIY bride, you can still consult a stylist for a one-time design plan and then execute it yourself with the help of **silk flowers wedding decor**.
