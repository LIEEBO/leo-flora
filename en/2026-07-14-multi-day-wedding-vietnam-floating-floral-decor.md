# Multi-Day Wedding in Vietnam: Floating Floral Decor Ideas

Planning a wedding that blends tradition with modern design can feel overwhelming—but when done right, the result is breathtaking. Nhu and Long’s multi-day celebration in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, featured a jaw-dropping contemporary ceremony and reception at Gem, with flowers floating overhead. If you’re a DIY bride or wedding planner looking to replicate this ethereal look without the fresh-flower price tag, you’re in the right place. In this post, we’ll show you how to achieve that same floating floral magic using **faux floral arrangements**, **wholesale silk flowers**, and other **affordable wedding flowers**.

## Why Floating Floral Installations Work

Floating florals—suspended from ceilings, arches, or even tree branches—create an immersive, romantic atmosphere. They draw guests’ eyes upward and make a space feel larger and more magical. In Nhu and Long’s wedding, the overhead blooms added a sense of whimsy and luxury. The best part? You don’t need a tropical venue or an unlimited budget. With the right materials, you can create a similar effect anywhere.

## Recreate the Look with Faux Floral Arrangements

Fresh flowers for large installations can cost thousands and wilt within hours. That’s where **faux floral arrangements** shine. High-quality silk blooms look incredibly realistic and last forever—perfect for multi-day events. For a floating design, choose lightweight stems like baby’s breath, spray roses, or wisteria. Use clear fishing line to suspend them from the ceiling at varying heights. Group clusters of three to five stems for visual depth. Pro tip: mix in a few larger blooms like peonies or hydrangeas for drama.

## Save Big with Wholesale Silk Flowers

Buying individual stems from craft stores adds up quickly. Instead, source **wholesale silk flowers** online. Many suppliers offer bulk discounts on roses, orchids, and greenery. You can order by the case and have enough for multiple installations—ceremony, reception, and even the bridal party bouquets. Look for retailers that offer sample swatches so you can match colors to your wedding palette. This approach keeps your project within budget and gives you total creative control.

## Affordable Wedding Flowers: Mixing Real and Faux

Not ready to go all-faux? No problem. Many savvy brides combine real and fake blooms to save money. Use **affordable wedding flowers** like carnations, mums, or eucalyptus for ground-level arrangements (centerpieces, aisle markers), then splurge on high-quality faux for overhead installations where guests won’t touch them. This strategy keeps costs down while still delivering a lush, cohesive look. For Nhu and Long’s floating canopy, you could use faux wisteria and real greenery—just make sure the real stems are well-hydrated before hanging.

## Practical Tips for DIY Floating Florals

Ready to try it yourself? Here’s a step-by-step checklist:

1. **Choose your venue.** You need a ceiling or structure that can support weight. Check with your venue about hanging restrictions.
2. **Select lightweight faux blooms.** Avoid heavy flowers like sunflowers or large succulents. Stick to airy options: baby’s breath, lavender, or fern fronds.
3. **Use clear fishing line.** It’s strong, invisible, and easy to adjust. Attach stems with floral tape or zip ties.
4. **Create a grid.** If your ceiling is high, install a lightweight wire grid (like chicken wire) to attach lines. This makes hanging even and secure.
5. **Test your layout.** Hang a few stems first, then step back to check spacing. You want a floating cloud effect, not a dense carpet.
6. **Add lighting.** String lights or uplighting behind the florals will make them glow and hide any imperfections.

## Conclusion

Nhu and Long’s floating floral wedding proves that with a little creativity, you can achieve a high-end look without a luxury budget. By using **faux floral arrangements**, **wholesale silk flowers**, and smart **affordable wedding flowers** strategies, you can transform any space into a dreamy, romantic setting. Whether you’re planning a multi-day affair or a single-night celebration, overhead blooms are a surefire way to wow your guests. Start sourcing your materials early, practice your hanging technique, and don’t be afraid to mix real and faux. Your wedding will be just as spectacular—and your wallet will thank you.
