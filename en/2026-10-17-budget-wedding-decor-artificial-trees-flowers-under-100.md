# Budget Wedding Decor: Artificial Trees & Flowers Under £100

Planning a wedding on a budget doesn't mean sacrificing style. In fact, some of the most memorable weddings use clever, cost-effective decor that looks anything but cheap. Artificial trees and flowers are a perfect example: they deliver big visual impact, last forever, and cost a fraction of fresh florals. Whether you're a DIY bride or a wedding planner looking to maximize your budget, this guide will show you how to impress guests with stunning artificial foliage and flowers—all for under £100.

## Why Choose Artificial Trees and Flowers for Your Wedding?

Fresh flowers are beautiful, but they come with a hefty price tag and a short lifespan. Artificial alternatives offer several advantages:

- **Cost-effective:** You can reuse them for years, making them a smart investment.
- **Durable:** No wilting, no water, no last-minute panic.
- **Versatile:** Available in any color, size, and style to match your theme.
- **Allergy-friendly:** Perfect for guests with pollen sensitivities.

With realistic artificial flowers, you can achieve the lush, romantic look of a garden wedding without the garden budget. And when it comes to creating a show-stopping focal point, a wedding flower wall backdrop made from faux blooms is both affordable and Instagram-worthy.

## Styling Ideas for Artificial Trees Under £100

Artificial trees are a fantastic way to add height and drama to your venue. Here are some budget-friendly ideas:

### 1. Entrance Statement

Place a pair of artificial trees flanking the entrance to your ceremony or reception. Choose trees with delicate blossoms or lush greenery to set a romantic tone. Under £100, you can find realistic ficus, olive, or cherry blossom trees that look stunning in photos.

### 2. Aisle Decor

Line your aisle with small potted artificial trees or topiary. They're easy to move and can be repurposed as centerpieces later. Add fairy lights or ribbon for a personal touch.

### 3. Photo Backdrop

Create a magical photo area by grouping artificial trees of varying heights. Drape them with fabric and add a wedding flower wall backdrop behind them for a layered, textured look. Guests will love taking photos in front of it.

### 4. Table Centerpieces

Use small artificial trees as the base for your table decor. Surround them with candles and a fake flowers centerpiece for a cohesive look. This is where artificial flowers truly shine—you can create a lush, full arrangement without worrying about wilting.

## Creating a Wedding Flower Wall Backdrop on a Budget

A flower wall is one of the hottest trends in wedding decor, but fresh flower walls can cost thousands. With artificial flowers, you can DIY one for under £100. Here's how:

- **Choose a base:** A foam board or wooden frame works well. You can also rent a flower wall stand.
- **Select realistic artificial flowers:** Look for high-quality silk or latex flowers that mimic real petals. Mix roses, peonies, and greenery for depth.
- **Arrange and attach:** Use floral foam or a hot glue gun to secure flowers to the base. Start from the center and work outward.
- **Add lighting:** String fairy lights or use uplighting to make the wall glow.

## Fake Flowers Centerpiece Ideas That Look Real

Centerpieces are the heart of your table decor. Here are some ideas using fake flowers centerpiece arrangements that will fool even the most discerning guests:

- **Classic Rose Dome:** Cluster realistic artificial roses in a dome shape atop a pedestal vase. Add greenery for contrast.
- **Wildflower Mix:** Combine faux wildflowers, lavender, and daisies in mason jars for a rustic feel.
- **Tropical Touch:** Use artificial orchids and monstera leaves for a modern, exotic look.
- **Candlelit Glow:** Surround a fake flowers centerpiece with LED candles for a warm, romantic ambiance.

## Practical Tips for Buying Artificial Trees and Flowers

- **Set a budget:** Stick to under £100 per item or per arrangement to stay on track.
- **Check quality:** Look for UV-resistant, fade-resistant materials if your event is outdoors.
- **Order samples:** Before buying in bulk, order a sample to check color and quality.
- **Shop off-season:** Many suppliers offer discounts on artificial flowers during off-peak wedding months.
- **DIY where possible:** Assembling your own arrangements can save significantly.
