## Practical Tips for Styling Artificial Lavender

- **Mix with other textures:** Combine the lavender with eucalyptus, baby’s breath, or even dried flowers to add depth and dimension to your arrangement. The key is to vary heights and textures for a natural look.
- **Use in unexpected places:** Don’t limit yourself to tables. Place a bowl planter on a windowsill, a bathroom counter, or a shelf in the entryway. It brightens any corner without needing sunlight or water.
- **Keep it dust-free:** To maintain its fresh appearance, simply give it a gentle dusting with a soft cloth or a hairdryer on a cool setting. Avoid water or harsh cleaners.
- **Repurpose after the wedding:** If you’ve used these arrangements as centerpieces, consider donating them to a local nursing home or hospital. They’ll bring joy long after the last dance.
