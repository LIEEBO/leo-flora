## Real Feel Garlands & Wreaths: The Secret to Stunning Wedding Decor

When planning a wedding, every detail matters—especially the flowers. But fresh greenery can be costly, high-maintenance, and perishable. That’s why savvy brides and wedding planners are turning to real feel garlands and wreaths. These lifelike faux florals offer the beauty of fresh greenery without the stress. In this guide, we’ll explore how these versatile pieces can transform your ceremony and reception, and why they’re a smart choice for affordable wedding flowers.

### Why Real Feel Garlands and Wreaths Are a Wedding Must-Have

Real feel garlands and wreaths—also known as real touch, PE, or realistic faux greenery—have taken the wedding industry by storm. They’re designed to mimic the texture and appearance of fresh pine, cedar, and other foliage, making them nearly indistinguishable from the real thing. Here’s why they’re perfect for your big day:

- **Lifelike Appearance:** Guests will do a double-take, thinking your decor is fresh-cut.
- **Durability:** Unlike fresh greenery, these pieces won’t wilt, brown, or droop, even after hours of celebration.
- **Allergy-Friendly:** For couples or guests with pollen sensitivities, real feel options provide all the beauty without the sneezing.
- **Easy Storage:** After the wedding, you can repurpose them for your home or sell them to another couple—zero waste.

### How to Use Garlands and Wreaths in Wedding Decor

Garlands and wreaths are incredibly versatile. Here are some creative ways to incorporate them into your wedding:

- **Ceremony Arch:** Drape a lush garland over an arch or canopy for a romantic focal point.
- **Aisle Accents:** Line the aisle with small wreaths attached to chairs or pews.
- **Reception Tables:** Lay garlands down the center of long tables, alternating with candles and silk flowers for a lush, organic feel.
- **Mantel or Buffet Backdrop:** If your venue has a fireplace or buffet table, a garland adds instant elegance.
- **Welcome Sign:** Hang a wreath above your welcome sign or at the entrance to guide guests.

### Choosing the Right Garlands and Wreaths for Your Wedding

When selecting real feel garlands and wreaths, consider the following:

- **Length and Size:** Garlands come in lengths from 5 to 15 feet, perfect for draping or wrapping. Wreaths range from 24 to 36 inches—choose based on where they’ll hang.
- **Style:** Pine, cedar, or a mix—select based on your theme. Pine is classic, cedar is softer, and a mix adds depth.
- **Lighting:** Lit options (with warm white LEDs) create a magical glow for evening events, while unlit are ideal for daytime or minimalist looks.
- **Shape:** Wreaths are available in round or oval—oval adds a modern twist.

### Practical Tips for Using Artificial Flowers for Wedding

Real feel garlands and wreaths are just one part of your floral decor. To create a cohesive look, pair them with **silk flowers wedding decor** elements like centerpieces, bouquets, and corsages. Here are some pro tips:

- **Mix Textures:** Combine glossy magnolia leaves, matte eucalyptus, and soft silk roses for visual interest.
- **Color Coordination:** Choose greenery that complements your wedding palette—add white or blush flowers for a classic feel, or bold hues for a modern vibe.
- **Test Before You Buy:** Order samples to see the color and texture in person—especially if you’re ordering online.
- **Repurpose After the Wedding:** Use your garlands as home decor or donate them to a local church or community center.

### Why Real Feel Is a Smart Choice for Affordable Wedding Flowers

One of the biggest wedding expenses is flowers. Fresh arrangements can cost thousands—and they wilt. Real feel garlands and wreaths offer a budget-friendly alternative that looks just as stunning. Because they’re reusable, you can save money without sacrificing style. Plus, they’re perfect for **artificial flowers for wedding** setups that need to withstand outdoor weather or long photo sessions.
