# A Garden Ceremony Gave Way to an Intimate Evening at Bastide du Roy

If you're dreaming of a wedding that feels like two distinct celebrations in one day, this Bastide du Roy wedding is your perfect inspiration. The couple masterfully designed their color palette in two acts: a joyful, garden-style ceremony bursting with pink, coral, blue, and fresh green, followed by an intimate reception grounded in ivory, cream, and soft neutrals. For DIY brides and wedding planners seeking **affordable wedding flowers** and **wedding venue decoration ideas**, this real wedding offers a treasure trove of practical takeaways.

## Two-Act Color Palette: From Vibrant Garden to Elegant Evening

The genius of this design lies in its contrast. For the outdoor ceremony, the couple leaned into a lush, expressive garden atmosphere. Think of it as nature's own palette—vivid, unapologetic, and full of life. This is where **wholesale silk flowers** shine: you can buy blooms in bulk to create oversized arrangements without breaking the bank. Imagine coral peonies, blue delphiniums, and fresh greenery lining the aisle. The key is to use volume and variety to mimic a natural garden.

As the sun set, the reception space transformed. The vibrant hues gave way to a serene combination of ivory, cream, and soft blush. This shift isn't just beautiful—it's smart. It allows the evening to feel more intimate and elegant, perfect for candlelit dinners. For DIY decorators, this two-act approach means you can reuse ceremony flowers (with some strategic swapping of ribbon or vase colors) to save money. Plus, by choosing **affordable wedding flowers** like faux roses and hydrangeas in both bright and neutral tones, you can create two distinct looks from one flower purchase.

## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas: How to Transition from Day to Night

One of the biggest challenges for wedding planners is making a venue feel different for the ceremony and reception. Here's how this couple nailed it:

- **Ceremony backdrop:** A lush floral arch using **wholesale silk flowers** in vibrant shades. This can be repurposed as a photo booth backdrop or even a sweetheart table feature later.
- **Table settings:** For the reception, neutral linens and ivory blooms create a calm, romantic mood. Add gold or brass candleholders for warmth.
- **Lighting:** String lights and lanterns are your best friends. They instantly shift the vibe from daytime garden to evening soiree.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

Whether you're planning your own wedding or styling for a client, here are actionable ideas inspired by this Bastide du Roy celebration:

1. **Buy in bulk:** Use **wholesale silk flowers** to get the volume you need for a garden look without the cost of fresh flowers. Mix brights for the ceremony and neutrals for the reception.
2. **Create a color story:** Choose 3-4 vibrant colors for the ceremony and 2-3 neutrals for the reception. This gives you a cohesive yet distinct two-part event.
3. **Repurpose strategically:** After the ceremony, move arch flowers to the reception entrance or use them as centerpieces. This saves time and money.
4. **Focus on key areas:** For **wedding venue decoration ideas**, prioritize the aisle, altar, and reception tables. Don't feel you need to decorate every corner—intimacy comes from focal points.
