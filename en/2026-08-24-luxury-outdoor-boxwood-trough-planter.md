## Practical Tips for Using Boxwood Troughs

- **Mix and match sizes**: With four sizes available, you can create depth and visual interest by combining different heights. Use taller troughs at the back and shorter ones in front for a layered effect.
- **Anchor them safely**: Outdoors, wind can be a factor. Secure the troughs with heavy-duty adhesive or place them against a sturdy wall. For events, you can also use sandbags inside the planter (hidden by the foliage) for extra stability.
- **Clean occasionally**: While maintenance-free, a gentle rinse with water will keep the leaves dust-free and looking their best, especially after being outdoors for a while.
- **Combine with lights**: Wrap string lights around the boxwood for a magical evening ambiance. The dense foliage holds the lights beautifully, creating a warm glow.
