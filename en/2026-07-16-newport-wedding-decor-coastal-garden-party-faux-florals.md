When you think of a summer wedding in Newport, Rhode Island, images of historic estates, ocean breezes, and lush gardens come to mind. The Castle Hill Inn, perched on a 40-acre peninsula overlooking Narragansett Bay, offers the perfect backdrop for a romantic, airy coastal garden-party celebration. In this article, we’ll explore the design elements of such a wedding and share practical tips for recreating the look with faux floral arrangements—perfect for DIY brides and wedding planners who want timeless elegance without the stress.

## The Inspiration: A Newport Summer Garden Party

The original wedding featured a classic and formal yet dreamy floral-forward aesthetic. The color palette—white, ivory, green, and pale blue—echoed the serene coastal setting. The ceremony overlooked the bay, with the natural landscape serving as a stunning backdrop. For those planning a similar event, the key is to blend formal structure with relaxed garden charm. Think tall, airy centerpieces, garlands along the aisle, and delicate touches of blue in linens or glassware.

## Why Choose Faux Floral Arrangements for Your Wedding?

While fresh flowers are beautiful, they come with limitations: wilting in heat, limited availability, and high cost. Faux floral arrangements offer a durable, reusable alternative that can be prepared well in advance. For a coastal garden party, silk botanicals like peonies, hydrangeas, and eucalyptus can mimic the lushness of fresh blooms without the worry of salt air or sun damage. They also allow you to create dramatic, oversized centerpieces that stay perfect throughout the day.

## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas: Bringing the Coast Indoors

To capture the spirit of a Newport summer, focus on natural textures and soft colors. Use ivory linens, pale blue napkins, and wooden accents. For the ceremony, drape greenery along the aisle or create a simple arch with white blooms. At the reception, mix tall and low centerpieces. A fake flowers centerpiece featuring white roses, blue thistle, and silver brunia can echo the ocean hues. Add candles in glass hurricanes for a warm, romantic glow.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

- **Choose high-quality silk flowers**: Look for realistic petals and stems. Brands like Afloral or Ling’s Moment offer great options for wedding decor.
- **Mix textures**: Combine matte and glossy leaves, open blooms and buds, to create depth.
- **Use foam or floral wire**: Secure stems in a sturdy base, especially for tall arrangements.
- **Test lighting**: Faux florals can look different under natural vs. artificial light. Check your setup before the big day.
- **Prep early**: Assemble centerpieces and bouquets weeks in advance. Store them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

## Conclusion

A Newport-inspired coastal garden party wedding is all about elegance, nature, and timeless romance. By incorporating faux floral arrangements and a fake flowers centerpiece, you can achieve the same dreamy look while saving time, money, and stress. Whether you’re a DIY bride or a seasoned planner, these wedding venue decoration ideas will help you create a celebration that feels both classic and fresh—just like a summer day by the sea.

Remember, the best weddings reflect the couple’s personality and the beauty of their surroundings. With a little creativity and the right materials, you can capture the spirit of any location, no matter where you are.
