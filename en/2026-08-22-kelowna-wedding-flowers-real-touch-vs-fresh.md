## Kelowna Wedding Flowers: Real Touch vs Fresh – What Brides Need to Know

Planning your wedding in Kelowna or the Okanagan? One of the biggest decisions you'll face is choosing your flowers. With soaring costs and unpredictable weather, more brides are weighing traditional fresh blooms against premium **realistic artificial flowers**. Both can be stunning, but they offer very different experiences. Let's break down the pros, cons, and cost differences so you can make the best choice for your big day.

### What Are Real Touch Wedding Flowers?

Real Touch flowers are high-quality faux florals designed to look and feel incredibly lifelike. Unlike older silk versions, modern Real Touch blooms have natural texture, movement, and photograph beautifully. Many brides are surprised they can't tell the difference—especially in photos. These are the **realistic artificial flowers** that are taking over the wedding market, and for good reason.

### Fresh Flowers: The Classic Choice

Fresh flowers have always been the traditional option. They bring natural fragrance and organic, one-of-a-kind charm. However, in the Okanagan, heat and sourcing can affect quality and pricing. Fresh blooms are sensitive to weather, must be prepared within days of the wedding, and are typically single-use. For outdoor summer weddings, they can wilt quickly, adding stress to your day.

### The Cost Difference: Real Touch vs Fresh

Cost is often the deciding factor. In Canada, wedding flower budgets can range from $1,000 to $7,000+ for fresh arrangements. At Pickadilly's, Shannon sees brides shocked by quotes like:

- Fresh floral arches: $1,000–$3,000+
- Centrepieces: $100–$300 each
- Bouquets: $250–$400

With Real Touch floral rentals, you can achieve a similar elevated look with more control. Typical pricing includes:

- Floral pillars: $700–$900
- Centrepieces: $18–$65
- Bouquets: starting at $125

This allows you to create a full, impactful design while saving thousands—without compromising on style. Plus, you know exactly what your flowers will look like ahead of time, no surprises.

### Practical Tips for Choosing Your Wedding Flowers

When deciding between fresh and Real Touch, consider these factors:

1. **Venue & Weather**: If you're having an outdoor wedding in Kelowna's summer heat, Real Touch is heat-safe and won't wilt.
2. **Budget**: Real Touch rentals are significantly more affordable, especially for large installations like arches, pillars, and aisle flowers.
3. **Sustainability**: Real Touch flowers are reusable and more sustainable than single-use fresh blooms.
4. **Aesthetics**: Both can be beautiful, but Real Touch offers consistency—every petal is perfect.
5. **Photography**: Real Touch flowers photograph just as well as fresh, often indistinguishable.

For **wedding venue decoration ideas**, think about mixing Real Touch statement pieces (like pillars or a sweetheart table cascade) with a few fresh accents for fragrance. And for your **bridal bouquet artificial flowers**, Real Touch bouquets are lightweight, durable, and can be kept as a keepsake.

### Conclusion

Whether you choose fresh or Real Touch, your wedding flowers should reflect your style and budget. Real Touch flowers offer a practical, beautiful alternative that many brides are embracing—especially in the Okanagan.
