When Chloe and Jack said their vows on the oceanfront lawn at Four Seasons Resort Oahu at Ko Olina, they created a celebration that felt like a garden in full bloom at the edge of the Pacific. ## Setting the Scene: Arch and Aisle Flowers

The rounded floral arch is the centerpiece of any oceanfront ceremony. At Chloe and Jack's wedding, the arch was lush with blooms in soft butter yellow and apricot, accented with pale blue ribbons that fluttered in the sea breeze. Low aisle flowers lined the walkway, creating a garden path that led to the water's edge.

To achieve this look affordably, consider using silk flowers wedding decor. High-quality silk blooms from leoflowers can be arranged to mimic the exact palette and texture of fresh flowers, and they won't wilt in the sun or wind. Here's how to recreate the arch and aisle:

- **Choose a sturdy arch frame** (wood or metal) and cover it with a base of greenery—real or artificial.
- **Cluster silk flowers** in your chosen palette at the top and sides of the arch, using zip ties or floral wire for easy attachment.
- **For aisle flowers**, use low, wide containers filled with artificial flowers and place them at intervals along the aisle. Add lanterns or candles for evening ambiance.
- **Mix in real greenery** if you like, but keep the flowers artificial for longevity and cost savings.

## Reception Details: Lanterns, Linens, and Dance Floors

The reception carried the garden party theme with woven lanterns hanging from the ceiling, patterned linens in soft pastels, and a checkerboard dance floor that added a playful touch. These wedding venue decoration ideas are easy to adapt to any location:

- **Woven lanterns**: Hang them at varying heights for a whimsical, organic feel. You can find affordable lanterns at craft stores or online, and they double as lighting for evening.
- **Patterned linens**: Choose tablecloths and napkins in subtle patterns that complement your flower palette. This adds texture without overwhelming the space.
- **Checkerboard dance floor**: A rental company can install one, or you can create a similar effect with decals on an existing floor.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

Recreating an oceanfront garden party requires careful planning, especially when it comes to florals. Here are some practical tips to keep your budget in check and your vision on track:

1. **Order samples early**: Before committing to a large order of artificial flowers, request samples to check color, quality, and texture. This is especially important for pastel palettes like butter yellow and apricot, which can vary under different lighting.
2. **Mix high and low**: Use artificial flowers for high-impact areas like the arch and centerpieces, and supplement with real greenery or inexpensive filler flowers for volume.
3. **Plan for wind and sun**: If your venue is outdoors, secure all arrangements with weights or ties. Silk flowers hold up better than fresh in wind, but they still need anchoring.
4. **Reuse and repurpose**: After the ceremony, move aisle flowers to the reception area as centerpieces or decor accents. Artificial flowers make this easy—no wilting, no water needed.
5. **Work with a supplier**: For consistent quality and bulk pricing, source your artificial flowers from a reputable manufacturer. leofaux provides a wide selection of silk flowers and custom arrangements, making it simple to achieve a cohesive look.
