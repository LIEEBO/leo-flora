## Practical Tips for Using Artificial Baby's Breath

- **Fluff and shape:** Gently separate the stems and bend the wires to create a natural, airy look. Real Baby's Breath has a wild, wispy quality — don't over-tidy.
- **Mix real and faux:** Combine artificial Baby's Breath with fresh greenery or a few real blooms for a hybrid arrangement that fools the eye.
- **Clean gently:** Dust with a hairdryer on cool or a soft brush. Avoid water, which can damage some fabrics.
- **Store smart:** Keep stems in a cool, dry place. Use a garment bag or box to prevent crushing.
- **Color coordinate:** Artificial Baby's Breath comes in white, cream, blush, and even dyed colors. Match your palette or use it to add a subtle tint.
