When you think of a dreamy seaside wedding, the image of a romantic, airy garden party by the ocean likely comes to mind. That’s exactly what one Rhode Island couple achieved at the historic Castle Hill Inn in Newport, overlooking Narragansett Bay. Their celebration was a masterclass in classic, floral-forward design—think white, ivory, green, and pale blue, with an elegant yet effortless coastal vibe. Whether you’re a DIY bride or a seasoned wedding planner, this real wedding offers plenty of inspiration for creating your own stunning event. And the best part? You can achieve a similar look with a blend of fresh and **faux floral arrangements** that save money and last longer.

## The Magic of a Coastal Garden-Party Vibe

The couple’s vision was clear: a romantic and airy coastal garden party. The ceremony took place on the lawn, with the sparkling bay as the backdrop. To replicate this look, focus on soft, neutral colors like cream, sage green, and powder blue. **Wedding venue decoration ideas** often start with the ceremony arch—consider draping it with flowing white fabric and clusters of blooms. For a budget-friendly twist, use **realistic artificial flowers** in your arch and aisle markers. They photograph beautifully and won’t wilt in the sea breeze, making them perfect for outdoor weddings.

## Floral Forward Design: How to Make It Yours

This wedding leaned heavily into a floral-forward aesthetic, meaning flowers were the star of the show—not just accents. Think oversized centerpieces, lush garlands on tables, and petals scattered along the aisle. If you’re working with a tight budget, invest in high-quality **faux floral arrangements** for the bulk of your decor. Mix in a few fresh blooms for fragrance and authenticity. For example, use realistic artificial hydrangeas and roses as the base, then add fresh eucalyptus or baby’s breath. This hybrid approach gives you the best of both worlds: the lush look of a high-end florist without the sky-high price tag.

## Practical Tips for a Seaside Wedding That Lasts

Outdoor weddings, especially by the water, come with unique challenges. Wind, sun, and humidity can quickly ruin fresh flowers. That’s where **realistic artificial flowers** shine. They hold up in any weather, so your ceremony arch and reception centerpieces stay perfect all day. Here are a few tips to incorporate them seamlessly:

- **Use silk flowers for high-traffic areas** like the altar, head table, and photo backdrop. Guests will never notice the difference if you choose quality stems.
- **Mix textures** by pairing matte petals with glossy leaves. This adds depth and realism to your arrangements.
- **Add personal touches** like heirloom brooches or ribbons to your faux bouquets. They become keepsakes you can treasure forever.
- **Plan for lighting**—soft, warm lighting (string lights or candles) makes any floral arrangement look magical, whether real or artificial.

## Why Faux Florals Are a Smart Choice for Wedding Decor

Modern **faux floral arrangements** have come a long way. Today’s realistic artificial flowers are made from high-quality silk, latex, or foam, with natural-looking colors and textures. They’re a game-changer for wedding venue decoration because you can prepare everything weeks in advance. No last-minute scrambling to keep flowers fresh. Plus, you can repurpose them for bridal showers, baby showers, or even your home decor after the wedding. For planners, this means less stress and more control over the final look. And for DIY brides, it’s a chance to create a Pinterest-worthy event on a realistic budget.

## Final Thoughts: Capture Your Own Newport Summer

This Castle Hill Inn wedding proves that a coastal garden-party theme is timeless and achievable. By blending fresh and **faux floral arrangements**, you can create a stunning, cohesive look that withstands the elements and your budget. Whether you’re planning a seaside celebration or a garden party in your backyard, let the spirit of Newport inspire you—soft colors, natural textures, and flowers that feel like they belong to the landscape. With **realistic artificial flowers**, you can capture that magic and hold onto it long after the last dance.
