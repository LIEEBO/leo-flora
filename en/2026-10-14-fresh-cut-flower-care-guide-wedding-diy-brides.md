## Step-by-Step Guide to Caring for Fresh-Cut Flowers

### 1. Start with a Clean Vase

Before you even think about arranging your flowers, make sure your vase is spotless. Wash it with warm soapy water and rinse thoroughly. Clean scissors or shears are also essential to prevent bacteria from entering the stems. Bacteria can shorten the life of your flowers, so cleanliness is key.

### 2. Trim the Stems

Cut 1–2 cm from the bottom of the stems at a 45-degree angle. This increases the surface area for water absorption. Remove any leaves that will sit below the water line, as they can rot and introduce bacteria.

### 3. Use a Quick Dip (Optional)

If you have a floral quick dip, dip the freshly cut stems for about 5 seconds, then immediately place them into water. This can help seal in moisture and provide nutrients. However, this step is optional and depends on the products you have available.

### 4. Prepare the Vase Water

Fill the vase with fresh, room-temperature water. Add a floral preservative or flower food sachet and mix until dissolved. This provides nutrients and helps control bacteria. If you don't have commercial flower food, you can make your own by mixing a teaspoon of sugar, a teaspoon of bleach, and a quart of water.

### 5. Maintain Your Arrangement

Every 2–3 days, empty and rinse the vase, refill with fresh water, and add more flower food. Re-trim the stems and remove any wilted leaves or petals. This keeps the water clean and the flowers hydrated.

### 6. Keep Flowers in the Right Position

Avoid direct sunlight, heaters, AC vents, and fans. Also, keep flowers away from ripening fruit, as the ethylene gas they release can shorten flower life.
