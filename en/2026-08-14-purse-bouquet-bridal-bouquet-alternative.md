## What Is a Purse Bouquet?

A purse bouquet is exactly what it sounds like—a beautiful clutch or small handbag that doubles as a bouquet. It's typically made with a hard-shell or soft fabric bag, adorned with fresh or artificial flowers, and carried like a purse. The stems are often shortened or removed entirely, with blooms arranged around the bag's opening or cascading down the front. The result is a functional accessory that keeps your hands free when you need them, yet still gives you that floral pop for photos and ceremony.

Unlike a traditional bouquet, a purse bouquet can be slipped over your wrist or held by its handle, leaving your hands free for hand-holding, hugging, or holding a drink. It's also a keepsake—after the wedding, you can remove the flowers and use the purse again.

## How to Make Your Own Purse Bouquet

Creating a purse bouquet is easier than you think. Here's a simple step-by-step guide:

1. **Choose your bag**: Pick a clutch or small handbag with a flat top or a wide opening. Hard-shell bags work best because they provide structure. Avoid bags with delicate fabric that might tear.

2. **Select your flowers**: Fresh flowers are lovely, but **wholesale silk flowers** are a smart choice for durability and cost-effectiveness. They look realistic, come in every color, and can be reused for other projects. Choose blooms that match your wedding palette—roses, peonies, or even succulents.

3. **Prepare the flowers**: Trim stems to about 2–3 inches. If using silk, you can simply bend or cut the stems. For fresh flowers, use floral tape to secure the stems together.

4. **Attach the flowers**: Apply a small amount of hot glue to the base of the flower cluster and press it onto the top of the bag. You can also create a removable arrangement by inserting stems into a floral foam block placed inside the bag—this allows you to swap flowers later.

5. **Add accents**: Consider adding ribbon, pearls, or lace around the handle or the base of the flowers for extra charm.

6. **Let it dry**: If using glue, give it time to set. For fresh flowers, keep the bag in a cool place until the ceremony.

- **Coordinate with bridesmaids**: Give each bridesmaid a smaller purse bouquet in a different shade. This creates a cohesive look and doubles as a gift.

- **Practice carrying it**: Walk around with your purse bouquet before the big day to get comfortable with the weight and position.
