## The Castle Hill Inn Aesthetic: Romantic, Formal, and Floral-Forward

Set on a 40-acre historic estate overlooking Narragansett Bay, this wedding was all about classic formality with a soft, romantic twist. The ceremony backdrop featured the bay itself, while the design vibe leaned into a “dreamy floral-forward” look. Think lush greenery, delicate blooms in soft whites and pale blues, and an overall sense of effortless elegance. For DIY brides and wedding planners, this style is incredibly adaptable—especially when you substitute fresh flowers with high-quality faux alternatives. Using bridal bouquet artificial flowers in ivory and pale blue, for example, can replicate the garden-party feel without the need for a florist on site.

## Why Realistic Artificial Flowers Are Perfect for a Coastal Garden Party

One of the biggest challenges of a seaside wedding is the weather. Wind, humidity, and sun can quickly wilt fresh blooms. Realistic artificial flowers solve that problem beautifully. Modern silk flowers look so authentic that guests won’t know the difference—especially when you choose varieties like peonies, roses, and hydrangeas in soft coastal colors. For this Newport-inspired look, focus on textures: mix matte petals with subtle sheen, and add faux greenery like eucalyptus or ferns. The result is a bouquet that stays perfect from the ceremony to the last dance, making it an ideal choice for both the bridal bouquet and centerpieces.

## Affordable Wedding Flowers That Look High-End

Let’s talk budget. Fresh flowers for a wedding like this can easily run into thousands of dollars, especially with a floral-forward design. Affordable wedding flowers don’t have to mean sacrificing style. By choosing realistic artificial flowers, you can get the same lush, layered look for a fraction of the cost. For example, a bridal bouquet artificial flowers in white and pale blue can cost under $50, while a fresh equivalent might be $200 or more. Plus, you can reuse them as home decor after the wedding—a win-win for savvy planners.

## Practical Tips for Recreating This Look with Faux Florals

Here are some actionable steps to bring the Castle Hill Inn vibe to your own wedding:

- **Choose a cohesive color palette**: Stick to white, ivory, green, and pale blue. These colors read as timeless and coastal.
- **Mix flower types**: Combine large blooms like garden roses with smaller accent flowers like lisianthus or baby’s breath. Faux versions are widely available.
- **Incorporate greenery**: Faux eucalyptus, ivy, and ferns add volume and texture without extra cost.
- **Focus on the bouquet**: Your bridal bouquet artificial flowers will be photographed most. Invest in high-quality silk blooms for that realistic touch.
- **Use affordable wedding flowers for centerpieces**: Group small faux arrangements in low vases for an airy, garden-party feel.
- **Don’t forget the ceremony arch**: A simple arch draped with faux greenery and white blooms mirrors the natural beauty of the Narragansett Bay backdrop.
