## Practical Tips for Recreating This Look

- **Choose the right palette:** Stick to champagne, blush, ivory, and peach for a soft, romantic feel. Add tropical greenery like monstera or palm leaves to tie in the beach setting.
- **Mix textures:** Combine classic roses with tropical foliage and trailing vines to create depth and visual interest.
- **Plan for the weather:** Real flowers can wilt in heat and humidity. Silk flowers wedding decor is a smart alternative—they’re durable, lightweight, and hold up beautifully outdoors.
- **DIY ahead of time:** With faux floral arrangements, you can build your arch, aisle pieces, and centerpieces weeks in advance, reducing last-minute stress.
