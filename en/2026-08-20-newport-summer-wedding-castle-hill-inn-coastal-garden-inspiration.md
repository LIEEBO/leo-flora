## The Vision: A Romantic Coastal Garden Party

The couple envisioned a celebration that felt both classic and fresh—a formal affair softened by the natural beauty of the Narragansett Bay. The 40-acre estate provided the perfect backdrop, with the ceremony overlooking the water and the reception spaces flowing with airy, garden-inspired decor. The design was intentionally floral-forward, with blooms used generously to create a sense of abundance and romance. Yet, the overall vibe remained light and breezy, thanks to a palette of white, ivory, green, and pale blue.

This aesthetic is incredibly popular for seaside weddings, and it's easy to see why. It's timeless, photogenic, and works beautifully with the surrounding landscape. The key is to balance formality with ease—think structured tablescapes softened by cascading greenery, or classic white chairs against a backdrop of wildflowers.

## Recreating the Look with Affordable Wedding Flowers

One of the biggest concerns for any bride is the floral budget. Fresh flowers, especially in the volume shown here, can be a significant expense. The good news? You can achieve the same lush, romantic look with affordable wedding flowers that don't compromise on style. Here are a few practical ways to do it:

*   **Mix fresh and faux:** Use fresh flowers for the ceremony arch and a few key reception pieces, then switch to high-quality artificial stems for centerpieces and aisle markers. No one will notice the difference, and you'll save a bundle.
*   **Choose in-season blooms:** If you're set on fresh flowers, pick varieties that are in season during your wedding month. For a summer wedding, that might mean peonies, garden roses, or dahlias—all of which are more affordable when they're locally grown.
*   **Go green-heavy:** Greenery is your best friend. It's inexpensive, adds volume, and creates that lush, garden feel. Use eucalyptus, olive branches, or ferns to fill out arrangements and reduce the number of flowers needed.

## The Secret to Realistic Artificial Flowers

If you're considering artificial flowers for your wedding, you're in good company. Modern realistic artificial flowers are a game-changer, especially for brides who want to keep their bouquet or repurpose decor. The key is to choose stems that look and feel real. Look for flowers with natural color variations, soft petals, and realistic textures. High-quality silk, latex, or foam flowers can be incredibly convincing.

When creating your bridal bouquet with artificial flowers, think about the same principles you'd use with fresh blooms: varying heights, a mix of focal flowers and fillers, and a touch of greenery for contrast. The advantage? You can assemble your bouquet weeks in advance, and it will look perfect on the day—no wilting, no last-minute panic, and no need to keep it in the fridge.

## Practical Tips for a Floral-Forward Wedding on a Budget

Ready to bring this coastal garden vision to life? Here are some actionable tips to help you plan a stunning floral-forward wedding without breaking the bank:

1.  **Create a focal point:** Invest your floral budget in one or two show-stopping pieces—like the ceremony arch or the head table. This is where you can splurge on premium blooms or high-end artificial arrangements.
2.  **Repurpose everything:** Plan your florals so they can do double duty. Ceremony arrangements can be moved to the reception, and bridesmaids' bouquets can become centerpieces. With artificial flowers, this is even easier—just pack them and move them yourself.
3.  **Use the venue's natural beauty:** Castle Hill Inn is stunning on its own, so you don't need to over-decorate. Let the ocean views and historic architecture shine. This approach saves money and keeps the focus on what matters—the celebration and the scenery.
4.  **Consider a flower wall or installation:** A lush flower wall for photos or a hanging installation above the dance floor can create a huge impact with fewer stems than you might think. And if you use artificial blooms, you can sell it afterward or use it in your home.
