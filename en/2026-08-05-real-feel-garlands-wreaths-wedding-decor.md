When planning a wedding, every detail counts—from the centerpieces to the aisle decor. But fresh greenery can be costly, high-maintenance, and short-lived. That's why more DIY brides and wedding planners are turning to **real feel garlands and wreaths**. These lifelike faux florals offer the beauty of fresh foliage without the stress, and they're perfect for everything from ceremony arches to reception tables. In this guide, we'll explore why these versatile pieces are a game-changer for wedding decor, and how you can use them to create stunning, budget-friendly settings.

## Why Choose Real Feel Garlands and Wreaths for Weddings?

Real feel (also called real touch, PE, or realistic) garlands and wreaths are designed to mimic the texture and appearance of fresh greenery. For weddings, they offer several standout benefits:

- **Lifelike Appearance**: Guests won't be able to tell the difference—these pieces look and feel like the real thing, adding warmth and elegance to any venue.
- **Durability**: Unlike fresh greenery, they won't wilt or brown, even during long receptions or in warm weather. You can set up decorations days in advance.
- **Allergy-Friendly**: Perfect for couples and guests with pollen sensitivities, these faux options let everyone enjoy the decor without sneezing or itchy eyes.
- **Cost-Effective**: When you consider that you can reuse them for showers, rehearsal dinners, and even home decor afterward, they're a smart investment. Plus, they're a great option for **affordable wedding flowers**.

## How to Use Garlands and Wreaths in Wedding Decor

Garlands and wreaths are incredibly versatile. Here are some creative ways to incorporate them into your wedding:

- **Ceremony Backdrops**: Drape a 10-foot garland over an arch or along a wooden frame for a lush, romantic altar.
- **Table Runners**: Lay garlands down the center of banquet tables, weaving in candles and small blooms for a rustic or elegant look.
- **Aisle Decor**: Line the aisle with small wreaths attached to chairs or pews, or lay garlands along the edges.
- **Mantel and Staircase Decor**: For a venue with a fireplace or grand staircase, wrap garlands around banisters and place wreaths above the mantel.
- **Photo Booth Backdrops**: Create a stunning backdrop using a mix of garlands and wreaths for memorable guest photos.

### Choosing the Right Size and Style

Garlands come in lengths from 5 to 15 feet, while wreaths range from 24 to 36 inches. When planning your wedding decor, consider the scale of your venue:

- **Large Venues**: Opt for longer garlands (12-15 feet) and larger wreaths (36 inches) to make a statement.
- **Intimate Gatherings**: Smaller garlands (5-8 feet) and wreaths (24 inches) work well for cozy spaces.

Styles include pine, cedar, or a mix of both. Pine offers a classic, evergreen look, while cedar adds a softer, more textured feel. Choose lit or unlit versions depending on the ambiance you want—lit options can add a magical glow to evening events.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

Working with real feel garlands and wreaths is a breeze, but here are some insider tips to get the best results:

- **Mix and Match**: Combine different greenery styles and add your own touches—like faux berries, flowers, or ribbons—to match your wedding palette.
- **Use as a Base**: These garlands are perfect as a base for **fake flowers centerpiece** creations. Add a few stems of silk blooms to create lush, custom centerpieces.
- **Think Beyond the Ceremony**: After the wedding, repurpose the garlands and wreaths for your home or gift them to your bridal party as keepsakes.
- **Store Properly**: To keep them looking great for future events, store them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. They'll last for years.
