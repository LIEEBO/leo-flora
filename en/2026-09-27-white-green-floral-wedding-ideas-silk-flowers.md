## Why White and Green Florals Never Go Out of Style

There are floral combinations that simply work, and white and green is one of them. Soft white peonies sit alongside lush green hydrangeas, delicate white blossom and trailing greenery, creating an arrangement that looks like it was picked from an English country garden on a summer morning. It is no wonder this palette is a firm favourite with interior designers, wedding planners and DIY brides alike.

If you are planning a wedding, this classic pairing offers endless possibilities. And with high-quality artificial flowers, you can achieve the look for a fraction of the cost of fresh blooms — without worrying about wilting before the reception even begins.

## Creative Ways to Use White and Green Florals

### 1. Stunning Ceremony Backdrops

A white and green arch or backdrop is a timeless choice for the ceremony. Use trailing greenery garlands and clusters of white peonies to frame the altar. Add sheer fabric for a romantic touch. Artificial garlands are especially practical here — they hold their shape, need no water, and can be arranged the day before.

### 2. Table Centrepieces That Wow

A **fake flowers centerpiece** can be just as elegant as fresh flowers. For a white and green theme, try low arrangements of white hydrangeas with eucalyptus and fern. Mix in candles and glass votives for a warm glow. Because artificial flowers are durable, you can create these centrepieces well in advance and store them safely.

### 3. Aisle and Chair Decorations

Line the aisle with small posies of white blossom and greenery tied to chair backs. Use garlands along the aisle runner or pew ends. Artificial flowers are lightweight and easy to attach with ribbon or zip ties.

### 4. Reception Venue Transformations

From the head table to the cake table, white and green florals can tie the whole venue together. Consider a greenery garland draped over the head table, white peony arrangements on guest tables, and a floral hoop or hanging installation above the dance floor. For more **wedding venue decoration ideas**, think about how you can reuse ceremony flowers at the reception — a major advantage of artificial blooms.

## Practical Tips for DIY Brides and Planners

- **Order early and in bulk.** Wholesale suppliers offer better prices, and you will have time to practise arrangements.
- **Mix textures.** Combine roses, peonies, hydrangeas and trailing greenery for a natural look.
- **Use a base.** Floral foam or a wire frame helps artificial flowers hold their shape.
- **Bend and shape.** Most silk flowers have wired stems, so you can adjust them to fit any container.
- **Store carefully.** After the event, wrap flowers in tissue paper and store in boxes for future use.
