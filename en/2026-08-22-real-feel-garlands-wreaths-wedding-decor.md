# Real Feel Garlands & Wreaths: Elevate Your Wedding Decor with Lifelike Greenery

When planning a wedding, every detail matters—from the ceremony backdrop to the reception tables. One of the most versatile and budget-friendly ways to add elegance and warmth is with **real feel garlands and wreaths**. Also known as real touch, PE, or realistic faux greenery, these decorations have become a favorite among wedding planners and DIY brides. They offer the beauty of fresh greenery without the worry of wilting, and they can be used in countless creative ways.

## Why Choose Real Feel Garlands and Wreaths for Weddings?

Real feel garlands and wreaths are designed to mimic the look and texture of fresh pine, cedar, or mixed greenery. They are crafted with high-quality PE (polyethylene) materials that feel surprisingly lifelike. For weddings, they are a game-changer because they provide:

- **Lifelike Appearance** – Guests will admire the lush, realistic look, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere.
- **Durability** – Unlike fresh greenery, these pieces won’t wilt or dry out during your event, even in warm weather or under bright lights.
- **Allergy-Friendly** – Many guests suffer from pollen allergies; faux greenery lets everyone enjoy the decor without sneezing.
- **Easy Storage & Reuse** – After the wedding, you can repurpose them for your home or sell them to another couple, making them a smart investment.

## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas with Garlands and Wreaths

### 1. Ceremony Backdrops and Arches

Drape a 10- to 15-foot real feel garland over a wooden arch or a simple trellis. Add some sheer fabric and a few faux flowers for a romantic focal point. You can also use wreaths as standalone decorations on either side of the arch for symmetry.

### 2. Table Runners and Centerpieces

Instead of expensive fresh flower arrangements, lay a garland down the center of your dining tables. Intersperse with candles, lanterns, and **wholesale silk flowers** in your wedding colors. This creates a lush, cohesive look at a fraction of the cost.

### 3. Aisle and Pew Decorations

Line the aisle with small wreaths or clusters of garland attached to chairs or pews. This adds a touch of elegance without obstructing the view. You can also create a runner effect by placing short segments of garland along the aisle edges.

### 4. Reception Entrance and Photo Walls

Hang a large wreath on the entrance door or create a photo wall using multiple wreaths and garlands. This not only beautifies the space but also provides a perfect backdrop for guest photos.

## Affordable Wedding Flowers: How to Save Without Sacrificing Style

Weddings are expensive, and flowers often take up a big chunk of the budget. **Affordable wedding flowers** don’t have to mean sacrificing beauty. Here are some practical tips:

- **Buy Wholesale** – Purchase **wholesale silk flowers** and real feel greenery in bulk. This cuts costs significantly compared to retail or fresh florals.
- **Mix Fresh and Faux** – Use real flowers for the bridal bouquet and boutonnieres, but rely on faux garlands and wreaths for large installations. This is a common strategy used by professional planners.
- **Rent or Reuse** – If you’re not planning to keep the decor, consider renting real feel garlands from a local decor company. Alternatively, buy them and resell after the wedding.
- **Choose Timeless Styles** – Opt for classic pine or cedar garlands that work for both winter and summer weddings. They can also be used for holidays later, giving you more value.

## Practical Tips for Using Real Feel Greenery

- **Bend and Shape** – Real feel garlands and wreaths are flexible. You can bend them to fit arches, mantels, or table runners. Use floral wire to secure them in place.
- **Combine Textures** – Mix pine and cedar styles for a more natural look. Add berries, pinecones, or faux flowers to match your theme.
- **Lighting** – Many real feel garlands come with built-in lights, which can add a magical glow to evening weddings. If unlit, you can weave string lights through them yourself.
- **Storage** – After the wedding, gently clean them and store in a cool, dry place. They’ll last for years, ready for anniversaries or holiday decor.
