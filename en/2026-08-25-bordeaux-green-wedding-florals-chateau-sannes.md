When Carly and Thomas chose Chateau de Sannes in Provence for their wedding, they knew the venue would be the star. But it was their thoughtful floral palette—deep bordeaux and lush green—that truly tied the celebration to the stunning French countryside. From an intimate garden ceremony to a long family-style table under the trees, every detail flowed with the property's natural charm. If you're dreaming of a wedding that feels both elegant and effortless, here's how to capture that same magic—using silk flowers wedding decor that looks just as beautiful as the real thing.

## Why Choose Faux Florals for a Destination Wedding?

Planning a wedding abroad comes with its own set of challenges—especially when it comes to flowers. Real blooms can wilt in the heat, require refrigeration, and often need to be sourced locally, which can limit your color palette. That's why many couples and planners are turning to faux floral arrangements. They offer the flexibility to design exactly what you envision, without worrying about seasonality or perishability.

At Chateau de Sannes, the bordeaux and green theme was brought to life with lush, layered florals that echoed the vineyards and olive groves around the property. Using realistic artificial flowers allowed the couple to achieve that same lush, romantic look—while ensuring every stem stayed picture-perfect from the ceremony to the last dance.

## Designing Your Palette: Bordeaux and Green

Bordeaux is a rich, wine-inspired hue that adds depth and sophistication to any wedding palette. Paired with varying shades of green—from sage to eucalyptus—it creates a natural, earthy elegance that works beautifully for a garden or vineyard setting. Think deep burgundy dahlias, garden roses, and ranunculus, mixed with olive branches, ivy, and ferns.

With faux floral arrangements, you can mix textures and shapes freely. Velvet petals, silk leaves, and matte stems can mimic the look of fresh flowers so closely that guests won't know the difference—until they touch them. And because you're not limited by what's in season, you can have your exact dream blooms, no matter the date.

## Practical Tips for Using Faux Florals in Your Wedding Decor

1. **Mix high-quality silk flowers with a few real accents** – This creates a realistic, lush look while keeping costs down and ensuring durability.
2. **Use realistic artificial flowers in key focal points** – Think bouquets, centerpieces, and ceremony arches. These are where photos will be taken, so invest in premium pieces.
3. **Consider the venue's lighting** – Silks can look different in natural vs. artificial light. Test your arrangements at the venue if possible, or ask your decorator for a mock-up.
4. **Plan for easy transport** – Faux florals are lightweight and can be shipped ahead, saving you the stress of coordinating with local florists.
5. **Repurpose your florals** – Use ceremony flowers for the reception, or take them home as keepsakes. That's a benefit real flowers can't offer.

## Creating a Flowing Ceremony and Reception

At Chateau de Sannes, the couple's goal was to let guests move easily between the garden ceremony and the long family-style table. Bonjour Weddings designed a layout that encouraged mingling, with floral installations guiding the way. You can achieve the same flow by using consistent floral elements throughout your venue—like matching garlands on chairs and table runners.

Faux floral arrangements are perfect for this because they can be pre-arranged and placed exactly where you need them, without worrying about wilting or water spills. You can even hang them from trees or drape them along banisters, creating a cohesive story from one space to the next.

## Capturing the Beauty: Photography and Faux Florals

One of the biggest concerns brides have about silk flowers is how they'll photograph. The good news? Modern realistic artificial flowers are designed to catch light beautifully, just like fresh petals. Sophie Mort Photography captured the blue skies and vibrant greenery at Chateau de Sannes, and the bordeaux florals popped perfectly against the stone walls and soft light.

To ensure your faux florals look amazing in photos, choose high-quality pieces with subtle color variations and natural petal textures. Avoid overly shiny materials, and ask your photographer to take a few close-ups—you'll be amazed at how lifelike they appear.
