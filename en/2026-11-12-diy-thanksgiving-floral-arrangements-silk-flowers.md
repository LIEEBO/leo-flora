### 1. Rustic Harvest Centerpiece: Sunflowers and Maple Leaves

Nothing says "autumn" like the bold warmth of sunflowers paired with the fiery hues of maple leaves. This fall centerpiece is ideal for long farmhouse tables or as a statement piece for a Thanksgiving buffet.

**Materials You'll Need:**
- Real Touch Artificial Sunflowers Bouquet (5 stems, 13" tall, latex-coated silk for a soft, realistic feel)
- Real-Touch Artificial Maple Leaf Stems (3-pack, 14" branches in red, orange, and yellow)
- A low wooden tray or dough bowl (12–18" wide)
- Twine or raffia for tying stems
- Optional: Mini pumpkins or gourds for added texture

**How to Assemble:**
1. **Start with the greenery:** Lay the maple leaf stems in a loose, cascading pattern across the tray, allowing some leaves to spill over the edges for a natural look.
2. **Add the sunflowers:** Cluster 3–4 sunflower stems in the center, trimming the stems to varying heights (8–12") for dimension. The latex-coated petals mimic the velvety texture of fresh sunflowers, making them a standout in any arrangement.
3. **Fill in the gaps:** Tuck in the remaining sunflower stems and a few extra maple leaves around the base. Secure loose stems with twine if needed.
4. **Finish with accents:** Nestle mini pumpkins or gourds between the flowers for a harvest-inspired touch.

**Pro Tip:** These sunflowers are UV-resistant, so they won't fade if displayed near a sunny window—perfect for reusing year after year.

### 2. Elegant Pumpkin Centerpiece: Roses and Eucalyptus

For a more refined Thanksgiving tablescape, combine the softness of roses with the silvery green of eucalyptus. This arrangement works beautifully as a wedding venue decoration idea, especially for fall weddings.

**Materials You'll Need:**
- Real Touch Artificial Roses (12 stems, 10" tall, in cream or blush)
- Artificial Eucalyptus Stems (6 stems, 18" long)
- A medium-sized pumpkin (real or artificial) hollowed out to hold a water source (if using fresh) or floral foam
- Floral foam and a knife
- Moss for covering the foam

**How to Assemble:**
1. **Prepare the pumpkin:** Cut off the top and scoop out the inside. Soak floral foam in water (if using fresh flowers) and cut to fit inside the pumpkin. For artificial flowers, dry foam is fine.
2. **Arrange the eucalyptus:** Insert eucalyptus stems around the edge of the pumpkin, letting them drape naturally.
3. **Add roses:** Cluster roses in the center, varying heights for a full look.
4. **Cover the foam:** Tuck moss around the base of the stems to hide the foam.

### 3. Whimsical Harvest Wreath: Berries and Leaves

A floral wreath adds a welcoming touch to your front door or as a backdrop for a dessert table. This DIY project is simple and uses seasonal elements.

**Materials You'll Need:**
- Grapevine wreath base (12–18" diameter)
- Artificial Berry Stems (assorted colors: red, orange, burgundy)
- Artificial Maple Leaf Stems (3–4 stems)
- Hot glue gun and glue sticks
- Ribbon for a bow (optional)

**How to Assemble:**
1. **Start with leaves:** Tuck maple leaf stems into the wreath, distributing them evenly.
2. **Add berries:** Insert berry stems, clustering them for impact.
3. **Secure with glue:** Use hot glue to attach any loose stems.
4. **Add a bow:** Tie a ribbon bow at the bottom for a festive touch.

**Pro Tip:** Hang the wreath on a covered door to protect it from direct weather, or use it indoors as a wedding flower wall backdrop for a fall-themed photo booth.
