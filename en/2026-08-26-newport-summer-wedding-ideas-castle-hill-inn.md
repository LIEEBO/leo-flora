## A Newport Summer Wedding at Castle Hill Inn: Inspiration for Your Coastal Celebration

Imagine exchanging vows on a lush lawn overlooking the sparkling Narragansett Bay, with a gentle sea breeze carrying the scent of salt and roses. That was the dreamy reality for one couple at the historic Castle Hill Inn in Newport, Rhode Island. Their wedding captured the essence of a Newport summer—classic, elegant, and effortlessly romantic. In this article, we'll break down the design elements of this stunning celebration and share practical tips on how you can achieve a similar look for your own wedding, including how to incorporate faux floral arrangements for lasting beauty.

## The Vision: Romantic, Airy, and Floral-Forward

The couple envisioned a romantic and airy coastal garden-party wedding. The design was classic and formal, yet it featured a dreamy, floral-forward aesthetic that softened the grandeur of the 40-acre estate. The color palette was simple and timeless: white, ivory, green, and pale blue. These hues echoed the natural surroundings—the white clapboard buildings, the deep green lawns, and the endless blue of the bay.

For brides and planners aiming to recreate this vibe, the key is balance. You want the flowers to feel abundant and lush, but not overwhelming. Think of cascading centerpieces, verdant ceremony arches, and delicate petals scattered along the aisle. To achieve this look without breaking the bank, consider using high-quality fake flowers centerpiece options that mimic the freshness of real blooms. Modern faux florals are incredibly realistic and can be reused for multiple events, making them a smart choice for budget-conscious couples.

## Ceremony by the Sea: Creating a Stunning Backdrop

The ceremony took place with the Narragansett Bay as the backdrop. The couple stood beneath a simple yet elegant arch adorned with greenery and white blooms. To recreate this, focus on the natural beauty of your venue. If you're near water, let that be the star. Use an arch or a simple frame decorated with faux floral arrangements that echo the surrounding landscape. For a coastal wedding, incorporate elements like driftwood, seashells, or even starfish into your floral decor. This personal touch makes the setting feel uniquely yours.

When planning your ceremony decor, think about the sightlines. You want your guests to see you clearly, but also to enjoy the scenery. Low-profile arrangements on the chairs or along the aisle can guide the eye without blocking the view. And don't forget about the wind—a common factor at seaside venues. Securing your florals with wire and using sturdy stems can prevent disaster. Faux flowers are particularly advantageous here, as they won't wilt or droop in the heat or breeze.

## Reception Design: Elegant Tablescapes and Floral Accents

Inside the reception, the floral-forward theme continued. Tables were dressed in crisp white linens, with low centerpieces of garden roses, hydrangeas, and trailing greenery. To add height and drama, some tables featured tall, clear vases with cascading blooms. The pale blue accents appeared in the napkins, the glassware, and even in the wedding cake's delicate frosting details.

For your own reception, consider mixing heights and textures. A combination of tall and low centerpieces creates visual interest and encourages conversation across the table. If you're using fake flowers centerpiece designs, you can pre-assemble them weeks in advance, saving you time and stress on the big day. Look for faux florals in shades of white, ivory, and pale blue to match your palette. Add candles in mercury glass or brass holders for a warm, romantic glow that complements the greenery.

## Practical Tips for a Floral-Forward Wedding

Here are some actionable tips to help you bring this style to life:

- **Invest in quality faux flowers:** Not all fake flowers are created equal. Look for ones with realistic petals and stems. Brands like Afloral and Ling's Moment offer stunning options that are perfect for weddings.
- **Mix faux and fresh:** If you love the look of fresh flowers but want to save money, use faux flowers for the bridal party and large installations, and fresh flowers for the smaller, more intimate details like the boutonnieres.
- **Use greenery liberally:** Greenery is inexpensive (even in faux form) and instantly adds a lush, garden-party feel. Think eucalyptus, ferns, and olive branches.
- **Consider the venue's architecture:** Castle Hill Inn has classic New England charm. Your decor should complement the venue, not fight it. For a modern space, you might choose more minimalist florals; for a historic venue, go for classic, romantic blooms.
- **Repurpose ceremony florals:** After the ceremony, move the arch florals to the reception entrance or the sweetheart table. This is a great way to get more use out of your arrangements.

## Final Thoughts: Your Own Newport-Inspired Wedding

This Castle Hill Inn wedding is a beautiful example of how to capture the spirit of a place—Newport's coastal elegance—through thoughtful design. Whether you're planning a wedding in Rhode Island or anywhere else, you can adapt this style by focusing on a neutral palette, abundant florals, and a deep respect for your venue's natural beauty. And remember, with modern faux floral arrangements, you can achieve that lush, romantic look while being kind to your budget and the environment. Happy planning!
