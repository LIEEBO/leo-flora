# Coastal Garden Wedding Ideas from a Newport Castle Hill Celebration

Imagine saying your vows on a lush lawn overlooking the sparkling Narragansett Bay, the salty breeze mingling with the scent of fresh flowers. That was the dreamy reality for one couple at the historic Castle Hill Inn in Newport, Rhode Island. Their wedding—a romantic, airy coastal garden party—captured the essence of a Newport summer. But you don't need a 40-acre estate to recreate this aesthetic. With a little creativity and the right faux floral arrangements, you can bring that same effortless elegance to your own venue.

## The Vision: Classic, Formal, and Floral-Forward

The design of this wedding was a beautiful balance: classic and formal, yet with a dreamy, floral-forward aesthetic. The color palette—white, ivory, green, and pale blue—echoed the natural surroundings, creating a serene and timeless backdrop. The ceremony itself overlooked the bay, with simple yet elegant arrangements that framed the view without overwhelming it.

For your own coastal garden wedding, think about how you can translate this vision. Instead of trying to replicate the exact grandeur of a historic estate, focus on the feeling: airy, romantic, and connected to nature. Incorporate elements like flowing linen, delicate glassware, and, of course, an abundance of flowers. And here's a secret: you can achieve that lush, garden-like abundance with realistic artificial flowers that look just as beautiful in photos as they do in person.

## Why Choose Faux Floral Arrangements for Your Coastal Wedding?

Planning a wedding by the sea comes with its own set of challenges—wind, salt air, and unpredictable weather can wreak havoc on fresh blooms. That's where faux floral arrangements truly shine. They offer the same visual impact as fresh flowers, but with none of the fragility. You can design your centerpieces, bouquets, and ceremony arches weeks in advance, knowing they'll look perfect on the day.

Modern realistic artificial flowers are incredibly lifelike, with soft petals and natural color variations. Whether you're dreaming of ivory roses, delicate white hydrangeas, or trailing greenery, you can find silk flowers wedding decor that rivals the real thing. Plus, you can repurpose them after the celebration, making them a sustainable and budget-friendly choice.

## Designing Your Coastal Garden Tablescape

One of the most important elements of this Newport wedding was the tablescape. The reception tables were adorned with lush, low centerpieces that encouraged conversation and highlighted the natural beauty of the setting. To recreate this look, consider using a mix of tall and low arrangements to add depth and visual interest.

A great tip is to pair your centerpieces with other natural elements like seashells, driftwood, or candles in glass hurricanes. This not only enhances the coastal theme but also creates a warm, inviting glow as the sun sets. If you're using silk flowers wedding decor, you can easily mix them with fresh greenery for a fuller, more organic look—just be sure to keep the fresh stems hydrated until the last minute.

## Practical Tips for a Floral-Forward Wedding on a Budget

You don't need a celebrity wedding budget to achieve a floral-forward design. Here are a few practical tips to help you get the most out of your flower budget:

- **Invest in high-quality faux flowers**: Choose realistic artificial flowers that feel soft and look natural. They're worth the investment because they'll last forever and can be reused for other events.
- **Mix high and low**: Use a few statement pieces (like an arch or a large centerpiece) and fill in with smaller, budget-friendly arrangements.
- **Repurpose ceremony decor**: After the ceremony, move your altar flowers to the reception. This saves money and ensures nothing goes to waste.
- **Consider a flower wall or backdrop**: A floral wall made of silk flowers is a stunning photo opportunity and can double as a ceremony backdrop or a dessert table feature.

## Bringing It All Together

The beauty of a coastal garden wedding is its timelessness. It's not about following trends but about creating a space that feels personal and serene. By incorporating faux floral arrangements into your design, you can achieve that lush, romantic look without the stress of wilting petals or last-minute changes. Whether you're planning a grand estate affair or an intimate backyard gathering, the spirit of a Newport summer—airy, elegant, and full of light—is within your reach.

So, take a page from this Castle Hill celebration: embrace the natural beauty around you, choose flowers that speak to your heart, and let your love story shine. With a little planning and the right artificial blooms, your wedding will be just as breathtaking as the one that graced the shores of Narragansett Bay.
