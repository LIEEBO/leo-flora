# Spring Wedding Decor: 6 Dried Flower Ideas for Venues

Spring is a season of renewal, and there's no better way to capture that fresh, romantic feeling than with dried flowers. For DIY brides and wedding planners, dried botanicals offer a sustainable, long-lasting alternative to fresh blooms—without sacrificing style. Whether you're dreaming of a sun-drenched meadow aesthetic or a vintage cottage-core vibe, these six dreamy dried flower ideas will help you refresh your wedding venue decoration ideas and create a memorable spring celebration.

## Why Choose Dried Flowers for Spring Weddings?

Dried flowers are not only beautiful but also practical. They can be prepared well in advance, won't wilt during a long ceremony, and double as keepsakes for guests. Plus, they align with eco-conscious values. However, to keep them looking their best, follow the golden rule: keep them high and dry. Avoid humid spaces like bathrooms, and place arrangements in bright, indirect light to preserve their soft pastel hues.

If you're looking for the look of dried flowers but with added durability and color consistency, consider realistic artificial flowers. High-quality faux blooms mimic the texture and charm of dried stems while offering flexibility for intricate designs. ## 1. Bunny Tails (Lagurus) for Whimsical Accents

Fluffy and cloud-like, bunny tails add a touch of whimsy to any arrangement. Use bleached white or soft pastel pinks in petite bud vases on guest tables or as part of a bridal bouquet. Their soft texture contrasts beautifully with sleeker stems, making them a favorite for spring wedding venue decoration ideas.

**Style Tip:** Cluster a few stems in a small vase for a minimalist centerpiece, or mix with other dried grasses for a meadow-inspired look.

## 2. Lavender for a Calming Aroma

Lavender brings a serene, French countryside vibe and a soothing scent that lingers for months. A simple bunch tied with a velvet ribbon can be laid on a welcome table or incorporated into boutonnieres. For a cohesive look, pair lavender with neutral linens and rustic wood elements.

**Style Tip:** Hang small lavender bundles on chair backs for a fragrant, charming detail.

## 3. Preserved Eucalyptus for Lush Greenery

No spring arrangement is complete without greenery. Preserved eucalyptus adds volume and a spa-like scent. The 'Parvifolia' variety is perfect for a draping, romantic effect, spilling over the edge of a vase. Use it to frame arches or as a base for larger faux floral arrangements.

**Style Tip:** Combine eucalyptus with realistic artificial flowers to create a lush, full look that lasts all day.

## 4. Dried Chamomile Daisies for Vintage Charm

Tiny and delicate, chamomile daisies bring a cottage-core aesthetic that's trending in weddings. They work best as fillers in bouquets or as scattered petals along the aisle. Because they're fragile, give them space and handle with care.

**Style Tip:** Mix chamomile with bunny tails and lavender for a quintessential spring bouquet.

## 5. Oat Stems for Golden Texture

Golden and swaying, oat stems add movement and warmth. They're a great alternative to heavy winter branches and can be used in tall centerpieces or as a backdrop accent. Their neutral tone complements any color palette.

**Style Tip:** Pair oat stems with soft pinks and creams for a romantic, sun-kissed look.

## 6. Pampas Grass for Boho Elegance

Though not listed in the original, pampas grass is a staple for spring weddings. Its fluffy plumes add drama and texture. Use it in large floor arrangements or as a focal point in an arch. For a more polished look, consider realistic artificial flowers that mimic pampas without the shedding.

**Style Tip:** Keep pampas away from high-traffic areas to avoid shedding, or opt for faux versions for a mess-free alternative.

## Pro Tips for Styling Your Spring Sanctuary

- **Mix Textures:** Combine fluffy, spiky, and smooth stems for visual interest.
- **Play with Height:** Use varied vase heights to create depth on tables.
