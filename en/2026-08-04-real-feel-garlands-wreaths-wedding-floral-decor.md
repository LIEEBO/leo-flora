When planning a wedding, every detail matters—especially the flowers. But fresh greenery can be costly, fragile, and high-maintenance. That's where real feel garlands and wreaths come in. These lifelike decorations, also known as real touch, PE, or realistic faux florals, have taken the event decor world by storm. They offer the beauty of fresh greenery without the stress, making them a favorite among DIY brides and professional wedding planners alike.

Whether you're draping them over a mantel, framing an arch, or creating a stunning wedding flower wall backdrop, real feel garlands and wreaths deliver elegance and practicality. In this guide, we'll explore why these faux florals are a game-changer for weddings and how you can use them to create unforgettable decor.

## Why Choose Real Feel Garlands and Wreaths for Weddings?

Real feel garlands and wreaths are designed to mimic the look and texture of fresh pine, cedar, and mixed greenery. Unlike live foliage, they won't wilt, dry out, or shed needles—even after hours of photos and dancing. This durability makes them ideal for weddings, where decor needs to look perfect from the ceremony to the last dance.

For brides who love the romance of fresh flowers but worry about allergies, real feel options are a blessing. They provide the lush, green aesthetic without triggering pollen sensitivities. Plus, they're reusable, so you can repurpose them for bridal showers, rehearsal dinners, or even your first anniversary celebration.

Another major advantage is cost-effectiveness. High-quality artificial flowers for wedding decor can be more budget-friendly than fresh blooms, especially when you factor in the savings from not needing a florist to assemble and deliver last-minute arrangements. With real feel garlands, you can achieve a high-end look at a fraction of the price.

## Creative Ways to Use Real Feel Garlands and Wreaths

### 1. Ceremony Arches and Aisles

A lush garland draped over a wooden arch creates a stunning focal point for your vows. Mix pine and cedar garlands for a textured, forest-inspired look. You can also line the aisle with shorter garlands or place wreaths on shepherd's hooks for a whimsical touch.

### 2. Reception Tables and Mantels

Garlands are perfect for table runners or mantel drapes. Use unlit versions for a clean, natural look, or opt for lit garlands to add a warm, romantic glow to evening receptions. Pair them with candles and bridal bouquet artificial flowers for a cohesive centerpiece design.

### 3. Wedding Flower Wall Backdrop

One of the most popular trends in wedding decor is the flower wall backdrop. While fresh flower walls are stunning, they're also expensive and require constant hydration. Real feel garlands and wreaths can be arranged on a wooden or acrylic frame to create a lush, photo-ready backdrop that stays beautiful all day. Add a few artificial flowers for wedding accents to make it uniquely yours.

### 4. Hanging Installations

Suspend garlands from the ceiling or across a barn beam to create a canopy effect. This works beautifully for outdoor weddings or rustic venues. Wreaths can also be hung on chairs, doors, or windows to tie the theme together.

## Practical Tips for Choosing and Styling

When selecting real feel garlands and wreaths, consider the length and size you need. Garlands typically range from 5 to 15 feet, so measure your space before ordering. Wreaths come in 24 to 36 inches—choose a size that suits your venue's scale.

Think about the style: pine offers a classic, sharp look; cedar is softer and more flexible; mixed options give you the best of both. For lighting, decide whether you want lit or unlit. Lit garlands are perfect for evening events, but they require access to power outlets or battery packs.

Don't be afraid to mix real feel greenery with fresh flowers. The faux greenery provides a sturdy base, while fresh blooms add fragrance and a personal touch. This hybrid approach is a smart way to stretch your floral budget.

Finally, consider storage. After the wedding, gently clean your garlands and wreaths with a soft brush or cloth, then store them in a cool, dry place. With proper care, they'll be ready for your next event.

## Why Real Feel Decor is a Smart Investment

Investing in real feel garlands and wreaths is a smart choice for any wedding. They save you time, money, and stress. You can set them up days in advance without worrying about wilting. They're also eco-friendly, as they reduce waste compared to disposable fresh flowers.

For DIY brides, these decorations are a dream. You can customize them with ribbons, fairy lights, or your favorite artificial flowers for wedding themes. For planners, they offer reliability and repeatability—use them for multiple events and clients.

In short, real feel garlands and wreaths are not just for Christmas. They're versatile, beautiful, and practical additions to any wedding floral design. Whether you're creating a grand entrance or an intimate tablescape, these lifelike greens will elevate your decor and delight your guests.
