# Floral Foam Bricks Compared: Strass, Oasis & Get It Fresh — Which One Suits Your Stems?

When you're planning a wedding, every detail matters — from the dress to the centerpieces. If you're working with fresh flowers, the floral foam you choose can make or break your arrangements. And if you're mixing in **realistic artificial flowers** for a budget-friendly, long-lasting look, understanding foam basics still helps you build a sturdy base.

Not all floral foam is created equal. The spec sheet — soak time, absorption, density — matters more than the brand name on the box. If you've ever ended up with wilting stems or a crumbling brick, it usually comes down to those factors, not luck. Here's what those terms mean, why they matter, and how five popular bricks compare.

## Understanding Floral Foam Terminology

Before we compare, let's decode the jargon:

- **Soak time:** How long a dry brick needs to sit in water before it's fully saturated. Pull it out early and you'll have dry pockets inside that starve stems from the inside out.
- **Water absorption and weight after soak:** How much moisture the foam holds once saturated — this is the water reserve your flowers draw from over the life of the arrangement.
- **Density (soft, firm, or hard):** Determines how easily a stem pushes in without snapping, crumbling the block, or leaving oversized holes that won't hold thinner stems upright.
- **Suitability:** Which stem types the foam is designed to support.
- **pH level:** Floral foam sits in direct contact with cut stems; a neutral pH avoids accelerating stem damage or bacterial growth.

## The Five Bricks, Side by Side

### STRASS® Premium — The Heavyweight

At 49g, this is the heaviest brick, soaking to 1.85kg and holding 1.8L of water. Its firm/hard density is built for heavy installation work: thick, woody, or strong stems in large-scale arrangements that need serious structural support. This is a specialist foam, not an everyday all-rounder.

### STRASS® IDEAL — The Dependable All-Rounder

A firm, all-stems workhorse at 47g, soaking in 60 seconds to hold 1.7L. A long-established, dependable choice for everyday mixed arrangements.

### OASIS® IDEAL — The Trusted Twin

Also firm and rated for all stems, at 47g and 1.75L absorption after a 65-second soak. Like STRASS, OASIS is a long-standing, trusted name, and IDEAL performs almost identically as a reliable all-rounder.

### GET IT FRESH® PREMIUM — The Value Champion

A newer, more economical entrant, but the specs tell the real story: 47g, 60-second soak, 1.7L absorption, firm density, all-stems suitability — matching STRASS IDEAL and OASIS IDEAL feature for feature. It delivers equivalent quality at a lower price point, making it a strong-value all-rounder rather than a budget compromise.

### OASIS® Spring — The Gentle Option

The lightest brick (33g) and the only soft-density option, absorbing 1.6L after a 65-second soak. Made with slightly fewer binding ingredients, it's formulated specifically for softer, more delicate stems that firmer foams would tear or crush. Also a newer, economical addition to the range.

## Practical Tips for Choosing and Using Floral Foam

- **Match foam to stem type:** Soft foam (like OASIS Spring) for delicate stems; firm or hard foam for woody or thick stems.
- **Soak fully:** Always let the brick submerge until it sinks on its own — don't push it down. This ensures no dry pockets.
- **Cut, don't crumble:** Use a knife to cut foam to size. Crumbling creates air pockets that reduce water uptake.
- **Secure your arrangement:** For large installations, use a foam cage or tape grid to keep the brick in place.
- **Consider artificial blooms:** If you're using **realistic artificial flowers** from a supplier like leoflowers, foam isn't necessary — but a foam base can still anchor your stems if you're mixing real and faux.

## Wedding Venue Decoration Ideas with Foam and Faux Flowers

Floral foam isn't just for fresh flowers. Here are a few **wedding venue decoration ideas** that combine both:

- **Centerpieces:** Use firm foam for a base of fresh greenery, then tuck in **realistic artificial flowers** for pops of color that won't wilt.
- **Arch and backdrop installations:** STRASS Premium is ideal for heavy, large-scale designs. For faux flower walls, skip foam and use a wire or mesh frame instead.
- **Bouquets and boutonnieres:** Soft foam works for delicate fresh stems, but for **affordable wedding flowers** that last all day, consider high-quality artificial alternatives from yiwuleoflowers.

## Conclusion: Which Foam Suits Your Stems?

STRASS and OASIS remain the established, proven names in floral foam, while GET IT FRESH and Spring are newer, more affordable alternatives.
