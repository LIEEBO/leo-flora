The couple's vision was clear: a garden party by the sea. This theme perfectly captures the essence of a spring wedding in a coastal town like Cape May. ## Incorporating Artificial Flowers for a Flawless Look

One of the biggest concerns for DIY brides and planners is keeping flowers fresh and within budget. This is where **bridal bouquet artificial flowers** shine. Modern artificial flowers are incredibly realistic, and they offer several practical advantages:

- **Durability:** They won't wilt in the heat or get crushed during transportation.
- **Cost-Effective:** You can achieve a lush, high-end look for a fraction of the cost of fresh flowers, especially for large installations.
- **Allergy-Friendly:** Perfect for guests with allergies.
- **Long-Lasting Keepsakes:** Your bouquet and centerpieces can be kept as mementos long after the wedding.

For this garden party theme, consider a bridal bouquet with realistic peonies, garden roses, and eucalyptus leaves in shades of pink and green. The texture and movement of quality faux stems are indistinguishable from the real thing.

## Designing a Stunning Wedding Flower Wall Backdrop

A **wedding flower wall backdrop** is a show-stopping element that adds depth and a perfect photo opportunity for guests. In this garden party setup, a flower wall covered in pink and green blooms would be the ultimate focal point behind the sweetheart table or as a ceremony arch. Here are some tips for creating your own:

1.  **Choose Your Base:** Use a sturdy wooden or PVC frame and attach a grid or chicken wire to hold the stems.
2.  **Select Your Flowers:** Mix large blooms (like hydrangeas or peonies) with smaller fillers (like spray roses or baby's breath) and greenery for a full, organic look.
3.  **Layer and Fluff:** Start from the bottom and work your way up, placing flowers close together. Fluff the stems to create volume and hide any gaps.
4.  **Lighting Matters:** Illuminate the wall with warm uplighting to make the colors pop, especially for evening events.

## Practical Tips for Your Garden Party Wedding

### 1. Mix Real and Artificial

You don't have to choose between real and faux. A hybrid approach is smart: use fresh flowers for small, intimate pieces like the cake topper or guest favors, and use **artificial flowers for wedding** decor that requires volume and structure, such as the flower wall, aisle markers, and hanging installations. This saves money and stress without sacrificing beauty.

### 2. Consider the Venue's Architecture

Congress Hall has a beautiful, historic charm. When decorating, work with the venue's existing features. Use greenery to soften architectural lines and add floral touches to staircases, mantels, and doorways. This creates a cohesive look that feels integrated with the space.

### 3. Don't Forget the Details

Small details make a big impact. Incorporate pink and green into your table linens, napkin rings, place cards, and even your wedding cake design. A naked cake with fresh (or faux) berries and greenery is a perfect fit for this theme.

### 4. Plan for the Weather

A garden party is at the mercy of the weather. Have a backup plan for rain or high winds. If you're using artificial flowers, they'll hold up fine, but you'll need to secure everything properly. Consider using weighted bases for tall centerpieces and ensuring your flower wall is anchored securely.
